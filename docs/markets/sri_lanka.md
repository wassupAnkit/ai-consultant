# Sri Lanka: source-market counselling guide

> Researched October 2026 for Edge, Expert Education & Visa Services' internal counsellor assistant. Visa rules, foreign-exchange orders, fees and exam calendars change often. Every figure is marked "as of" with its source; check the linked official page before quoting it to a student or family. Where only a secondary source (news, law firm, lender, agent blog) was available, it is labelled as secondary. Statements marked "not verified" could not be confirmed and must not be presented as fact.

---

## 1. Student profile & goals

**Why students leave.**
- **Too few state university places.** Sri Lanka's state universities are tuition-free, but most students who qualify still don't get a place. UGC's own statistics show 173,541 students qualified from the 2023 A/L and 44,852 were admitted, which is 25.85% ([UGC University Statistics 2024, ch. 2](https://www.ugc.ac.lk/downloads/statistics/stat_2024/Chapter%202.pdf)). Qualified-to-admitted ratios were similar in 2020–2022 (same table).
- **Exam scale.** For the 2025 A/L, 176,527 of 281,810 candidates (62.64%) qualified for university entrance. Results came out on 31 March 2026 ([News 1st, 1 Apr 2026](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2026/04/01/over-62-64-qualify-for-university-admission-as-2025-a-l-results-are-released)). Comparing the two years' figures (a rough derivation, not a UGC statistic), well over 100,000 qualified students a year don't get a state place. Many of them are prospects for private, transnational (TNE) or overseas study.
- **Economic-crisis push and migration intent.** ICEF names poor job prospects, low wages and corruption as push factors. It notes that study abroad "often aims at permanent residency", and gives youth unemployment as 25% (2023 estimate) ([ICEF Monitor, Jan 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/01/recruiting-in-sri-lanka-demand-for-study-abroad-remains-high-tne-poised-for-further-growth/)). Labour emigration hit a record of 312,836 departures for foreign employment in 2024, per SLBFE data ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/q7k0)). Expect many families to treat study abroad as a migration pathway. Probe the genuine-student story carefully (see §6).

**Where they go (secondary sources; definitions differ by source).**

| Destination | Latest figure found | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Australia | ~19,000 Sri Lankan students in 2024, +41% vs 2019 | [ICEF, Jan 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/01/recruiting-in-sri-lanka-demand-for-study-abroad-remains-high-tne-poised-for-further-growth/) |
| Canada | 8,075 in 2023, +443% vs 2019 | same |
| UK | 5,500 in 2022/23, +84% y/y (Sri Lanka #11 market) | same |
| USA | 3,661 in 2024/25 (3,424 in 2023/24) | [Open Doors Sri Lanka fact sheet 2025](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_Sri-Lanka_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf) |
| Japan, Malaysia | "several thousand" each | [ICEF, Jan 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/01/recruiting-in-sri-lanka-demand-for-study-abroad-remains-high-tne-poised-for-further-growth/) |

**Transnational education (TNE) at home is a major competitor.** In 2022/23, more than 50,000 Sri Lankans studied for UK degrees through TNE, making Sri Lanka the UK's second-largest TNE market. Edith Cowan University opened a Colombo campus in 2023, and Curtin opened at SLIIT at the end of 2024 ([ICEF, Jan 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/01/recruiting-in-sri-lanka-demand-for-study-abroad-remains-high-tne-poised-for-further-growth/)). Many applicants will be **top-up or transfer students**: they started a UK-franchised or foreign-affiliated programme in Colombo and want to finish abroad.

**Fields.** Nursing/health, IT and project management were reported as popular choices for Canada ([ApplyBoard, Aug 2021](https://www.applyboard.com/blog/applyinsights-study-permit-trends-in-canada-sri-lanka-nepal)). The government says it is open to foreign partners in medicine, engineering, business, finance, IT and AI ([ICEF, Jan 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/01/recruiting-in-sri-lanka-demand-for-study-abroad-remains-high-tne-poised-for-further-growth/)). In the US, 90.8% of Sri Lankan students attend doctoral universities ([Open Doors 2025](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_Sri-Lanka_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf)), which suggests a heavy graduate/STEM tilt.

**Age and gap patterns.**
- Students often take a gap year or are older than expected at the point of entry. State-university admission has long involved a wait after the A/L. In 2022 the Education Ministry and UGC announced plans to cut that waiting time so admissions finish within a few months of the exam ([Sunday Times, Feb 2022](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/220220/education/ed-ministry-and-ugc-to-reduce-waiting-time-for-university-admission-473493.html)).
- Many A/L students also sit the exam a second time as private candidates. 60,397 of the 2025 A/L candidates sat privately ([News 1st](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2026/04/01/over-62-64-qualify-for-university-admission-as-2025-a-l-results-are-released)).
- **Don't treat a 1–2 year gap after A/L as a red flag by itself.** In Sri Lanka it is structural. Still, document what the student did during the gap: retake, private campus, work.

**Segments (practice notes; no data verified).**
- Colombo and Western Province families with international-school (Edexcel/Cambridge) backgrounds tend to aim at UK, Australia or US universities.
- Students from the North and East (Jaffna, Trincomalee) may have family links abroad, for example in Canada, the UK or Australia. *The size and location of the Tamil diaspora was not verified this session.*

## 2. Family & decision-making

- **Parents usually pay.** Loans need a parent or relative as co-borrower: People's Bank's Wisdom education loan must be "considered jointly with parent/spouse/close relative" ([People's Bank Education Loan, Aug 2024](https://www.peoplesbank.lk/roastoth/2024/08/Education-Loan.pdf)). Plan for joint meetings with the parent who holds the funds and signs the loan.
- **Mind the 2022 trauma.** During the crisis, students abroad couldn't get money out of Sri Lanka. Students reported a US$1,000/month cap and banks without dollars ([The Morning, 20 Apr 2022](https://www.themorning.lk/sri-lankan-students-unable-to-pay-tuition-fees-as-economic-crisis-grows); [ICEF, Apr 2022](https://monitor.icef.com/2022/04/economic-crisis-at-home-pressures-sri-lankan-students-abroad)). Parents may ask about transfer reliability. Answer with the current rules in §4, not reassurance.
- **Language for drafts.** Civil documents may be in Sinhala, Tamil or English. The Registrar General's Department (RGD) translates Sinhala↔English and Tamil↔English ([RGD](https://rgd.gov.lk/web/index.php/en/services/civil-registration/translation/translate-certificate-of-birth)).
  - For parent messages, ask which language the family prefers: Sinhala (Sinhala script), Tamil (Tamil script) or English. Don't assume.
  - Many Colombo families work comfortably in English. *Not verified with data.*
- **Trust.** Families have been burned by fake agents (§7). Give them written fee breakdowns, provider-direct payment, and links to official pages they can check themselves.

## 3. Academic background

**Secondary: G.C.E. O/L and A/L (Department of Examinations, doenets.lk).**

| Grade | Marks | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | 75–100 | Distinction |
| B | 65–74 | Very Good Pass |
| C | 50–64 | Credit Pass |
| S | 35–49 | Ordinary Pass |
| W/F | 0–34 | Weak / Fail |

Source: [Nuffic, Sri Lanka assessment systems](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/node/103). Universities often treat S as the minimum pass ([Oxford Brookes](https://www.brookes.ac.uk/study/international-students/your-country/sri-lanka/entry-requirements)).

- **Z-score is for local use only.** State university selection uses district-wise Z-scores, not raw grades ([Daily Mirror on 2024 cut-offs](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Minimum-Z-scores-for-2024-AL-university-admissions-released/108-317558)). The Z-score appears on the result sheet, but overseas offers ignore it and use the subject grades.
- **How UK, Australian and Canadian universities read the A/L (as of Oct 2026).** Offers are made on the letter grades of the A/L subjects, compared grade for grade with UK A-levels. The A/L is not converted into a UK points score.
  - UK universities publish a direct match. Surrey treats Sri Lankan ABB as UK ABB and BBB as BBB, and also asks for O/L English and Maths at C ([Surrey](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/sri-lanka/entry-requirements)). LSE matches AAA and AAB the same way and reads Combined Mathematics A as Maths A ([LSE](https://www.lse.ac.uk/study-at-lse/international-students/sri-lanka)). Aberdeen asks for BCC for Business, Arts and Social Sciences and AAA for Medicine ([Aberdeen](https://abdn.ac.uk/study/international/inmycountry/sri-lanka/entry)).
  - The A/L has no A* grade. Loughborough accepts an A wherever it would ask for an A* ([Loughborough](https://lboro.ac.uk/international/country/sri-lanka/applicationinformation/entryrequirements/undergraduate/)).
  - A few universities also list a UCAS tariff figure next to the grades. Oxford Brookes gives ABB as 128 points, BBB as 120 and CSS as 64 ([Oxford Brookes](https://www.brookes.ac.uk/study/international-students/your-country/sri-lanka/entry-requirements)). Quote the university's grade requirement to the family, not a points total.
  - **Australia.** The University of Melbourne publishes A/L guides for each course, for example ABB for Commerce (with English and Maths) and AAB for Biomedicine ([Melbourne](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/undergraduate-study/international-applications/entry-requirements/indicative-entry-scores-international-students/qualifications/sri-lanka-a-levels)). Our fetch of that page was blocked, so these figures come from the search-indexed copy. Check the live page before quoting them.
  - **Canada.** No Canadian university's Sri Lankan A/L requirement was checked this session (*not verified*). Ask the institution for its published equivalence.
  - Subject prerequisites, such as Combined Maths, Physics or Chemistry for engineering, matter as much as the grades. Always check the course page.
- **London/Cambridge/Edexcel A-levels.** International-school students sit these instead of the local A/L. Treat them as UK qualifications. *No Sri Lanka-specific source was checked.*

**University.**
- **Degree length matters.**
  - A **3-year general** bachelor's is SLQF level 5, minimum 90 credits, with no specialisation or thesis.
  - A **4-year special/honours** degree is SLQF level 6, 120 credits.
  - "General" is sometimes not printed on the certificate, so check the credit count on the transcript ([Nuffic, higher education](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/node/106)).
- **Destinations treat the two differently (as of Oct 2026).**
  - UK master's entry asks much more of a 3-year general degree. LJMU asks for CGPA 3.3/4 (or 80%) from a 3-year general degree, but only 2.8/4 (55%) from a 4-year special degree ([LJMU](https://ljmu.ac.uk/study/courses/international-entry-requirements/sri-lanka)). Surrey reads a UK 2:1 as 80% from a 3-year degree or 60% from a 4-year special degree, and a 2:2 as 70% or 55% ([Surrey](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/sri-lanka/entry-requirements)).
  - Some selective UK universities list only the 4-year degree. LSE normally requires a 4-year special/honours or professional bachelor's with a 2:1, or GPA 3.3/4 ([LSE](https://www.lse.ac.uk/study-at-lse/international-students/sri-lanka)). Aberdeen "considers" 4-year special degrees and says nothing about 3-year degrees ([Aberdeen](https://abdn.ac.uk/study/international/inmycountry/sri-lanka/entry)).
  - Nuffic (Netherlands) compares a general degree to 3 years of HBO or 1 year of WO, and a special degree to an HBO bachelor's or 2 years of WO ([Nuffic](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/node/106)).
  - UK ENIC's comparability statement was not checked, because it is a paid service (*not verified*).
  - Expect US and Canadian graduate schools, and German master's admissions, to read a 3-year general degree as short of a 4-year bachelor's. No Sri Lanka-specific US or Canadian university page was checked (*not verified*). Australia's own bachelor's usually takes 3 years, but how Australian universities treat a Sri Lankan general degree was *not verified*. Confirm with each institution.
- **University of London (UoL) degrees taken in Sri Lanka.** UoL programmes are taught through local Recognised Teaching Centres, such as the Royal Institute of Colombo, or studied by distance learning ([UoL Sri Lanka page](https://london.ac.uk/study/where-study/teaching-centre/sri-lanka); [UoL, RIC](https://london.ac.uk/ways-study/royal-institute-colombo); both pages were blocked to our fetch and read through search listings).
  - The awarding body is the University of London, so assess the degree as a UK bachelor's. Check its classification and the awarding body named on the transcript.
  - It is not a Sri Lankan "external degree" (see the INZ note below).
  - The UGC does not check foreign-university programmes delivered by local private institutes, and the ministry has asked it to design monitoring ([Daily Mirror, 15 Feb 2025](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/front-page/Unregulated-private-degree-institutions-flourish-amid-oversight-gaps/238-302425)). That affects recognition inside Sri Lanka, not overseas admission.
  - Whether a given UK, Australian or Canadian university treats a distance-learning UoL degree exactly like an on-campus degree was *not verified*.
- **Sri Lankan "external degrees" are a different thing.** Some Sri Lankan state universities run their own external, exam-based degrees. One example is the Buddhist and Pali University's 3-year BA External, with fees charged per examination ([BPU](https://bpu.ac.lk/?p=16522)). Treat these like 3-year general degrees, and note that INZ excluded them (below).
- **GPA scale.** It varies by university. One example is University of Colombo: A+ (80–100) = 4.00, B (60–64) = 3.00, C− (40–44) = 2.00, and D or E is a fail ([Nuffic](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/node/103)). Always attach the issuing university's own scale.
- **NZ immigration reference (archived, June 2025).** It listed bachelor's degrees from 20 Sri Lankan universities at NZQCF Level 7. It excluded "external" degrees, coursework-only master's, MBAs and MBBS, and noted that Sri Lankan universities often issue documents in English ([INZ ops manual, archived](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/opsmanual/90353.htm)). The "external degree" flag is a useful screening cue.
- **Private degree-awarding institutes.** These are recognised by Ministerial "Degree Awarding Institute Orders" under Section 25A of the Universities Act, published in the Gazette ([example: ESOFT, Sunday Times 2019](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/190915/education/esoft-metro-campus-receives-degree-awarding-status-368226.html); [Gazette 1890/12, 2014](https://www.documents.gov.lk/view/extra-gazettes/2014/11/1890-12_E.pdf)). *A current official list was not retrieved. Check the UGC site before relying on any institute's status.* For HND/top-up students, check the awarding body (often a UK university or Pearson). The Colombo campus is not the awarding body.
- **Medium of instruction (MOI).** Sri Lankan university documents are often in English ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/opsmanual/90353.htm)). Whether a given destination accepts an MOI letter instead of IELTS/PTE is institution-specific. *Not verified.*
- **Verification.** O/L and A/L certificates from 2001 onward are verified and authenticated online. Apply on the DoE certificate portal, choosing "Foreign Ministry Certificate (Direct to Foreign Ministry)". The DoE issues a certificate number, then the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) Consular Affairs Division authenticates it for Rs. 800 per document plus DoE charges. Only a soft copy is issued online; pre-2001 certificates go through the DoE in Battaramulla ([MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.lk/certificates-issued-by-the-department-of-examinations); [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/94400/online-authentication-of-ol-and-al-certificates-launched)). For degrees, the university's examinations branch issues transcripts. *The exact MFA pre-verification route for degrees was not confirmed.*

## 4. Money

**Authority.** Foreign exchange is governed by the Foreign Exchange Act No. 12 of 2017. The Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) Department of Foreign Exchange administers it, and the Minister of Finance issues Section 22 Orders to limit outflows ([CBSL press release, 24 Jul 2023](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/press/pr/press_20230724_new_order_issued_relaxing_certain_limitations_suspensions_imposed_on_outward_remittances_of_foreign_exchange_e.pdf)).

**Remitting tuition and living costs.**
- **What is allowed.** Family members in Sri Lanka may send tuition, course fees and living expenses to students abroad through an Authorised Dealer bank, with "relevant documentary evidence as usually required by ADs". Students may also use AD-issued debit or credit cards abroad. CBSL said the 2020 crisis measures did not restrict current transactions such as overseas education ([CBSL notice, 20 Apr 2020](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/en/news/remittance-of-funds-to-sri-lankans-abroad-to-pursue-studies-and-on-short-term-visits-to-meet-their-expenses)).
- **Current CBSL position (Department of Foreign Exchange FAQs, as of Oct 2026).** Parents may remit tuition, living and other study costs through an AD bank, against documentary evidence. An AD may load a travel card for a student abroad once it has verified the study with documents from the foreign institution or a government authority. Parents may also repay a child's overseas student loan through a licensed bank. The FAQs set no rupee or dollar cap on education remittances ([DFE FAQs](https://www.dfe.lk/web/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=402&Itemid=742&lang=en)).
- **Documents banks ask for.** The FAQs don't list them. In practice, expect the offer or CAS/CoE, the institution's fee invoice, the student's passport and visa, and proof of the parent–student relationship (practice note; *not verified* against any bank's checklist).
- **The 2022 reality.** Despite the rule, banks short of dollars rationed transfers. Students reported waiting lists and kerb-market purchases ([The Morning, Apr 2022](https://www.themorning.lk/sri-lankan-students-unable-to-pay-tuition-fees-as-economic-crisis-grows); [ICEF, Apr 2022](https://monitor.icef.com/2022/04/economic-crisis-at-home-pressures-sri-lankan-students-abroad)).
- **Relaxations since 2023.** A June 2023 Order relaxed emigrant and capital limits ([CBSL, Jul 2023](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/press/pr/press_20230724_new_order_issued_relaxing_certain_limitations_suspensions_imposed_on_outward_remittances_of_foreign_exchange_e.pdf)).
- **Limits in place in 2025 (capital and emigrant transfers only).** Gazette 2441/14 of 18 June 2025 (six months from 20 June 2025) kept capital-side limits, including an emigrant's initial migration allowance capped at USD 100,000 ([D. L. & F. De Saram, 2025](https://www.desaram.com/foreign-exchange-regulations-sri-lanka-outward-remittances-2025/)). A December 2025 Gazette reportedly continued that cap ([Gazette 2467/67](https://www.documents.gov.lk/view/extra-gazettes/2025/12/2467-67_E.pdf); secondary summary). These rules target capital and emigrant transfers, not student fees.
  - On 18 June 2026 the government issued a further Section 22 Order (Gazette 2493/38) and Regulations No. 2 of 2026 on fund transfers abroad by emigrants (Gazette 2493/37) ([DFE regulations list](https://www.dfe.lk/web/index.php?option=com_rdevelopment&task=allgazettes&Itemid=483&lang=en)). Their texts could not be read: the gazette site was unreachable on 9 Oct 2026. *Their limits are not verified.* Families who plan to emigrate later should know the emigrant allowance is separate from student remittances.
- **PFCA (Personal Foreign Currency Account).** Under CBSL FEA Direction No. 04 of 2021 (in force 22 Mar 2021), a PFCA may be debited for outward remittances on current transactions "of the account holder and/or immediate family members" (spouse, parents, children) ([Direction 04/2021](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/laws/cdg/Foreign_Exchange_Act_Direction_No_4_of_2021_e.pdf)). A parent working in the Gulf with a PFCA can therefore pay a child's fees directly. A new Direction No. 01 of 2026, "Personal Foreign Currency Accounts", was issued on 3 March 2026 ([DFE directions list](https://www.dfe.lk/web/index.php?option=com_rdevelopment&task=alldirection&Itemid=482&lang=en)). It probably replaces the 2021 Direction, but *whether it keeps the family-remittance permission is not verified*. Ask the parent's bank.

**Currency volatility.** The rupee hit about LKR 372/USD in May 2022 ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/sri-lanka/currency)) and recovered to about 303 in October 2025 ([FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DEXSLUS)). It weakened again in 2026: down 3.6% YTD by early May, which CBSL linked to the Middle East conflict ([Ada Derana Biz](https://bizenglish.adaderana.lk/sri-lankan-rupee-depreciates-3-6-against-us-dollar-in-2026/)), and about 334.7 on 31 July 2026 (secondary: [ValutaFX](https://www.valutafx.com/history/usd-lkr-2026-07-31)).
- Build a 10–15% FX buffer into budgets. This is a counsellor practice suggestion, not a rule.
- IMF context: the EFF approved on 20 March 2023 (about US$3bn) has disbursed about US$2.4bn after the combined 5th/6th review on 27 May 2026 ([Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/breaking-news/IMF-approves-US-695-Million-disbursement-to-Sri-Lanka-after-completing-fifth-and-sixth-reviews/108-341423)). A staff-level agreement on the 7th review (about US$345m) was reached on 4 Oct 2026, pending the 2027 Budget ([Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/top-story/IMF-reaches-staff-level-agreement-345-m-tranche-hinges-on-2027-Budget/26-798238)).

**Proof-of-funds red flags.**
- **Fabricated funds documents.** In 2018 Immigration New Zealand found a Sri Lankan finance company creating fraudulent funds documents for student applicants. 83 of 88 pending applications were declined, and agents were investigated ([News 1st, Aug 2018](https://www.newsfirst.lk/2018/08/01/new-zealand-uncovers-sri-lankan-student-visa-scam/); [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/SL-visa-scam-revealed-but-no-review-of-past-applications/108-153381)). Prefer licensed commercial banks and verify letters directly.
- **Gold pawning.** It is a mainstream credit product: banks publish per-8g advance limits ([SDB, Apr 2026](https://www.sdb.lk/personal/pawning/pawning)), and CBSL reported Rs 241bn in pawning in the first nine months of 2020 ([Daily FT](https://ft.lk/front-page/Gold-pawning-grows-to-Rs-241-b/44-709467)). Pawning-funded balances or recent lump-sum deposits must be explained with a loan agreement. Don't present them as savings.
- **Other warning signs.** Watch for fixed deposits opened weeks before lodgement, sponsors whose declared income can't explain the balance, and remitter-parents whose PFCA history doesn't match their stated Gulf employment. These are general integrity practice; no Sri Lanka-specific official guidance was found.

## 5. Origin-side steps & documents

- **Police clearance.** Apply online via the Sri Lanka Police website's E-Services, "Clearance Certificates" ([police.lk](https://www.police.lk/?p=21493)). It is open to citizens aged 16+.
  - Scans needed: passport bio page, NIC front and back, birth certificate (original language), and an affidavit if the NIC and passport names differ.
  - Fee: Rs 5,000 per the embassy notice; police.lk reportedly states Rs 5,100 for overseas applicants. Usually issued in about 14 working days ([Sri Lanka Embassy Abu Dhabi notice, Jun 2026](https://abudhabi.embassy.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Notice-Police-Clearance-Certificate-Online.pdf)).
  - Confirm the fee on police.lk before paying.
- **Authentication / legalisation (no Apostille).** As of 30 June 2026, Sri Lanka is **not** a party to the Apostille Convention. HCCH's status table lists 130 Contracting Parties, and Sri Lanka is not among them ([HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)).
  - The claim that Sri Lanka joined in 2024 is wrong. *No pending accession was found.*
  - Sri Lankan documents need MFA authentication, then legalisation by the destination's mission where it asks for that.
  - Documents are authenticated by the MFA Consular Affairs Division on its e-DAS platform ([MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.lk/consular-affairs-division-2)). Appointments are by e-channelling only, at Colombo HQ and the regional consular offices in Jaffna, Trincomalee, Kandy, Kurunegala and Matara ([announcement, Sep 2022](https://srilankaembassy.at/sri-lanka-news-de/consular-affairs-division-of-the-ministry-of-foreign-affairs-launches-echanneling-appointment-system-for-authentication-services/?lang=de)).
  - RGD birth, marriage and death certificates can be authenticated fully online, with the copy delivered by email ([MFA online consular services](https://mfa.gov.lk/online-consular-services/); [LBO, Sep 2024](https://lankabusinessonline.com/?p=242453)).
- **MFA attestation after the May 2026 outage (status as of 9 Oct 2026).** On 17–18 May 2026 the e-DAS system failed, and all attestation at the Battaramulla HQ and the five regional offices was suspended "until further notice" ([Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/122627/all-document-attestation-services-at-foreign-ministry-consular-division-suspended); [Daily Mirror, 18 May 2026](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Technical-failure-disrupts-Foreign-Ministry-attestation-services/108-340536)). On 19 May it resumed at Battaramulla "on a limited basis", with full restoration still pending ([Colombo Times, 19 May 2026](https://colombotimes.lk/Consular-services-gradually-restored-2026-05-19.html)). As of 9 Oct 2026 the MFA's Consular Affairs page shows no suspension notice ([MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.lk/consular-affairs-division-2)). *Full restoration, including the regional offices, is not confirmed.* Before booking, check the e-Channelling slots or call the Authentication & Verification Section on +94 112 275 522 (number from the MFA page).
- **Translations.** RGD translates birth certificates at its translator offices: Rs 600 per copy, about 3 working days, original or a Divisional Secretariat certified copy required ([RGD](https://rgd.gov.lk/web/index.php/en/services/civil-registration/translation/translate-certificate-of-birth)). Private sworn translators also exist. *Their appointment process is not verified officially.*
- **Medicals.**
  - **UK TB test:** the IOM Migration Health Assessment Centre in Colombo (Elvitigala Mawatha, Colombo 8) is the Home Office-approved clinic. It charges US$60 for ages 11+, payable in LKR, and waits can stretch to about 10 days in the June–September peak ([GOV.UK, updated Jul 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tuberculosis-test-for-a-uk-visa-clinic-in-sri-lanka/tuberculosis-testing-in-sri-lanka)).
  - **Australia:** examinations are done by Home Affairs panel physicians with a HAP ID ([Australian High Commission Colombo](https://srilanka.embassy.gov.au/clmb/120120HEALTHREQUIREMENTSANDMEDICALEXAMINATIONS.html); [Home Affairs](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/health/arrange-your-health-examinations)).
  - *Panel clinic names for Canada and NZ were not verified.*
- **Visa centres.**
  - VFS handles UK, Australia, Canada, NZ and Schengen applications in Sri Lanka ([VFS statement, Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/opinion/VFS-Global-statement-on-Sri-Lanka-E-Visa/14-761491)).

**Australia.**
- **Evidence level.** A September 2025 report put Sri Lanka at Level 1 ([Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/113090/australia-updates-student-visa-evidence-levels-sri-lanka-now-among-low-risk-countries)). Reports after January 2026 say Level 2. Levels aren't published, so always run the [Document Checklist Tool](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/web-evidentiary-tool).
- **Refusals are rising despite the "moderate" label.**
  - A reported 38% of Sri Lankan student applications were refused in early 2026 ([Ada Derana, 5 May 2026](https://adaderana.lk/news/122152), citing The Noticer).
  - Offshore higher-education approval rates for Sri Lankans were 44% before April 2026. They jumped to 84% in April, but processing volumes fell 87% ([THE, 30 May 2026](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/743189)).
  - Sri Lanka was the sixth-largest source market in 2025–26 ([THE, Jul 2026](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/744222)).
- **Admissions.** Universities publish A/L grade guides for each course, for example Melbourne's ABB for Commerce, not ATAR-style points. See §3 ([Melbourne](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/undergraduate-study/international-applications/entry-requirements/indicative-entry-scores-international-students/qualifications/sri-lanka-a-levels); search-indexed copy).
- **Implication.** The Genuine Student narrative must explain why Australia rather than the TNE option at home. THE reported refusals where TNE alternatives exist ([THE](https://www.stg.timeshighereducation.com/node/741433); headline only, article not read). As of October 2026, offshore processing follows Ministerial Direction 115 (from 14 Nov 2025; National Planning Level 295,000 for 2026; priority by provider allocation, not a hard cap) ([Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/ministerial-direction-115)), and the visa charge rose from AUD 2,000 to AUD 2,500 on 1 July 2026 ([Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/student-visa-application-charge-increase)).

**UK.**
- Refusals for Sri Lankans rose sharply into 2026. THE reported they trebled in Q1 2026 versus Q1 2025 ([THE](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/743075)).
- The UK also links student visas to asylum. 2,862 Sri Lankans claimed asylum in the year cited, and Home Office plans reportedly named Sri Lanka among nationalities facing tougher scrutiny ([Ada Derana, May 2025](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/108094/uk-visa-applications-for-some-nations-including-sri-lanka-could-be-restricted-report)).
- Universities facing the tighter Basic Compliance Assessment (refusal rate under 5%) are cutting "high-risk" recruitment ([ICEF](https://monitor.icef.com/2026/05/uk-sponsored-study-visa-issuances-down-rejection-rates-up-and-more/)). *Whether any university has paused Sri Lanka recruitment was not verified.*
- **Admissions.** Universities compare A/L grades with UK A-level grades one for one; Surrey, for example, treats ABB as ABB. There is no A* in the A/L. For master's entry, a 3-year general degree needs a much higher mark than a 4-year special degree: Surrey asks 80% against 60% for a 2:1. University of London degrees taken in Sri Lanka are UK awards. See §3 ([Surrey](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/sri-lanka/entry-requirements)).
- Funds seasoning and credibility interviews are the main hurdles. The Graduate Route drops to 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027 (PhD stays at 3 years) ([PIE News](https://thepienews.com/shorter-graduate-route-from-january-2027-uk-govt-confirms/)). From 1 June 2026 a sponsor must keep its visa refusal rate below 5% ([Wonkhe](https://wonkhe.com/blogs/the-new-ukvi-student-sponsor-regime-goes-live/)), so a 22% national refusal rate makes Sri Lankan files commercially "high risk" for some universities.

**Canada.**
- Historic approval rates for Sri Lankans were about 45% (2016–2020), below the all-country average ([ApplyBoard](https://www.applyboard.com/blog/applyinsights-study-permit-trends-in-canada-sri-lanka-nepal)).
- Sri Lanka was never on the SDS list ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/student-direct-stream/eligibility.html)), so the end of SDS changed little. As of November 2025, IRCC's 2026 target is 408,000 study permits (155,000 for new arrivals), and from 1 January 2026 master's and PhD students at public DLIs no longer need a provincial attestation letter ([CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2025/11/canada-announces-2026-study-permit-cap-1162628.html)).
- Diaspora family ties can help with settlement but must not blur temporary intent. *No current Sri Lanka approval rate was verified.*
- **Admissions.** Graduate schools generally expect a 4-year bachelor's. A 3-year general degree may need a bridging year or a qualifying year (*no Canadian university's Sri Lanka page was verified*; see §3). For undergraduate entry, ask each institution for its published A/L equivalence.

**USA.** Sri Lankans are concentrated at doctoral institutions (90.8%) and private institutions (83.5%) ([Open Doors 2025](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_Sri-Lanka_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf)). Funded graduate (RA/TA) applicants are the strongest profile. Prepare for 214(b) ties questioning. Since June 2025 F/M/J applicants face online-presence vetting with social-media profiles set to public, and since 6 September 2025 they must interview in their country of nationality or residence ([Miller Mayer](https://millermayer.com/dos-resumes-visa-processing-for-foreign-students-and-exchange-visitors-announces-enhanced-social-media-vetting/)). Budget for the $250 visa integrity fee, which some posts were collecting as of May 2026 ([Boston University ISSO](https://www.bu.edu/isso/2026/05/19/visa-integrity-fee/)). *No Sri Lanka F-1 refusal rate was verified.*

**New Zealand.** Remember the 2018 funds-fraud case (§4). From 16 November 2026 a new Short Term Graduate Work Visa (up to 6 months) opens and PSWV eligibility extends to Level 7 graduate diplomas ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/post-study-work-visa-eligibility-extended-from-16-november-2026/); [INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/new-and-updated-post-study-work-visa-options)). *Current Sri Lanka grant rates were not verified.*

**Germany / Ireland.** The German Embassy Colombo's student information lists a blocked account of €11,904/yr (€992/month) ([German Embassy Colombo student info, 2024](https://colombo.diplo.de/blob/2668040/f8ea50b8e1bce1300138bf2669202934/student-2024-data.pdf)). Processing takes "at least several weeks" because files go to German immigration authorities ([German Embassy Colombo](https://colombo.diplo.de/lk-en/service/2702770-2702770)). A 3-year general degree may not meet German master's entry. *Ireland: no Sri Lanka-specific data verified.*

## 7. Counselling do's & don'ts

**Do**
- Ask early whether the degree is 3-year general or 4-year special, and whether it is "external" (§3).
- Have parents remit through an AD bank or PFCA and keep the bank's remittance advice (§4).
- Get DoE certificates authenticated online early, and check whether e-DAS is running (§5).
- Show families the TNE-vs-overseas cost comparison honestly. Case officers will ask about it.

**Don't**
- Don't accept funds letters from non-bank finance companies without direct verification (2018 NZ case).
- Don't promise PR outcomes. Many Sri Lankan families frame study as migration (§1), and that framing hurts GS, UK credibility and US 214(b) assessments.
- Don't route clients to "guaranteed visa" shortcuts. Fake agents are a documented problem:
  - The Sri Lankan Embassy in Moscow warned about fake education and employment agencies sending Sri Lankans on student visas with false job promises ([Embassy Moscow notice](https://www.srilankaembassy.ru/en/642-notice-on-fake-education-and-employment-agencies)).
  - Fake migration agents targeted Sri Lankan students for Australia ([Daily Mirror, 2023](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Fake-agents-dupe-aspiring-SL-student-migrants/108-258219)).

**Agent regulation.**
- **No dedicated regulator or licensing scheme for education agents in Sri Lanka was found.** After three Sri Lankan students died in Azerbaijan in 2020, the Ministry of Education said it planned a "regulatory framework" for agents, but no details followed ([The PIE News, Feb 2020](https://thepienews.com/sri-lanka-students-azerbaijan-agents/)).
- Institutes that teach courses and send students overseas were said to need TVEC registration ([Sunday Times, c. 2011](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/110904/News/nws_31.html)).
- In May 2026 the Ministry of Education warned families against unauthorised agents claiming links to its new 200-place overseas scholarship for top A/L achievers, which is applied for directly ([Newswire, 6 May 2026](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/05/06/govt-scholarship-programme-parents-and-students-warned-against-unauthorized-agents/)).
- Destination codes such as Australia's ESOS agent rules still apply to us.

**Crisis and wellbeing helplines** (checked 9 Oct 2026):
- **1926, National Mental Health Helpline.** NIMH lists this number on its official site ([NIMH](https://nimh.health.gov.lk/)). It is toll-free and has run since October 2018 ([Ada Derana, Oct 2018](https://adaderana.lk/news/50744/nimh-introduces-mental-health-helpline-1926)). It is also on WhatsApp at 075 555 1926 ([Airtel Lanka](https://www.airtel.lk/?p=8729)). *Hours and languages are not stated on the official page.*
- **1333, CCCline.** A 24/7, confidential, toll-free crisis line ([LifeLine International](https://lifeline-international.com/member/sri-lanka/)). It is run by the CCC Foundation, and callers can choose Sinhala, Tamil or English ([Daily Mirror, Sep 2022](https://dailymirror.lk/life/CCCline-1333-A-confidential-support-system-when-you-need-it-the-most/243-244604)).
- **Sri Lanka Sumithrayo** (emotional support, free and anonymous). Call 0707 308 308, or 0767 520 620, which is also on WhatsApp. Centre hours vary between 9am and 4pm ([Sumithrayo](https://srilankasumithrayo.lk/)).
- These are home-country lines. Once the student is abroad, also give them the destination's crisis line and the university's student-support service. If someone is in immediate danger, call local emergency services.
- Counsellors are not clinicians. Share the number, then involve a senior colleague.

**Communication note:** Families follow several faiths, so keep messages to students and parents culturally neutral. Avoid religious idioms, blessings and festival greetings unless the family uses them first. Plan contact around Poya days and festival closures (calendar below).

## 8. What Edge should check/ask first

- Which A/L did the student sit (local DoE or Edexcel/Cambridge), and when? Any retakes or private-candidate sittings?
- Is the student on the state-university waitlist, or already in a local private, TNE or HND programme? Who awards the qualification?
- For graduates: is the degree 3-year general, 4-year special/honours, or "external"? What GPA scale does the university use?
- Who is the sponsor? Where are the funds held (AD bank, PFCA, finance company), and how old are they? Is any part pawning- or loan-based?
- Does the family plan to emigrate? Is any relative abroad, and where?
- Any previous refusals, especially UK or Australia? Any asylum history in the family?
- Which A/L grades did the student get, in which three subjects? Do they meet the course's subject prerequisites? (Universities compare grades; the Z-score is irrelevant.)
- Is the degree a University of London award, or another foreign university's degree taught in Sri Lanka? Who is the awarding body?
- Are DoE certificates and RGD birth certificates authenticated? Is e-DAS fully running? It was only partly restored after the May 2026 outage (§5).
- Do names match across NIC, passport and certificates?
- Preferred language and script for parent communications: Sinhala, Tamil or English?
