# Thailand: source-market counselling guide

> Researched October 2026 for Edge, Expert Education & Visa Services' internal counsellor assistant. Visa rules, fees, thresholds and calendars change often. Every figure is marked "as of" with its source; check the linked official page before quoting it to a student or family. Where only a secondary source was available, it is labelled as secondary.

---

## 1. Student profile & goals

**Different from South Asian and Philippine markets.** Thai demand leans towards **English-language study, short courses, younger students, and "degree-and-return"**, rather than work-and-settle.

- **English first.** Thailand ranked 116th of 123 in the EF English Proficiency Index 2025, with a score of 402, in the "very low" band and 13 points down on the previous year ([EF EPI 2025 Thailand](https://www.ef.edu/epi/regions/asia/thailand/); [EF fact sheet](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-thailand-english.pdf)). Many Thai students prefer universities that offer ESL or intensive English pathways ([US Trade.gov, Thailand education services](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/thailand-education-services)). In 2022 Thailand was the #2 source of new ELICOS students in Australia by commencements ([ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/?p=37566)).
- **Short courses face cost pressure.** Australian ELICOS enrolments fell about 35% in 2025, and the AUD 2,000 visa charge (from July 2025) is reported to hit short-course providers hardest ([ICEF Monitor, 2026](https://monitor.icef.com/2026/04/australia-multiple-data-indicators-signal-further-declines-ahead-for-international-student-numbers/); [The PIE via BUILA](https://www.buila.ac.uk/articles/australias-international-student-picture-splits-as-elicos-falls-sharply)). For short English courses, compare visitor-visa study options (section 6) with a Student visa.
- **Prestige destinations.** US Trade.gov describes the UK as the most desirable destination for Thai students, followed by the US ([Trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/thailand-education-services)). There were 5,022 Thai students in US higher education in 2024/25, ranking Thailand 28th as a place of origin ([Open Doors 2025 Thailand fact sheet](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_Thailand_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf)).
- **Degree-and-return to family business.** Parents' business connections often shape career paths, and sons often join their fathers' businesses ([Cultural Atlas, Thai family](https://theculturalatlas.org/thai-culture/thai-culture-family)). A foreign master's degree is often meant to be *used in Thailand*.
- **Government scholars.** OCSC (Office of the Civil Service Commission) scholars must be admitted to OCSC-recognised institutions, and many carry a return-to-serve obligation ([University of Groningen, RTG scholarship](https://www.rug.nl/education/scholarships/royal-thai-government-scholarship-program)). OCSC also certifies degrees for civil-service appointment and pay. In 2024 it publicly refused to endorse a third-party "equivalency" of an unaccredited US institution ([Thairath, 2024](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/society/2800459)).
  - **Bond terms (OCSC, 2026 round).** The OCSC announcement of 14 November 2025 for the 2026 (BE 2569) government scholarships binds recipients to serve the assigned ministry or agency for **at least twice the funded study period**. A recipient who does not serve must repay all funding received **plus twice that amount as a penalty**, i.e. three times in total. Withdrawing after signing the contract also triggers repayment. Applicants must be 35 or under, and US or UK study must be at an institution ranked in the latest Top 20 overall or for the subject ([OCSC announcement, 2569 round](https://s3-bkk.nipa.cloud/scholar-ocsc-pub/Doc/016901_20251117_02.pdf)). The same document refers to guarantee contracts (สัญญาค้ำประกัน) for government and King's scholars, so a family member who signs as guarantor shares the liability.
  - **MHESI science and technology scholarships (proposed change).** On 16 September 2026 the Ministry of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation's scholarship committee approved cutting the service period from twice to once the study period (capped at 10 years), halving the breach penalty (from 3x to 2x total repayment), counting up to 2 years of postdoc or approved private-sector work, and allowing transfer after half the bond. These changes were to go to Cabinet ([THE STANDARD, 16 Sep 2026](https://thestandard.co/?p=1255973)). **Whether Cabinet has approved them and which cohorts they cover is not verified as of 9 Oct 2026.** Until then, quote the contract the scholar signed.
  - **King's Scholarship (ทุนเล่าเรียนหลวง).** A secondary source says it carries no civil-service bond ([Uni2Study, secondary, Aug 2026](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/ocsc-scholarship-civil-service-bond)). This is **not verified** against OCSC's own terms, and King's scholars still sign a contract and guarantee contract (OCSC document above), so have the student read their contract.
  - Ministry, university and agency scholarships each have their own contract and terms (not researched one by one). Ask for the contract before advising on course changes, deferrals or a switch of destination.
- **Younger students and high school.** Families send secondary students for English immersion or a foreign diploma. Destination child-student rules apply (section 6).

**How goals differ by segment.**

| Segment | Main question from family | Edge focus |
|---|---|---|
| ELICOS or short course (teen to adult) | Fast English improvement, safety, cost | Right visa type for the course length; homestay and welfare |
| High school (under 18) | Safety, guardianship, pathway to a good university | Welfare and guardian arrangements, the military-service timeline for boys |
| Bachelor's or master's, self-funded | Reputation, ranking, return value in Thailand | OCSC/employer recognition, ranking, family business fit |
| OCSC or agency scholar | Compliance with scholarship terms | OCSC-recognised institution list, bond terms |
| Regional (Malaysia, Singapore, etc.) | Lower cost, proximity | Institution recognition, Malaysia Graduate Pass |

## 2. Family & decision-making

- **Parents decide and pay.** Thai households are often patriarchal and defer to the oldest man. Family members are often expected to follow elders' advice and requests, and children are expected to care for parents in old age ([Cultural Atlas](https://theculturalatlas.org/thai-culture/thai-culture-family)). The same source notes that greater international study is giving students' own aspirations more weight, though family connections remain central. Bangkok families tend to be more "Western" than rural ones.
- **Kreng jai.** *Kreng jai* (consideration, reluctance to impose) can lead a Thai student or parent to decline what they want, or to agree in the meeting while holding reservations ([Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=208985)). Edge should not take "yes" as commitment. Confirm decisions in writing and check understanding with open questions.
- **Communication norms.**
  - Use a formal, polite tone.
  - Address parents respectfully and avoid pressure tactics.
  - Discuss money discreetly.
  - Expect the decision to be made at a family meeting after the counselling session, not during it.
- **Language for drafts.** Draft in **Thai** for parents and grandparents, using polite particles (ครับ/ค่ะ) according to the counsellor's gender. Draft in English for the student only where their English is strong enough. Given the EF EPI level above, default to a bilingual Thai/English summary for anything the family must act on. Use the Thai spelling of names exactly as on the ID card and house registration, and the English spelling exactly as on the passport.

## 3. Academic background

**Structure.** School is 6+3+3: Prathom 1–6, then Mathayom 1–3 (lower secondary) and Mathayom 4–6 (upper secondary, grades 10–12) ([Ministry of Education Thailand](https://bic.moe.go.th/images/stories/book/ed-eng-series/intro-ed08.pdf); [WENR](https://wenr.wes.org/archive/wenr-marchapril-2005-education-in-thailand/)). Nine years are compulsory, and twelve years of free basic education are guaranteed ([MoE](https://bic.moe.go.th/images/stories/book/ed-eng-series/intro-ed08.pdf)). Mathayom 6 completion is the entry requirement for higher education.

**Grading.** Thai transcripts use a 0–4 GPA scale, commonly in 0.5 steps: 4 = A/excellent, 1 = D, the lowest pass, and 0 = F ([OpenEduCat, secondary](https://openeducat.org/gradebook/southeast-asia/grading-scale/); [Monash College, Thailand entry](https://www.monashcollege.edu.au/study/courses/diplomas/dips-data/academic-entry-requirements-by-country/thailand)). Each school calculates its own GPA, and policies vary between public and international schools. Monash says that if the transcript shows no final GPA, the average of all Grade 10–12 subjects is used ([Monash College](https://www.monashcollege.edu.au/study/courses/diplomas/dips-data/academic-entry-requirements-by-country/thailand)). Always use the GPA printed on the official transcript, and record whether it is cumulative or final-year only.

**Domestic admission (TCAS).** Thai university entry runs through TCAS ([mytcas.com](https://www.mytcas.com)), in several rounds. Families often treat a foreign application as a parallel track to TCAS, so timing matters (section 7).

**International schools.** International schools are licensed under the Ministry of Education and the Office of the Private Education Commission (OPEC). They must be accredited by recognised bodies and are subject to ONESQA quality assessment ([Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/accreditation-for-international-schools-in-thailand); [ISAT](https://www.isat.or.th/home/accreditation/)). Their students usually present IB, A-level, AP or US-style diplomas rather than Mathayom 6. Check which credential the student actually holds.

**University calendar and degrees.**
- Many Thai universities moved to an August start in 2014; some, such as the Rajamangala group, reverted to June. As of 2026, start dates still vary: Thammasat starts on 3 August 2026, while Kasetsart's Thai-language graduate programmes start on 22 June 2026 ([Bangkok Post, 2016](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/general/911772/majority-agree-with-new-university-year); [Kasetsart 2026 calendar](https://www.grad.ku.ac.th/uploads/App69_F1/Schedule-2026-Academic-Calendar-ENG.pdf)).
- Thai bachelor's degrees are generally four years.
- Most programmes are taught in Thai. "International programmes", such as MUIC's, are taught in English ([MUIC](https://muic.mahidol.ac.th/eng/admissions/undergraduate-admission/thai-students-and-foreign-residents/)).

**MOI and English.**
- An MOI letter is only meaningful for English-taught international programmes or schools. Most Thai applicants will need a test.
- Thailand is not on the UK's majority English-speaking list ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english)).

**Professional licensing.** Outbound nursing and healthcare migration is not a defining Thai segment in the sources I found, unlike the Philippines. For a Thai health professional, check the destination regulator from scratch. I did not research Thai Nursing and Midwifery Council certification processes in depth.

**Credential evaluation.** Use the destination's standard route (WES for US and Canada, UK ENIC, or the provider's country page). If a family relies on a foreign degree being recognised back home, OCSC certification matters for government jobs, so tell them to check before enrolling ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/society/2800459)).

## 4. Money

**Sponsor norms.** Typically parents, often business owners or professionals, sometimes with grandparents or relatives contributing. The funding story usually involves **family-business income**. Expect company registration documents, business bank statements, and personal statements of the owner-parent. Ask for evidence linking business income to the sponsor's personal funds, such as dividends, salary or tax records.

**Government scholarships carry bonds** (details and the pending MHESI change in section 1). OCSC scholars must serve at least twice the funded period, or repay the funding plus a 2x penalty ([OCSC 2569 announcement](https://s3-bkk.nipa.cloud/scholar-ocsc-pub/Doc/016901_20251117_02.pdf)).

**FX and remittance (Bank of Thailand).**
- Exchange control is administered by the Bank of Thailand, with most transactions handled by commercial banks ([BOT exchange control](https://www.bot.or.th/en/our-roles/financial-markets/foreign-exchange-regulations/exchange-control-regulation.html)).
- Tuition is paid by bank transfer with the institution's invoice.
- BOT issued further FX relaxations on 1 September 2025, effective 1 December 2025 (Notification No. 34 and related circulars) ([Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/10/thailand-new-fx-ecosystem-milestone)). I could not verify from an official source the current documentary thresholds for an individual's outward education remittance, so ask the family's bank.
- **Cash.** Taking more than THB 50,000 out of Thailand (to non-neighbouring countries) needs BOT permission ([thailand.go.th](https://www.thailand.go.th/event-detail/009-010); [CAAT AIP GEN 1.3](https://aip.caat.or.th/2024-03-21-AIRAC/html/eAIP/VT-GEN-1.3-en-GB.html)). Sources conflict on the foreign-cash declaration threshold: USD 15,000 per the CAAT AIP and USD 20,000 per a Thai embassy page. Tell students to declare above the lower figure or carry cards instead.

**Loans.** Thailand's Student Loan Fund (กยศ., [studentloan.or.th](https://www.studentloan.or.th)) disburses to participating institutions. A secondary source says that makes it unusable for a full foreign degree ([Uni2Study, secondary](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/thailand-student-loan-fund-overseas-study)). I could not confirm this from the Fund itself. Some Thai banks offer overseas-education loans, but I could not verify current terms from bank sources.

**Proof-of-funds benchmarks (as of October 2026; verify):**
- **Australia:** AUD 29,710 per year living costs (from 10 May 2024) ([Navitas, secondary](https://www.navitas.com/news/article/australia-increases-international-students-financial-requirement/)).
- **Canada:** CAD 22,895 from 1 September 2025 ([Bridgepoint Law, secondary](https://bridgepointlaw.ca/canadian-study-permit-guide/)).
- **New Zealand:** NZD 20,000 per year for tertiary study, or NZD 1,667 per month for shorter courses ([INZ](https://immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/providing-evidence-and-documents-to-support-your-visa-application/student-fund-requirements/)).
- **US:** the amount on the I-20.

**Red flags.**
- Business income not reflected in personal or tax records.
- Large recent deposits.
- Funds held by a relative with no clear link to the student.
- Fixed deposits that mature after the course starts.
- Bank letters whose English names don't match the passport spelling.

## 5. Origin-side steps & documents

**Thai-side essentials (as of 9 Oct 2026):**
- **Military service deferment is a Thai process, not the destination embassy's.** Males register at 17 (Sor Dor 9), get the call-up notice (Sor Dor 35) at 20 and face selection on 1–12 April of the year they turn 21. A student abroad gets a student-status letter from the **Royal Thai Embassy or Consulate in the country of study**. He, a parent or a proxy then files it at the Thai district recruitment office where he is on the house register, and the Thai authorities issue the deferment (Sor Dor 41). The embassy itself "is not an authorised agency" for deferment. Send papers to the Thai embassy by end of January. Processing takes about 15 business days (Washington) to 4 weeks (Oslo); fees are USD 15 (Washington), AUD 25 (Canberra) and NOK 150 (Oslo) ([Oslo](https://oslo.thaiembassy.org/en/page/military-service-deferment); [Washington DC](https://washingtondc.thaiembassy.org/en/page/army-conscription); [Canberra](https://canberra.thaiembassy.org/en/content/request-for-military-draft-deferment)).
- **Legalisation now, apostille from 28 Feb 2027.** Until then, Thai documents need DCA legalisation (appointment only), then the destination embassy if it asks. DCA charges THB 200 per document for regular service (about 3 business days) or THB 400 for same-day express, plus THB 200 per translation check (secondary, Sep 2026: [Thailand Starter Kit](https://www.thailandstarterkit.com/legal/legalization-in-thailand/)). The Apostille Convention enters into force for Thailand on 28 Feb 2027. As of 9 Oct 2026 the HCCH lists no objection to Thailand's accession, and Australia, Canada, Germany, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK and the US are all parties ([HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41); [Thai MFA](https://mfa.go.th/en/content/apostille-convention-eng)). Re-check objections once the objection period closes.

| Item | Issuer / how | Notes (as of Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Department of Consular Affairs (DCA), MFA ([consular.mfa.go.th](https://consular.mfa.go.th)) | Check validity and that the English name matches school and bank records. |

Confirm which version the destination accepts. |

Appointment only ([Thai Embassy Stockholm](https://thaiembassy.se/en/services/legalization/)). |

- **Visa centres, medicals, biometrics:**
  - **UK:** VFS premium centres in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Pattaya and Phuket, plus mobile centres in Bangkok ([VFS release, Aug 2026](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/PDF/media-releases/2026/MR_EN_UKPAC-MVAC-in-Thailand_Aug-2026_Final.pdf); [gov.uk, Chiang Mai VAC](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-opens-new-visa-application-centre-in-chiang-mai)).
  - **UK TB test:** required, only at BNH Hospital or IOM, both in Bangkok ([gov.uk: TB testing in Thailand](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tuberculosis-test-for-a-uk-visa-clinics-in-thailand/tuberculosis-testing-in-thailand)).
  - **US:** Embassy Bangkok and Consulate General Chiang Mai, booked via ustraveldocs ([ustraveldocs Thailand](https://ustraveldocs.com/th/th-loc-post.asp)). Third-party sources disagree on whether Chiang Mai takes F-1 interviews, so confirm when booking.
  - **Australia and Canada:** I did not verify current VFS locations in Thailand. Check the official VFS Thailand pages.
  - **Health exams:** Australia, Canada and NZ health exams use each government's panel-physician finder.

## 6. Destination-by-destination guidance

### Australia
- **Course length decides the visa.** Short English courses of up to 3 months can be studied on a Visitor visa (600), provided study is not the main purpose of the trip ([Home Affairs Visitor 600](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/visitor-600)). Longer courses need a Student visa (500), which costs AUD 2,000 from 1 July 2025 ([BUILA/PIE](https://www.buila.ac.uk/articles/cost-of-australian-student-visa-jumps-to-aud-2-000)).
- **Genuine Student requirement.** It applies from 23 March 2024 ([ECU GS form](https://intranet.ecu.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0004/799168/ECU-Genuine-Student-Assessment-Form.pdf)). For Thai applicants the GS story is usually strong when there is clear return value, such as a family business, a Thai employer or an OCSC bond. It is weaker for repeat ELICOS enrolments with no progression.
- **Evidence levels.** These are applied through the Document Checklist Tool, and the Department does not publish country levels ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/australia-moves-india-into-highest-risk-category-student-visas?amp=1)). I found no public reporting of Thailand's level changing.
- **Processing.** MD115 prioritisation is by provider allocation ([Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/ministerial-direction-115)).
- **Under-18s.** Students under 18 need approved welfare arrangements, either through the school or a parent or relative on a Student Guardian visa (590) ([Home Affairs 590](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-guardian-590)).
- **Refusal themes:** repeated ELICOS with no progression, course choice that doesn't fit the stated career, funds that can't be traced to the sponsor, and English evidence gaps.

### United Kingdom
- **Course length decides the visa.**
  - Up to 6 months of study at an accredited institution, including English: Standard Visitor ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor/visit-to-study)).
  - English courses of more than 6 and up to 11 months (age 16+): Short-term study visa ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/study-visit-visa/overview)).
  - Longer courses: Student visa.
  - Ages 4–17 at independent schools: Child Student visa ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/child-study-visa)).
- **Credibility interviews.** These are possible, and universities increasingly pre-screen ([Univ. of Hertfordshire](https://www.herts.ac.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/428382/Credibility-Interviews-Guide-for-Students.pdf)). Stricter sponsor compliance thresholds applied from 1 June 2026 ([UKCISA](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-sponsorship-tightened-basic-compliance-assessment-metrics-and-new-rag-rating-now-live/)).
- **Graduate route.** 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027 ([Univ. of Surrey](https://help.surrey.ac.uk/article/how-long-will-i-be-eligible-stay-uk-if-my-application-graduate-route-visa-successful)). For degree-and-return families this matters less than ranking and recognition.
- **TB test:** required (section 5).

### Canada
- **Caps and PAL.** A national cap and Provincial Attestation Letters apply to most applicants; master's and PhD students at public institutions have been exempt since 1 January 2026 ([Bridgepoint Law, secondary](https://bridgepointlaw.ca/canadian-study-permit-guide/)).
- **Approval climate.** National new-permit approvals were 41% in January–September 2025 ([IRCC CIMM](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/transparency/committees/cimm-dec-04-2025/international-students.html)). I found no Thailand-specific rate.
- **Fit.** Canada suits English-pathway and college students, but cost and approval uncertainty should be explained up front.

### United States
- **214(b).** Students must show ties and intent to depart ([travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/visa-denials.html)). Thai degree-and-return profiles with a family business usually have ties to show. Coach students to explain them in their own English, because interview English matters.
- **Process changes.** Interviews are in the country of nationality or residence, and waivers were largely curtailed from September 2025 ([Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2025/9/new-home-country-only-visa-interview-rule-and-termination-of-the-interview-waiver)). F, M and J applicants are asked to make social media public ([Skift](https://skift.com/2025/06/25/state-department-asks-student-visa-applicants-to-make-social-media-accounts-public/)).
- **High school.** F-1 status allows public secondary school for at most 12 months, with full per-capita cost reimbursed. It does not allow public elementary school ([travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/study/student-visa/foreign-students-in-public-schools.html)). Private schools are the usual route for longer stays.

### New Zealand
- **Funds.** NZD 20,000 per year for tertiary study, or NZD 1,667 per month for shorter courses ([INZ](https://immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/providing-evidence-and-documents-to-support-your-visa-application/student-fund-requirements/)).
- **Fit.** Suitable for English, school and tertiary study. Confirm current visa conditions on INZ.

### Germany and Ireland (brief)
- No Thailand-specific findings. Standard routes apply: a German student visa via the German mission, with documents legalised through DCA until the apostille takes effect, and the Irish study visa.
- After 28 February 2027, check whether either country objected to Thailand's accession before relying on an apostille.

### Regional: Malaysia and others
- **Malaysia.** It is actively recruiting Thai students. EMGS reported 634 Thai students actively studying in Malaysia in 2025, citing proximity, affordability and the one-year Graduate Pass, and ran August 2026 education fairs in Chiang Mai, Bangkok and Hat Yai ([Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php/?id=2593456); [NST, Aug 2026](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/economy/2026/08/1517124/167000-international-students-contribute-rm20bil-annually)). EMGS processes all international-student visas ([education.gov.my/EMGS](https://educationmalaysia.gov.my)).
- **Branch campuses.** Malaysia hosts foreign branch campuses, e.g. offices of UK universities in Kuala Lumpur ([Exeter](https://news-archive.exeter.ac.uk/global/asia/articles/title_890742_en.html)). They can give a "UK degree near home" for families wary of distance.
- **Recognition.** Check OCSC or Thai employer recognition of the specific institution before enrolling.

## 7. Counselling do's & don'ts

**Do:**
- Identify the visa type by course length before quoting costs, especially for short English courses.
- Ask about military-service registration and deferment for every male student, and build the Thai-side deferment step (Royal Thai Embassy letter in the study country, filed at the Thai district recruitment office; section 5) into the timeline. Never send the family to the destination country's embassy for it.
- For degree-and-return families, check recognition at home (OCSC, Thai employers, family-business needs) and the institution's standing. Families value prestige, so be honest about rankings.
- Send bilingual Thai/English summaries to parents, and confirm decisions in writing (kreng jai).
- For under-18s, set up guardianship and welfare arrangements before the visa application.
- Plan legalisation lead time with the DCA (appointment only) until the apostille takes effect in February 2027.

**Don't:**
- Read a polite "yes" as agreement.
- Recommend unaccredited "fast" foreign degrees. OCSC publicly disowned third-party equivalency claims in 2024 ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/society/2800459)).
- Chain repeated ELICOS enrolments without visible progression. It undermines later genuine-student claims.
- Rely on cash transfers out of Thailand. Use bank transfers with invoices.

**Integrity and fraud.** Thai-specific fraud patterns in the sources I found centre on low-quality or unaccredited foreign institutions and their claims of local recognition, not on illegal labour recruitment as in the Philippines ([AseanNow, older report](https://aseannow.com/topic/349514-thai-students-warned-of-unqualified-foreign-schools/)). Check provider registration (Australia's CRICOS, UK sponsor list, US SEVP) and recognition at home.

**Regulation of education agents.** I found **no Thai government licence or register for education agents**. TIECA (Thai International Education Consultants Association) is a voluntary, self-regulating association registered with the Ministry of Commerce in 1997 ([Quality English–TIECA](https://quality-english.com/blog/quality-english/quality-english-and-thai-agency-association-tieca-sign-mou)). Its president was quoted saying there is "no authority to directly oversee these consultancies" ([AseanNow, undated](https://aseannow.com/topic/349514-thai-students-warned-of-unqualified-foreign-schools/)). Consumer complaints go to the Office of the Consumer Protection Board. Destination rules still bind us, e.g. Australia's onshore-transfer commission ban, reported effective 31 March 2026 ([CAQA, secondary](https://caqa.com.au/blogs/news/the-commission-rule-most-providers-still-don-t-know-about)).

**Crisis and wellbeing helplines** (checked 9 Oct 2026):
- **1323**, the Department of Mental Health's mental-health hotline: free, confidential counselling, 24 hours ([Department of Mental Health](https://www.dmh.go.th/); [1323alltime.camri.go.th](https://1323alltime.camri.go.th)). It is a Thai number, so a student already abroad needs a Thai SIM or the family to call; overseas reachability is not verified.
- **Samaritans of Thailand: 02-113-6789**, 12:00–22:00 daily ([samaritansthai.com](https://www.samaritansthai.com/)). Whether it takes calls in English is not verified.
- For immediate danger in Thailand, call 1669 (medical emergency) or 191 (police) (not verified on an official page in this research). Abroad, use the destination's emergency number and the institution's student-support service.
- **Communication note:** keep wellbeing and other messages culturally neutral. Avoid religious idioms or merit/karma framing unless the family uses them first, and never imply a setback is fate or a judgement on the student.

**Seasonal calendar.**
- **Schools.** The Thai school year traditionally runs from May to March ([HoneyKids Asia, secondary](https://honeykidsasia.com/thailand/?p=4082)). International schools typically start in August, e.g. TCIS on 3 August 2026 ([TCIS calendar](https://www.tcis.ac.th/sites/default/files/File/2026-2027%20-%20Calendar_1.pdf)).
- **Admissions.** TCAS rounds run roughly April to July (e.g. MUIC 2026) ([MUIC](https://muic.mahidol.ac.th/eng/admissions/undergraduate-admission/thai-students-and-foreign-residents/)).
- **Holidays.** Songkran (Thai New Year, 13–14 April in 2026) is the main family holiday. Other 2026 public holidays include the Visakha Bucha substitution day (1 June), Asalha Bucha (29 July), the Queen Mother's Birthday and Thai Mother's Day (12 August), the King Bhumibol Memorial Day (13 October) and Chulalongkorn Day (23 October) ([Royal Thai Embassy Berlin, official holidays 2026](https://image.mfa.go.th/mfa/0/kjBTSaxCcf/วันหยุด/วันหยุด2569/Holidays2026ENG.pdf)). Avoid deadlines and DCA appointments around them.
- **Military service.** Deferment deadlines fall before the call-up year; one university's 2024 deadline was in October 2023 ([Mahidol SIM](https://sim.sc.mahidol.ac.th/?p=3461)).
- **Military service abroad.** Thai embassies ask for deferment papers by the end of January before the 1–12 April selection ([Royal Thai Embassy Oslo](https://oslo.thaiembassy.org/en/page/military-service-deferment)).

## 8. What Edge should check/ask first

1. What is the goal: English improvement, short course, high school, degree-and-return, scholarship, or residence?
2. Student's age. If under 18, who will be the guardian, and what welfare arrangement is planned?
3. For male students: Sor Dor 9 registration status and the deferment plan.
4. Current credential: Mathayom 6 GPA (final or cumulative), an international-school diploma, or a Thai bachelor's degree. Is it a Thai or international programme?
5. English level and test plans. Is a pathway or ELICOS course needed first?
6. Course length, then the correct visa type (visitor, short-term study, or student).
7. Who pays and from what source? Family business, salary, or scholarship? Does the paperwork link the funds to the sponsor?
8. Does the degree need recognition at home (OCSC, government employer, family business)? Is there a scholarship bond?
9. Prior visa applications or refusals, and any previous study abroad.
10. Document readiness: passport name spelling, house registration, police clearance, translations, and DCA legalisation lead time (or apostille from 28 February 2027).
