# United States: dated facts register

Scope: F-1 (and J-1/M-1 where noted) student visas, SEVIS, status rules, OPT/CPT and entry restrictions, for Expert students from Nepal, India, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Philippines, Thailand and Sri Lanka.
Amounts are in USD. travel.state.gov and studyinthestates.dhs.gov block automated fetches. Where only those pages hold a fact, the row cites them and is marked "(prior knowledge — verify)" or "(secondary — verify)".
Checked: 2026-10-09.

Notes
- The "120 days before" rule is outdated. F-1 visas can now be *issued* up to 365 days early, but *entry* is still no more than 30 days before the I-20 start date. Sources: Study in the States I-20 page; 8 CFR 214.2(f)(5)(i).
- Parents visiting a graduating student from Nepal or Bangladesh may face the B1/B2 visa bond. Counsellors should not tell families that a visitor visa costs only $185. Source: embassy pages above.
- A public-profile instruction is not a waiver of DS-160 disclosure. All social media handles used in the past 5 years must be listed on the DS-160 (prior knowledge — verify).
