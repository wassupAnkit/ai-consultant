# New Zealand — Compliance guide for Expert counsellors

*Researched 9 October 2026. Rules are stated as at that date, and time-sensitive points carry "as of"; anything marked "not verified" could not be confirmed against an official source and must not be told to students as fact.*

## 1. Bottom line for counsellors
<!-- keywords: can i, is this allowed, red lines, nz compliance, quick summary, what should i do, guarantee, promise visa, mislead, new zealand law, never coach -->

**Offshore counsellors may advise on New Zealand student visas only. Everything else, and anything said while in New Zealand, needs a licensed adviser.**

- Advising on NZ immigration without a licence or exemption is an offence: up to 7 years' prison and/or NZ$100,000 when done knowingly. It applies even outside NZ ([IAA offences](https://iaa.govt.nz/about-us/what-we-do/offences-under-the-immigration-advisers-licensing-act)).
- You are exempt only while outside NZ and only for student visas ([IAA](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/can-i-give-advice/information-for-education-agents/)).
- Never advise on work, visitor, guardian, partner or residence visas, or on post-study pathways. Refer those to a licensed adviser or an NZ lawyer ([IAA factsheet](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/assets/documents/factsheets/factsheet-education-agents.pdf)).
- Never promise a visa or admission, and never give misleading information. A provider must end its contract with an agent shown to have done serious, deliberate or ongoing misleading or unlawful conduct ([Pastoral Care Code cl.38](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/assets/International/Study-in-NZ/nzqa-pastoral-care-code-of-practice-english.pdf)).
- Declare Expert's involvement on student visa applications. INZ publishes visa approval rates for each declared agent every year ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/study/for-education-providers/data-and-processing-times-for-international-student-visas/education-agent-performance-data/)).
- **Do instead:** give official INZ information, keep to student-visa advice, and refer anything else to the IAA register.

## 2. Immigration Advisers Licensing Act 2007
<!-- keywords: immigration advisers licensing act, licence, licensed adviser, licensed immigration adviser, lia, iaa register, check adviser, section 7, section 63, offence, exempt, nz lawyer, unlicensed, iaa nz -->

**Anyone giving NZ immigration advice, in NZ or offshore, must be licensed by the Immigration Advisers Authority (IAA) or be exempt.**

- **Definition:** immigration advice is "using or purporting to use, knowledge of or experience in immigration to advise, direct, assist, or represent" someone on an NZ immigration matter, whether or not for reward (s.7, quoted in the [IAA factsheet](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/assets/documents/factsheets/factsheet-education-agents.pdf), Jan 2025).
- **Not advice:** handing out public INZ information; directing someone to INZ or to the register of licensed advisers; clerical work, translation and settlement services. Information becomes advice "when you tailor it to the particular circumstances of an individual" ([IAA](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/for-advisers/adviser-tools/licensing-toolkit/who-needs-a-licence)).
- **Exempt:** NZ lawyers with a current practising certificate and their employees, MPs, diplomats, public servants, and offshore student-visa advisers (§3) ([IAA](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/for-advisers/adviser-tools/licensing-toolkit/who-needs-a-licence)).
- **Offences:** knowingly giving unlicensed advice (s.63) carries up to 7 years and/or NZ$100,000; knowingly engaging an unlicensed adviser (s.68) up to 2 years and/or NZ$10,000. Offences committed outside NZ can still be charged ([IAA](https://iaa.govt.nz/about-us/what-we-do/offences-under-the-immigration-advisers-licensing-act)). The s.63 penalty without knowledge is **not verified**.
- **Applications:** INZ will refuse to accept, or will decline, an application naming an adviser who is neither licensed nor exempt ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/getting-immigration-advice/)).
- **Check an adviser:** use the [IAA public register](https://app.mbieregisters.govt.nz/iaa/ui/start/searchForAnOccupationalRegistration), which shows licence type and history ([IAA](https://iaa.govt.nz/for-migrants)).

## 3. The offshore student-visa exemption
<!-- keywords: offshore exemption, student visa only, education agent exemption, partial removal of exemption, 2011 regulations, guardian visa, partner visa, partner work visa, work visa, visitor visa, post study work, residence pathway, husband, wife, spouse, which visa, answer a question, cover letter, advise on -->

**Offshore education agents may advise only on student visas, and only while they are outside New Zealand.**

- **The law:** the Immigration Advisers Licensing (Partial Removal of Exemption) Regulations 2011 limit the s.11(h) offshore exemption to student visa applications ([2011 Regulations](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2011/19/en/latest/)). The exact current text could not be loaded on 9 Oct 2026, so it is **not verified**.
- **Exempt (official wording):** "You are outside New Zealand and you advise on student visas only" ([IAA](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/can-i-give-advice/information-for-education-agents/)).
- **Not exempt:**
  - while you are in NZ, "even if you only advise on student visas";
  - advice on "work, visitor, residence, or any other immigration matters, in or outside New Zealand" ([IAA factsheet](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/assets/documents/factsheets/factsheet-education-agents.pdf));
  - advice to "secondary or related applicants" on other visa types, "such as work, visitor or guardian visas" ([IAA](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/for-advisers/adviser-tools/licensing-toolkit/who-needs-a-licence)). That includes a partner's work visa, a parent's guardian visa and post-study work.
- **Within the exemption:** telling a student which student visa fits, how to answer a form question, or writing a covering letter. All of these count as advice ([IAA factsheet](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/assets/documents/factsheets/factsheet-education-agents.pdf)).
- **Recording only:** without the exemption, you may still fill in a form by recording what the student tells you ([IAA factsheet](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/assets/documents/factsheets/factsheet-education-agents.pdf)).
- **Student-visa conditions:** explaining the work hours a student visa allows (up to 25 a week on new visas from 3 November 2025, [INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/upcoming-changes-to-student-visa-work-rights)) stays within the student-visa exemption *(inference)*.

## 4. Pastoral Care Code: provider obligations about agents
<!-- keywords: pastoral care code, code of practice, code 2021, clause 38, outcome 14, nzqa, code administrator, signatory, reference check, written contract, monitor agents, terminate agent, accurate information, prospective learners, subcontracted agent -->

**NZ providers are responsible for their agents under the Education (Pastoral Care of Tertiary and International Learners) Code of Practice 2021, which NZQA administers.**

- **Who counts as an agent:** anyone acting for a provider on any part of an international learner's enrolment or study, including accommodation and subcontracted agents ([Code, definitions](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/assets/International/Study-in-NZ/nzqa-pastoral-care-code-of-practice-english.pdf)).
- **Tertiary providers (clause 38)** must ([Code](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/assets/International/Study-in-NZ/nzqa-pastoral-care-code-of-practice-english.pdf)):
  - check and record references on prospective agents, to screen out false, misleading, deceptive or unlawful conduct;
  - sign a written contract with each agent;
  - monitor whether agents give "reliable information and advice about studying, working, and living in New Zealand" and act with integrity;
  - end the contract on evidence of serious, deliberate or ongoing misleading or unlawful conduct;
  - keep agents' information up to date.
- **Schools:** carry the same duties under Outcome 14, clauses 58–59 ([NZQA](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/the-code/the-code-for-education-providers/code-resources-for-tertiary-providers/education-agents-for-tertiary/)).
- **Outcome 9:** prospective learners must get "clear, accessible, accurate and sufficient information" before they study ([Code](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/assets/International/Study-in-NZ/nzqa-pastoral-care-code-of-practice-english.pdf)).
- **NZQA guidance:** it recommends that providers ask agents for declarations on conflicts of interest and on the Immigration Advisers Licensing Act, and check agents' marketing for immigration claims ([NZQA guide](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/the-code/the-code-for-education-providers/guide-to-managing-education-agents/)).
- **Administrator:** NZQA is the Code administrator. It monitors compliance and investigates complaints ([NZQA Code Administrator plan 2026–27](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/assets/Tertiary/The-Code/Code-Administrator-plan-2026-2027.pdf)).

## 5. Agents, commission and recognition
<!-- keywords: commission, enz recognised agency, enzra, agentlab, agent performance data, approval rate, decline rate, declare agent, funds transfer scheme, fts, anz, bnz, agent recognition, commission rules, poaching -->

**We found no NZ licence or recognition scheme for education agents as of 9 Oct 2026. INZ instead publishes each declared agent's visa outcomes.**

- **Recognition:** ENZ ended its Recognised Agency Programme (ENZRA) in mid-2023 ([ENZ](https://www.enz.govt.nz/news-and-research/ed-news/enz-discontinues-recognised-agency-programme)). It launched the AgentLab platform in August 2023 ([ENZ](https://www.enz.govt.nz/news-and-research/new-agentlab-platform-launched-to-support-education-agents)). The exact end date (July or August 2023) and the instruction to remove the ENZRA logo come from secondary reports and are **not verified** against ENZ.
- **Agent data:** each year INZ publishes approval rates and volumes for exempt agents declared on student visa applications. The markets include India, Nepal, the Philippines, Sri Lanka, Thailand and Vietnam, and the latest year is 2025 ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/study/for-education-providers/data-and-processing-times-for-international-student-visas/education-agent-performance-data/)).
- **Funds Transfer Scheme (FTS):** INZ may make it a condition of approval in principle ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/study/study-visas/funds-transfer-scheme/)). As of 7 Oct 2026 ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/bnz-to-join-immigration-new-zealands-funds-transfer-scheme/)):
  - it covers China, India, Nepal, the Philippines, Sri Lanka and Vietnam;
  - it runs through ANZ, and through BNZ for students at universities and polytechnics;
  - Nepal joined on 2 April 2026, where it is required for most students ([ENZ](https://www.enz.govt.nz/news-and-research/ed-news/update-from-immigration-new-zealand-8));
  - the student must complete the bank application personally.
- **Commission:** we found no NZ law that caps commission or bans commission on transfers, as of 9 Oct 2026. NZQA recommends that agent contracts ([NZQA guide](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/the-code/the-code-for-education-providers/guide-to-managing-education-agents/)):
  - set out a commission schedule;
  - pay no commission for learners already on a pathway with the provider;
  - bar agents from holding learner funds.

## 6. Complaints and escalation
<!-- keywords: complaint, complain, study complaints, istudent, dispute resolution scheme, drs, fair way, refund dispute, nzqa complaint, iaa complaint, report unlicensed adviser, student unhappy, grievance -->

**Students start with the provider. Code breaches go to NZQA, money and contract disputes go to Study Complaints, and bad immigration advice goes to the IAA.**

- **Provider first:** every tertiary provider must have a complaints process, and the student must use it first ([Ministry of Education](https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/school-leavers/available-assistance/tertiary-education-provider-complaints-process)). For international students that "includes any complaints about the agents your provider uses" ([NZQA](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/tertiary-education-in-new-zealand/what-to-expect-studying-training/)).
- **NZQA:** takes pastoral-care complaints the provider has not resolved, such as wellbeing, safety, accommodation or international learner rights. It does not settle academic or grade disputes ([Ministry of Education](https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/school-leavers/available-assistance/tertiary-education-provider-complaints-process)).
- **Study Complaints | Ngā Amuamu Tauira:** a free dispute resolution scheme for financial and contract disputes, including refunds. It replaced iStudent Complaints for international students from 1 January 2024 ([Ministry of Education](https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/school-leavers/available-assistance/tertiary-education-provider-complaints-process); [Study Complaints](https://www.studycomplaints.org.nz/)). It also covers international students at NZ secondary schools.
- **IAA:** takes complaints about licensed advisers and about anyone giving immigration advice without a licence ([IAA](https://iaa.govt.nz/for-migrants); [make a complaint](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/about-us/make-a-complaint/)).
- **Providers dropping an agent:** they should notify the learner and the relevant agency: NZQA, INZ, ENZ or the IAA ([NZQA guide](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/the-code/the-code-for-education-providers/guide-to-managing-education-agents/)).

## 7. Expert's own obligations in New Zealand
<!-- keywords: expert nz office, nz office, office in new zealand, opening, nz presence, our nz branch, visiting new zealand, visiting, hire, employ lia, expert obligations, declaration form, inz 1226, nz expansion -->

**Expert's offshore offices work under the student-visa exemption. Any Expert presence in NZ needs a licensed adviser for every visa conversation.** Points marked *(inference)* are Expert-practice readings, not stated rules.

- **Offshore offices:** advise on student visas only and declare Expert on each application ([IAA](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/can-i-give-advice/information-for-education-agents/)). The student-visa declaration (INZ 1226) has a section for a licensed or exempt person who acts for the student during processing ([INZ 1226](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/documents/forms-and-guides/inz1226.pdf)).
- **Staff in NZ:** they lose the exemption while they are in NZ, including on visits and at fairs ([IAA factsheet](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/assets/documents/factsheets/factsheet-education-agents.pdf)). An NZ office must employ a licensed adviser or refer every case *(inference)*.
- **As an employer:** knowingly using an unlicensed person to give immigration advice is an offence (s.68), and offences can be charged even if committed outside NZ ([IAA](https://iaa.govt.nz/about-us/what-we-do/offences-under-the-immigration-advisers-licensing-act)).
- **Contracts:** partner providers must check Expert's references, monitor its information and conduct, and end the contract for serious misconduct ([Code cl.38](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/assets/International/Study-in-NZ/nzqa-pastoral-care-code-of-practice-english.pdf)). Expert must answer for any sub-agents it uses *(inference)*.
- **Refusal record:** Expert's NZ approval rates are public, by market ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/study/for-education-providers/data-and-processing-times-for-international-student-visas/education-agent-performance-data/)). Weak files cost every Expert branch *(inference)*.
- **Not exempt:** an Expert staff member who is a licensed adviser cannot "choose" the exemption for student visas ([NZ Law Society](https://www.lawsociety.org.nz/news/newsroom/licensed-immigration-not-exempt-for-student-visas/) *(secondary; older article)*).
