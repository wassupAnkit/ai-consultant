# C. Campus and location

The `Campus` entity (key: institution slug + campus slug) is where the physical place a student will actually live and study is described: address and regional classification, geocode and distances, how to get from the airport and into the city, what is on site (housing beds, library, gym, medical centre, prayer rooms, childcare, parking), how safe it is after dark, and which courses run there. Regional status is the single most consequential campus fact because it changes post-study work length and migration points in Australia, New Zealand and Canada, so those rows carry a verification date and the official source. Today `ProgramRecord.campuses[]` (`src/ingest/programs.ts`) holds only campus names as free strings; the `CourseOffering` entity replaces that once campuses are first-class records. Living costs, safety indices and climate for the surrounding city are on the `City` entity in `city-cost-of-living.md`; accommodation types and arrival logistics are in `accommodation-arrival.md`; the migration consequences of regional study (485 extension, regional points, RCIP) are in `post-study-migration.md`.
