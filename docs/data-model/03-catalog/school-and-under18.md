# R. School-age and under-18 students

Parents sending a child abroad for secondary school, a foundation year at 17, or an early-entry diploma ask the S18 special-population questions: "How young can she go?", "Who will be responsible for him?", "Can I go and live with her?", "Is the homestay family checked?", "Does Year 12 there get him into university?". This domain records the under-18 visa and welfare rules (`VisaType`, `Destination×Level` with level = school), what a school offers an international student (`Institution` with `institution_type = school`), homestay and care arrangements (`Accommodation`) and how school study articulates into university (`PathwayArticulation`). The visa-side guardian pointer `E.under18_guardian_requirements` and `E.age_limits` live in E; this file holds the guardian's own visa, the welfare mechanics and the school-side facts. Dependant children of adult students are domain P, not here. Tier 3 (`05-priorities.md`); unlocks `family_planning` and school `program_selection` (S18, S14, S10, S11).
