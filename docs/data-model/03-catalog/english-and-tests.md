# I. English language and admissions tests

English is the gate every Nepali, Indian, Bangladeshi, Vietnamese, Filipino, Thai or Sri Lankan applicant must pass twice: once for the institution and once for the visa, often with different accepted tests, different minimums and different validity clocks. Today Edge stores only four flattened test blocks on a course (`ProgramRecord.englishRequirements{ielts, pte, toefl, duolingo, other[]}`) with `null` meaning both "not stated" and "not accepted". This domain introduces a **Test** entity (the catalog of every English, aptitude and language test a counsellor meets, keyed by `test_code`), a **TestAcceptance** entity (what a given course or visa accepts, with tri-state per-section minimums and variant flags such as home edition or one-skill retake), an equivalence table with a named authority, waiver rules (MOI, school English marks, prior English-medium study, internal placement tests), and the packaged English pathway as **PathwayArticulation** rows. Visa-side rows are limited to which tests and scores a visa accepts; visa rules themselves live in `destination-visa.md` (E). The five seed attributes keep their verbatim definitions and remain on Course / Institution as the counsellor's first-glance summary.
