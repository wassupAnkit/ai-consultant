# J. Pathways and articulation

The Pathways domain describes every published route into a destination course other than direct entry: foundation years, diploma-to-degree articulation, packaged English (ELICOS / pre-sessional), pre-masters and graduate-certificate-to-masters bridges, top-up degrees, 2+2 twinning, US community-college transfer, branch-to-home-campus moves and school-to-university packages. It answers the S3, S5 and S17 questions "My GPA is too low for direct entry, what else can I do?", "If I do the diploma first, how many years do I lose and how much more does it cost?", "Will I get a single CoE for the package or two visas?", "Does starting at the Malaysia campus still give me the UK Graduate Route?". The main entity is `PathwayArticulation` (from qualification to course); it references `Course` records at both ends and a pathway-provider `Institution`. Tuition of the pathway stage is recorded here only as an input to the cost-delta computation; the fee items themselves live in domain L, English pathway lengths by score gap in domain I, and visa packaging rules in domain E.
