# D. City and cost of living

The `City` entity (key: country + city slug) holds what it costs and what it feels like to live where a campus is: an itemised weekly and monthly budget (rent by housing type, utilities, groceries, transport, phone, entertainment, textbooks), the up-front money a student needs before the first pay cheque (bond, rent in advance, setup), the regional or metropolitan living-cost figure the visa authority uses, local wages and realistic part-time hours, flights and travel time from Kathmandu, Delhi and Dhaka, safety and women-safety indices with suburb notes, month-by-month climate, population, and the comfort signals South Asian students ask about most (diaspora, groceries, halal and vegetarian food, temples and mosques, remittance routes). Every money value follows the README convention `{amount, currency, period, year}` and every cost row records its `cost_source` and `cost_year` so figures from Numbeo, official statistics and university estimates are never silently mixed. The visa-side living-cost *rule* (who must show what) is in `destination-visa.md`; work-hour caps and wage law are in `life-and-work-rights.md`; per-type accommodation records are in `accommodation-arrival.md`; this file stores the city figures those rules and views consume.
