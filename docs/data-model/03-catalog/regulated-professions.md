# S. Regulated professions and registration

For nursing, teaching, engineering, accounting, medicine, pharmacy, physiotherapy, social work, law, architecture and the trades, the course is only half the answer: the student also asks "Will I be able to register and practise after this degree?", "Is my Nepali BSc Nursing recognised or do I start again?", "What English score does the nursing board want, and how recent?", "Is there an exam, how much does it cost, how long does registration take?", "Do I need a bridging program?". This domain models the `ProfessionalBody` (one record per destination × profession, with `state_variation` where registration is sub-national), the `Course`-level flag `course_leads_to_registration` and the `CourseOccupation` bridge. Professions are values in `possible_values`, not separate rows. Skills assessment for migration (the same body often acts as assessing authority) stays in G (`G.skills_assessment_authority`); course accreditation flags stay in A (`A.professionally_accredited`, `A.accreditation_bodies`); English test definitions stay in I. Registration facts are `quotable = yes` when they cite the regulator's page; judgement rows are `internal`. Tier 2 (`05-priorities.md`); unlocks `migration_pathway`, `career_roi` and `program_selection` for the health, education, engineering and accounting cohorts (S15, S16, S3, S6).
