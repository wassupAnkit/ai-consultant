# X. Agency — Expert Education public facts

Edge is an internal tool, but counsellors still ask it about their own company: "Which branch handles Pokhara students?", "Do we run PTE classes in Butwal and what do they cost?", "When is the next Australia education fair?", "What is our internal cut-off for July intake applications?", "What do we charge for a visitor-visa application?", "What does the pre-departure session cover?". This domain is the single `Agency` record for Expert Education & Visa Services, with a `region` axis for branch-level facts (`branches`, class schedules, event listings). Today the public facts live in `src/config/persona.ts` (`PERSONA.company`) and on experteducation.com pages ingested as `kb_chunks` with `docType` service/location/events; the rows here give each fact a stable name so it can be verified and refreshed rather than re-extracted from prose. Public facts are `quotable = yes`; anything about internal process, commissions or cut-offs is `internal`. Commercial partner terms are out of scope (reserved entity `PartnerAgreement`). Tier 3 (`05-priorities.md`); unlocks `company_services` and supports `test_preparation` and the S1/S7/S11/S20 questions.
