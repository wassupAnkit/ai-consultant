# A. Course

The Course domain is the academic record of a single program as an institution defines it: what level it sits at on the destination's qualifications framework, how it is structured (credits, thesis vs coursework, majors, placement, duration options), who may enter it (academic prerequisites, accepted foreign qualifications, gap and backlog rules, standardised-test and interview policy) and how it is delivered (mode, load, online share, status and capacity). It answers the S3 and S5 questions counsellors hear every day: "Is this a Master's or a graduate diploma?", "Will my three-year BSc be accepted?", "I have four backlogs, can I still get in?", "Is there an internship?", "Can I start online from Nepal?", "Is it accredited so I can register afterwards?". The main entity is `Course` (today's `kb_programs` / `ProgramRecord`); attributes that differ by campus, delivery mode or intake sit on `CourseOffering`. Money (tuition and fees), English and standardised-test acceptance details, scholarships, visa consequences, occupations and campus facts are owned by other domains and are only referenced here; the derived `psw_eligible` flag is the one exception because it is computed from course fields.
