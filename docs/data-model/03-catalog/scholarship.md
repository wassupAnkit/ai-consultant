# H. Scholarships, bursaries and discounts

Scholarships are the single most-asked money question after tuition, and today Edge holds them only as free text on a course (`ProgramRecord.scholarships[]{name, amount, text}`). This domain promotes **Scholarship** to a standalone entity keyed by `provider + slug`, so that one award can be linked to many courses, carry its own deadline, eligibility axes (level, discipline, nationality, campus, intake), competitiveness and payment mechanics, and be verified independently of the course page. The five seed attributes stay on `Institution` as the coarse "is there anything, roughly how much" summary the counsellor sees first; every other row is the detail that lets Edge say *which* award, *how much*, *by when*, *how likely* and *how it is paid*. Government schemes (Chevening, Australia Awards, Fulbright, DAAD, GKS, Irish GOI-IES, Erasmus Mundus) and PhD funding are modelled here as Scholarship rows with `provider_type = government`; `research-phd.md` (Q) references these rows rather than re-defining stipend, waiver, rounds or success rate. Discounts (sibling, alumni, early-payment) are modelled as Institution-level attributes because they are policies, not awards. Anything agent-negotiated is `quotable = internal`.
