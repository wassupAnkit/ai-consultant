# G. Occupation and career

This domain describes the destination labour market a course leads into: the `Occupation` record itself (code, title, skill level, crosswalks, framework version), its time-bounded membership of the skilled-occupation lists that gate the visas in `post-study-migration.md`, the assessing authority and Professional Year that convert a degree into a positive skills assessment, dated demand ratings, live vacancy counts, salaries with year and source, graduate-outcome rates including the international-graduate split, and the `CourseOccupation` bridge that ties a `kb_programs` record to one or more occupations with a stated confidence. It answers the counsellor's "will this course get me a job, and does the job get me PR" without duplicating visa rules (F), test facts (I) or registration steps for regulated professions (S, which holds the regulator and exam; G holds only the pointer `professional_licensing_body`). Occupation identifiers follow ANZSCO 6-digit (with the OSCA transition noted), NOC 2021 5-digit TEER, UK SOC 2020 4-digit and US SOC 2018, with ISCO-08 as the cross-destination key. Labour-market statistics are `quotable = yes` when they cite a named public dataset with a year; list-membership and visa-relevance rows are `disclaimer`; saturation and feasibility judgements are `internal`.
