# L. Fees and money

This domain holds every number a counsellor needs to answer "what will this actually cost
and how do we pay for it": tuition by offering and fee year with indexation, the long tail of
non-tuition `FeeItem` charges, first-payment and due-date rules, refunds on visa refusal,
tuition protection schemes, insurance premium tables by provider, GIC and blocked-account
products, education-loan products per origin country, indicative FX and remittance limits,
and the derived totals (`total_cost_of_attendance`, `first_year_cash_required`,
`net_fee_after_scholarship`). Institution *policies* about deposits, refund schedules,
payment plans and fee-increase wording live in `institution.md`; visa, biometrics, SEVIS,
medical and VAC fees live in `destination-visa.md`; the city living-cost breakdown lives in
`city-cost-of-living.md`; the *process* of paying from Nepal/India/Bangladesh lives in
`application-process.md`. Every `money` value follows the README convention
`{amount, currency, period, year}`, and any figure converted to NPR/INR/BDT carries
`fx_rate_used` and `fx_as_of` from `meta-provenance.md`.
