# Q. Research degrees and PhD

Research candidates ask different questions from coursework students: "Do I need a supervisor before I apply?", "Is the stipend enough to live on and is it taxed?", "Can I bring my family and can they work?", "How long does a PhD take and what if I run over?", "Can I work more than 48 hours?", "Does a research master's count for PR points?". This domain records the research-variant admission, supervision, candidature and privilege facts on `Course` (research variant), `Institution` and `Destination×Level`, and points at the money rows already defined in `scholarship.md` §PhD and research funding (`H.phd_scholarship_scheme`, `H.phd_scholarship_stipend_annual`, `H.phd_scholarship_tuition_waiver`) rather than re-defining them; the one money row kept here, `funding_stipend`, is the offer-level figure attached to a specific candidature. Visa privileges (work hours, dependants, post-study length) are cross-references into E, P and F. Tier 3 except funding rows (tier 2), per `05-priorities.md`; unlocks research `program_selection` and `scholarship_funding` (S3, S9, S15, S18).
