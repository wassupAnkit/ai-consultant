# M. Accommodation and arrival

This domain covers the six weeks around departure: where the student will sleep, and everything they must do between the visa grant and the first week of class. `Accommodation` rows (key: campus or city + type + provider) describe each housing option a counsellor can recommend: cost, bond, contract length, what is included, how far it is, whether it is guaranteed, when applications open and whether a visa refusal releases the student from the contract. Arrival rows sit on `Destination`, `Destination×Nationality`, `Region`, `Institution`, `City` and `Agency` and answer the practical questions students ask in the pre-departure session: airport pickup, temporary accommodation, orientation, SIM cards, opening a bank account before flying, getting a tax number, registering an address, what customs will confiscate, which vaccinations to carry proof of, whether the passport needs a transit visa at the layover hub, what to hand-carry, how cold it gets, and how long a home driving licence remains valid. Dated orientation, census and enrolment deadlines are `IntakeCalendar` rows in `intake-calendar.md`; the institution-wide housing-guarantee and airport-pickup flags are in `institution.md`; city rent medians and bond law are in `city-cost-of-living.md`; visa conditions such as 8533 are defined as `VisaCondition` rows in `destination-visa.md` and only their arrival-day action is stored here.
