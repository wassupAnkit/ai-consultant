# P. Family and dependants

This domain answers the S14 questions a married student, a parent or a couple asks before choosing a destination: "Can my wife come with me and work?", "Can my husband study too?", "How much extra money do I need to show for my child?", "Do my kids pay school fees in Victoria?", "Should my spouse be the main applicant instead?", "Is my pregnancy covered?". It has no entity of its own: rights and eligibility key on `Destination×Level` and `VisaRule`, money on `FeeItem` and `Region`, housing on `City`, evidence on `DocumentRequirement`. The single canonical "can dependants come at this level" fact is the seed row `E.dependents_allowed`; this file holds who counts as a dependant and what each of them may do. Visa-side fees and funds formulas stay in E (`E.dependant_visa_fee`, `E.official_living_figure_spouse`, `E.official_living_figure_child`, `E.dependant_funds_rule`); dependant English scores are `I.dependant_english_required`; family accommodation stock is M; family insurance prices are L. Every immigration fact is `disclaimer`; counsellor judgement rows (main-applicant swap, refusal-risk observations) are `internal`. Tier 2 throughout (`05-priorities.md`): it unlocks the `family_planning` playbook.
