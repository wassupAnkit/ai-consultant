# V. Meta and provenance

Every value in domains A–X carries a `Provenance` record. This is what lets Edge say *where*
a number came from, *how sure* it is, *when* it stops being trustworthy, *which* of two
conflicting figures wins and *why*, and whether the figure may be quoted to a student at
all. Today the codebase keeps provenance only at record level (`ProgramRecord.sourceUrl`,
`source`, `contentHash`, `model`, `extractedAt`, `updatedAt`, `evidence[]`, `confidence`;
`PartnerRecord.verified/updatedBy`; `KbPageDoc.lastCrawled/lastChanged/contentHash`;
`EvidenceItem.live/fetchedAt` at answer time); the catalog moves it to *value* level,
keyed by a `fact_id`, and adds verification, validity windows, conflict records, change
history, staleness policy, human overrides and usage signals. The rows below are the
attributes of that `Provenance` record plus the derived coverage and staleness views;
`PolicyChange` is referenced (as the trigger of a change) but its own fields are catalogued
with `destination-visa.md` and `destination-policy.md`.
