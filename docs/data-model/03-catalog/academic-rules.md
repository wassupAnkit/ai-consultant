# O. Academic rules

The Academic Rules domain holds the rules a student lives under once enrolled: how credit and grades work, how the year is divided, what counts as a full-time load, how attendance and course progress are monitored and what happens when they slip, when and how a visa holder may reduce load, take leave, study online, change course, level or provider, and what the institution must report to PRISMS, SEVIS or UKVI. It answers the S13 questions ("What is a WAM?", "What if I fail two subjects?", "Can I switch to a cheaper college after six months?", "Can I take a semester off to go home?") and the S10 / S17 risk questions that hang off them. The main entity is `Institution`; destination-wide rules that the institution merely implements (principal-course rule, level-change permit, SEVIS transfer) sit on `Destination` or `Destination×Level` and are `disclaimer`-quotable. Visa conditions themselves (8202, 8105, F-1 status) are defined in domain E; this domain records how the institution operationalises them.
