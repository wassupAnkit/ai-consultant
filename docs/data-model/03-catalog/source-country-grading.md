# U. Source-country grading and qualifications

Every eligibility answer starts with a conversion problem: the student holds a NEB GPA on 4.0, a CBSE percentage, a Bangladeshi HSC GPA on 5.0, a Vietnamese 10-point average, a Filipino GWA where 1.0 is best, a Thai GPAX or a Sri Lankan A/L letter string, and the course publishes its minimum in a destination scale. This domain gives Edge the **GradingScheme** entity (keyed by `origin + board or university`) with the scale, pass and distinction marks, band labels and a conversion formula to each destination scale *with the authority that endorses it* (WES, ECCTIS, NOOSR/AEI, anabin, IRCC ECA bodies, or the institution's own table), plus the **SourceCountry** facts that shape how a home qualification is read abroad: what Year 12 is called, whether the bachelor is 3 or 4 years and who accepts 3, distance-degree acceptance, backlog terminology, who issues MOI letters and transcripts, verification services, apostille availability, translation norms, name-format traps and the origin academic calendar (result months) needed for realistic timeline planning. Origin markets are NP, IN, BD, VN, PH, TH, LK; the `countries` column uses `origin:XX` style throughout. `course.md` (A) owns the course-side flags (`three_year_bachelor_accepted`, backlog policy, `moi_waiver_accepted`); this domain owns the origin-side facts those flags are evaluated against.
