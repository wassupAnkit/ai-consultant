# B. Institution

The `Institution` entity (key `kb_partners.slug`) carries everything about a provider that does not vary by course or campus: what kind of body it is, which regulators recognise it in its destination and in the student's home country, how it ranks and how students rate it, who studies there, how risky its visa profile is, and the admissions and money policies a counsellor must state correctly before an application goes in (offer types, deposits, refunds, deferrals, payment channels, release letters). It also lists the support services that decide whether a nervous first-time traveller or a family will be comfortable, and the public-facing partner status. Today `PartnerRecord` in `src/partners/registry.ts` stores only crawl plumbing (slug, name, aliases, country, website, domains, paths), so nearly every row below is `missing`. Money amounts for application fees, deposits and deferral fees live as `FeeItem` rows in `fees-and-money.md`; the rows here store the *policy* (charged or not, refundable or not, when due). Course-level overrides of institution defaults (study gap, backlogs, English waivers) live in `course.md`; scholarship facts in `scholarship.md`; visa-side rules in `destination-visa.md`.
