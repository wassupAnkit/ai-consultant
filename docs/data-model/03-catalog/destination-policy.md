# W. Destination policy and comparison

This domain holds the destination-level facts that sit above any single course, visa rule or city: "Is Canada still taking students from Nepal this year?", "Will my Australian degree be recognised by Tribhuvan University or the Public Service Commission when I come back?", "Do I need German to study in Germany?", "What is the realistic minimum budget for the UK versus Australia?", "What changed last month and does it affect my application?", "How much is AUD 30,000 in rupees?". Entities are `Destination`, `Region` (province/state allocations), `PolicyChange` (one record per announced change: announcement vs effective date, applies-to, transitional rule, affected catalog ids) and derived comparison rows. Value-level provenance and change history stay in V (`V.change_history`, `V.policy_change_ref`, `V.fx_rate_used`, `V.fx_as_of`); W's `policy_change_log` is the destination-level event those value records point at, and W's FX rows are indicative display rates only. Institution-level recognition (AIU equivalence, UGC Bangladesh) is B; city safety indexes are D (`D.safety_index`, `D.women_safety_index`). Immigration-policy rows are `disclaimer`; comparison and budget floors are `yes` when derived from quotable inputs; Expert's own reading of a policy is `internal`. Tier 2 (`05-priorities.md`); unlocks `comparative_decision`, `visa_risk_analysis` and `general_inquiry` (S2, S19, S8, S17).
