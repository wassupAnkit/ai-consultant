# N. Life and work rights

This domain answers the questions a student asks after the visa is granted and keeps asking
for the next three years: how work-hour caps are actually counted, what a shift pays and
what a payslip should show, how tax and pension refunds work, whether the Nepali or Indian
licence lets them drive, what the insurance really covers at the GP, where the mosque or
temple is, whether the semester break overlaps Dashain, who to call about a bad landlord or
an employer paying cash in hand, and which number to dial in an emergency. Core visa work
rights (hours in term and break, work-start rule, placement counting, self-employment and
gig rules, dependants) are owned by `destination-visa.md` and `family-dependants.md`; the
rows here are the finer sub-rules and the lived-experience layer that sit on top of them,
mostly keyed to `Destination`, `Region`, `City` and `Destination×Nationality`. Where the
city living-cost breakdown (`city-cost-of-living.md`), campus facilities
(`campus-location.md`) or arrival setup (`accommodation-arrival.md`) already hold the
canonical figure, this file references it rather than duplicating.
