# Philippines: source-market counselling guide

> Researched October 2026 for Edge, Expert Education & Visa Services' internal counsellor assistant. Visa rules, fees, thresholds and calendars change often. Every figure here is marked "as of" with its source; check the linked official page before quoting it to a student. Where only a secondary source was available, it is labelled as secondary.

---

## 1. Student profile & goals

**Context: a migration-and-remittance economy.** Overseas Filipinos sent a record USD 35.63 billion in cash remittances in 2025, with 39.7% coming from the United States, followed by Singapore and Saudi Arabia ([Philstar, Feb 2026, reporting BSP data](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/02/17/2508451/remittances-reach-record-high-356-billion/amp/)). Having a relative abroad is normal, so a relative abroad is often the sponsor, the role model and the destination anchor all at once. The Philippines is also one of Australia's four largest sources of nurses, an estimated 6% of its nursing workforce ([Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/731783)).

**Typical segments and goals:**

| Segment | Typical goal | Typical level/field | Counselling implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Licensed nurses (BSN + PRC licence) | Work and settle as a registered nurse | Postgraduate nursing, bridging, or straight to regulator assessment | Find out first whether the person needs a student visa at all. Registration-led or employer-led routes (section 6) may fit better than study. |
| Caregivers, allied-health and mid-career workers | Get a foothold abroad, then work rights, then residence | Vocational (aged care, community services, hospitality), graduate diplomas, master's | Highest genuine-student risk. Study must make sense as study, not as a job route. |
| Recent senior high graduates (post K-12) | Degree abroad, often where relatives live | Bachelor's, foundation, diploma | Usually a smaller segment. Parents or relatives abroad pay. |
| Family-linked applicants | Join or be near relatives (US, Canada, Australia, UK) | Any | Relatives in the destination are a funding strength but can raise immigrant-intent questions (US 214(b)). |

**Vocational-heavy profile in Australia (reported).** One analysis of Australian data reported that vocational education (VET) made up more than 70% of student visas granted to Filipinos. It also reported that offshore approval rates fell from about 80% in 2022–23 to about 40% in the first seven months of 2024 ([Independent Australia, commentary citing Studymove/government data, 2024](https://independentaustralia.net/politics/politics-display/implications-of-student-visa-boom-from-philippines,19022)). This is commentary rather than an official release, so use it as a direction of travel, not as a statistic to quote.

**Age.** In practice many Filipino applicants are mature and working, often with a spouse and children. Expect career-gap explanations, employment evidence and dependant questions to come up early. I found no official age breakdown, so treat this as a practitioner pattern, not a verified statistic.

**How goals differ by segment.** The nurse wants to know about registration and the timeline. The mid-career applicant wants to know about work rights, post-study work and permanent residence. The young graduate's family wants to know about university reputation and safety. Edge should identify the segment before recommending a destination.

## 2. Family & decision-making

- **Who decides.** Younger family members' opinions are generally treated as secondary to their elders'. The household is often described as matriarchal: "the head of the household is usually the oldest female", and she typically manages the pooled family finances ([Cultural Atlas, Filipino family](https://theculturalatlas.org/filipino-culture/filipino-culture-family)). A mother or grandmother can therefore be the real decision-maker even when the student or father does the talking.
- **Who pays.** It is often a relative abroad. Workers overseas commonly send money home for education ([Cultural Atlas](https://theculturalatlas.org/filipino-culture/filipino-culture-family)). Expect sponsors who are siblings, aunts, uncles or parents working in the US, the Middle East, Singapore and elsewhere. Their evidence comes from a third country, with its own pay slips, employment letters, tax records and immigration status.
- **Obligation and shame.** Children are expected to keep family harmony and avoid *hiya* (shame) ([Cultural Atlas](https://theculturalatlas.org/filipino-culture/filipino-culture-family)). Reciprocal obligation (*utang na loob*) is a recognised value in Philippine scholarship, and younger generations increasingly balance it against personal boundaries ([ejournals.ph](https://ejournals.ph/article.php?id=34039)). In practice, a student may hide a refusal, a debt or a weak academic record to avoid shaming the family. Ask directly and privately.
- **Spouses and dependants.** For mature applicants, the spouse is a co-decision-maker and often a co-applicant. Dependant rules vary by destination. The UK, for example, limits which Student-visa holders can bring family members ([gov.uk: Student visa family members](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/family-members)).
- **Language for drafts.** English is widely used. The Philippines ranked 28th of 123 in the EF English Proficiency Index 2025, with a score of 569 ([EF EPI 2025 fact sheet](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-philippines-english.pdf)). Draft in English by default. Offer a Filipino (Tagalog) or Taglish version for parents and grandparents, and ask about regional languages such as Cebuano or Ilocano for families outside Metro Manila. Keep the tone warm and respectful, using *po/opo* in Filipino drafts to elders.

## 3. Academic background

**K-12 transition.** The K-12 reform added two years of senior high school. Basic education is now 4 years of junior high plus 2 years of senior high school, with senior high implemented nationwide in 2016, and graduates receive a Senior High School diploma ([Nuffic, Philippines education system](https://nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/philippines/grades-and-study-results); [Alberta IQAS guide](https://www.alberta.ca/iqas-education-guide-philippines.aspx)). Many mature applicants finished the older 10-year basic cycle before university. Check each provider's Philippines entry page for how it treats pre-K-12 secondary plus a 4-year degree. I could not verify a single rule that applies across destinations.

**Basic-education grading.** DepEd uses a percentage scale on which 75 is the passing mark. Under DepEd Order 8 s. 2015 the bands are 90–100 Outstanding, 85–89 Very Satisfactory, 80–84 Satisfactory, 75–79 Fairly Satisfactory and below 75 Did Not Meet Expectations ([open-exam-prep summary of DO 8 s. 2015](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ph-let/prof-ed-assessment-learning/grading-systems-deped)). As of SY 2026–27, DepEd introduced a new transmutation table and announced a move to "zero-based" grading from SY 2027–28. A Senate inquiry has been sought ([The Post, 2026](https://thepost.net.ph/news/nation/gatchalian-seeks-senate-inquiry-into-deped-grading-system/)). Expect transcripts from 2026 onward to be read differently. The official school record is the Permanent Record (Form 137), and DepEd issues a CAV (Certification, Authentication and Verification) ([Nuffic](https://nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/philippines/grades-and-study-results)).

**University grading.** There is no single national scale. At UP and many other institutions, 1.0 is the highest grade, 3.0 is the lowest pass, 4.0 is "conditional" and 5.0 is a fail ([UPLB CAS rules](https://cas.uplb.edu.ph/?p=824); [Nuffic](https://nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/philippines/grades-and-study-results)). Some institutions use percentages (De La Salle) or letter grades (Ateneo) ([OpenEduCat, secondary](https://openeducat.org/articles/philippines-university-grading-system-explained/)). Edge must never read a Philippine "1.5" as a weak grade. Always ask for the grading legend on the back of the transcript.

**CHED authentication.** Higher-education records needed for an apostille go through CHED's Certification, Authentication and Verification (CAV). A school notice citing CHED-RO Memorandum No. 40 s. 2025 says that from 1 July 2025, requests must go through the online eCAV system and walk-ins are no longer accepted ([Southville notice](https://www.southville.edu.ph/ched-ro-memorandum-no-40-series-of-2025/)). State universities and colleges may issue their own CAV. I could not see the memo itself, so confirm the process with the registrar.

**Medium of instruction.** Many destination providers accept Philippine qualifications with MOI letters for admission. Visa rules are a separate matter. The Philippines is **not** on the UK's list of majority English-speaking countries ([gov.uk: Knowledge of English](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english)). UK degree-level sponsors with a good compliance record may assess English themselves, at CEFR B2 for degree level ([UK Parliament written answer via ParallelParliament](https://www.parallelparliament.co.uk/question/27647/visas-overseas-students)). Check each destination's test or exemption rules. Do not assume an MOI letter satisfies a visa requirement.

**Professional licensing (PRC).** Nurses sit the PRC Nursing Licensure Examination. The PRC issues certifications of good standing, passing and rating, and its request form has "abroad" and foreign-board purpose options (e.g. NCLEX, CGFNS) ([PRC PRD-26 action sheet](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/PRD-26%20Action%20Sheet%20for%20Certification%20%28for%20LERIS%29.docx)). Ratings and licences can be checked on PRC's online verification portal (LERIS). It has tabs for verifying an exam rating (exam, month/year, name, application number, birthdate) and a licence by name or by licence number plus birthdate, and PRC notes the data "may contain errors or may not be updated promptly" (checked 9 Oct 2026, [verification.prc.gov.ph](https://verification.prc.gov.ph/)). Use it as a first check only. Overseas regulators want the formal PRC certification sent through their own process. Destination regulators start from the degree and the PRC result. They do not transfer the Philippine licence directly (see section 6).

**Credential evaluation.** For the US and Canada, WES evaluates Philippine degrees, and the school sends the official transcript directly ([WES](https://www.wes.org/resource-library/blog/credential-advice/get-your-philippines-degree-evaluated/)). Alberta's IQAS publishes a Philippines guide ([alberta.ca](https://www.alberta.ca/iqas-education-guide-philippines.aspx)).

## 4. Money

**Sponsor norms.** Expect multi-sponsor funding: parents' savings, plus a relative abroad, plus sometimes a spouse's salary. Each sponsor needs a clear relationship (PSA birth or marriage certificates linking them), evidence of income, and evidence that the funds are genuinely theirs and available. A sponsor abroad should provide destination-country pay slips, an employment letter and immigration-status proof.

**FX and cash rules (Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, BSP).** As of October 2026, people may take up to **PHP 50,000** in local currency out of the country without prior BSP authorisation. Foreign currency over **USD 10,000** (or equivalent) must be declared on the electronic Currencies Declaration Form via eTravel ([PNA: BSP reminder on cross-border currency](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1226075); [DFA Brussels advisory](https://brusselspe.dfa.gov.ph/site-administrator/advisories/875-full-implementation-of-the-e-travel-customs-system)). Tuition should go by bank transfer. I could not verify the current BSP documentary thresholds for outward education remittances, so ask the student's bank.

**Proof-of-funds benchmarks (as of October 2026; verify before quoting):**
- **Australia:** AUD 29,710 per year living costs for the primary applicant, for applications from 10 May 2024, plus amounts for family members ([Navitas, secondary](https://www.navitas.com/news/article/australia-increases-international-students-financial-requirement/)).
- **Canada:** CAD 22,895 living costs for a single applicant outside Quebec from 1 September 2025, plus first-year tuition and travel ([Bridgepoint Law, secondary](https://bridgepointlaw.ca/canadian-study-permit-guide/)).
- **New Zealand:** NZD 20,000 per year for tertiary study ([Immigration NZ](https://immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/providing-evidence-and-documents-to-support-your-visa-application/student-fund-requirements/)).

**Red flags to catch before lodging:**
- A large lump-sum deposit just before applying. Advisers and Canadian practitioners flag this specifically ([Bridgepoint Law](https://bridgepointlaw.ca/canadian-study-permit-guide/)).
- Borrowed "show money", or a sponsor with no documented income.
- A sponsor relationship that the documents cannot prove.
- Funds that don't match the genuine-student story, e.g. a caregiver earning a low wage who suddenly holds large savings.
- Loans from lenders who are also offering "jobs abroad". That is a DMW illegal-recruitment pattern (section 7).

## 5. Origin-side steps & documents

| Item | Issuer / how | Notes (as of Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | DFA ([passport.gov.ph](https://passport.gov.ph)) | Check validity covers the course plus margin. |
| PSA birth / marriage certificate, CENOMAR | Philippine Statistics Authority via [PSA Serbilis](https://psaserbilis.com.ph) | Ordered online. Needed for identity, sponsor relationship and dependants. |
| NBI clearance | National Bureau of Investigation ([clearance.nbi.gov.ph](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph)) | Book online. Payment and biometrics in person ([Moneymax, secondary](https://www.moneymax.ph/government-services/articles/nbi-clearance-online)). |
| Apostille | DFA ([apostille.gov.ph](https://apostille.gov.ph); [appointment.apostille.gov.ph](https://appointment.apostille.gov.ph/)) | Online appointment only. PHP 100 regular (5 days) or PHP 200 expedited, with an e-Apostille option ([apostille.gov.ph](https://apostille.gov.ph/?p=256)). The Philippines became party to the Apostille Convention on 14 May 2019 ([Divina Law](https://www.divinalaw.com/?p=3623)). **Germany objected**, so Philippine apostilles are not valid there. The German Embassy legalises only newly issued, DFA-authenticated PSA civil documents ([German Embassy Manila](https://manila.diplo.de/ph-en/service/-/2673916)). |
| School records | DepEd CAV (basic ed); CHED eCAV (higher ed) | Needed before the DFA apostille ([Southville/CHED memo](https://www.southville.edu.ph/ched-ro-memorandum-no-40-series-of-2025/)). |
| PRC certifications | PRC (LERIS) | Good standing and rating, for nurses ([PRC form](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/PRD-26%20Action%20Sheet%20for%20Certification%20%28for%20LERIS%29.docx)). Quick licence check: [verification.prc.gov.ph](https://verification.prc.gov.ph/). |
| CFO | Commission on Filipinos Overseas ([cfo.gov.ph](https://cfo.gov.ph)) | The Guidance and Counseling Program is for marriage migrants. PDOS covers emigrants. **J-1 exchange visitors** must register with CFO and attend PDOS ([EVP Committee](https://evpcommittee.ph/programs-and-services/j-1-registration-pre-departure-orientation-seminar-pdos)). I found **no CFO requirement for ordinary degree students** on F-1/500/Student visas. A student who later emigrates (e.g. a PR grant, or marriage to a foreign national) falls into CFO's scope. |
| Departure screening | Bureau of Immigration ([immigration.gov.ph](https://immigration.gov.ph)) | The 2023 revised departure guidelines were suspended before taking effect ([PNA](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1208898)). The 2015 IACAT guidelines list age, education, financial capacity, travel history and destination as secondary-inspection triggers ([Philippine Embassy Canberra](https://philembassy.org.au/latest/advisory/copy-of-circular-036-2015-inter-agency-council-against-trafficking-iacat-revised-guidelines-on-departure-formalities-for-international-bound-passengers)). Students should carry the visa, CoE/CAS/I-20 and funding evidence in hand luggage. |
| Travel tax | TIEZA ([tieza.gov.ph](https://tieza.gov.ph/travel-tax-exemption)) | The student exemption category is for bona fide students on a foreign scholarship approved by a Philippine government agency ([TIEZA requirements](https://tieza.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Documentary-Requirements-for-Travel-Tax-Exemption.pdf)). Do not promise an exemption otherwise. |
| OEC / DMW | Department of Migrant Workers ([dmw.gov.ph](https://dmw.gov.ph)) | The OEC (OFW Clearance under RA 11641) is the exit document for OFWs, i.e. Filipinos engaged in paid work abroad ([Philippine Consulate Toronto OEC FAQ, 2024](https://www.philcongen-toronto.com/OEC/FAQs-OEC.pdf)). **Students do not need one**: the same FAQ says students on a study permit, even working part-time, show the permit at the airport instead. BI: employment-visa holders need an OEC ([Gulf News, Sept 2025](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/philippines/viral-airport-video-prompts-bureau-of-immigration-to-clarify-oec-requirement-for-ofws-1.500260887)). A nurse or caregiver leaving on a **work** visa needs DMW processing and an OEC. A graduate abroad who starts working (e.g. on a Canadian PGWP) needs an OEC for later departures (section 6, Canada). |

**Visa centres, medicals, biometrics:**
- **UK:** VFS centre in Makati (Circuit Corporate Tower Two), plus premium centres in Metro Manila, Davao and Cagayan de Oro ([VFS release 2024](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/PDF/media-releases/2024/MR_UK_VAC_launch_in_Manila_11032024.pdf); [InsiderPH](https://insiderph.com/growing-filipino-uk-travel-demand-drives-wider-visa-access)).
- **Canada:** VFS centres in Makati and Cebu ([VFS Canada–Manila](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/phl/en/can/attend-centre/manila); [Cebu](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/phl/en/can/attend-centre/cebu)).
- **Australia:** a Makati centre is reported ([Cosmo.ph, secondary](https://www.cosmo.ph/lifestyle/travel/visa-application-centers-metro-manila-a8140-20260322-bsc)).
- **US:** interviews at the US Embassy Manila ([ph.usembassy.gov](https://ph.usembassy.gov)).
- **UK TB test:** required, and only at Home Office-approved clinics: IOM Makati and PDS clinics in Manila, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo and Cagayan de Oro ([gov.uk: TB testing in the Philippines](https://gov.uk/government/publications/tuberculosis-test-for-a-uk-visa-clinics-in-the-philippines/tuberculosis-testing-in-the-philippines)).
- **Other health checks:** Australia, Canada and NZ health exams use each government's panel-physician list. Look up current clinics on the official finders, not from memory.

## 6. Destination-by-destination guidance

### Australia
- **Genuine Student (GS) requirement.** It applies to applications from 23 March 2024 and covers circumstances, ties, course choice and career benefit ([ECU GS form](https://intranet.ecu.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0004/799168/ECU-Genuine-Student-Assessment-Form.pdf)). For a mid-career caregiver, the course must logically advance the career, and returning home must be credible.
- **Risk settings.** The Department does not publish country evidence levels; applicants use the Document Checklist Tool ([summary of 2026 reporting](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/australia-moves-india-into-highest-risk-category-student-visas?amp=1)). A 2024 commentary reported the Philippines was moved to a higher-risk level in April 2024 ([Independent Australia](https://independentaustralia.net/politics/politics-display/implications-of-student-visa-boom-from-philippines,19022)). That is not officially confirmed, so assume enhanced financial and English evidence unless the checklist says otherwise.
- **Processing priority.** Ministerial Direction 115 has applied from 14 November 2025. Offshore applications are prioritised by how far the provider is through its allocation ([Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/ministerial-direction-115)). Provider choice affects processing speed.
- **Cost.** The visa application charge is AUD 2,000 from 1 July 2025 ([The PIE via BUILA](https://www.buila.ac.uk/articles/cost-of-australian-student-visa-jumps-to-aud-2-000)). Charges are indexed, so check the current fee.
- **Post-study.** From 1 July 2024, 485 settings changed, and the extra two-year extension for select degrees was discontinued ([Independent Australia](https://independentaustralia.net/politics/politics-display/implications-of-student-visa-boom-from-philippines,19022)). Verify current 485 terms on Home Affairs.
- **Nursing.** The NMBA's outcomes-based assessment (OBA) is a computer MCQ exam followed by an OSCE, for qualifications that are relevant but not substantially equivalent ([NMBA transition FAQ](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/FAQ/Transition-to-a-new-assessment-model-for-internationally-qualified-nurses-and-midwives)). A secondary guide says Philippine qualifications usually land in Stream B (OBA) and are outside the 2025 fast-track jurisdictions ([Uni2Study, secondary](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/ahpra-registration-filipino-nurses-australia)). I could not verify this on NMBA pages, which blocked fetching.
- **Common refusal themes:** GS answers that don't fit the work history, unexplained study gaps, weak funds provenance, a course that "downgrades" a prior degree, and inconsistent dependant information.

### United Kingdom
- **Credibility checks.** UKVI can interview applicants and refuse if not satisfied they are genuine. Factors include immigration history, education and post-study plans, finances, and the agent and provider ([Univ. of Hertfordshire guide](https://www.herts.ac.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/428382/Credibility-Interviews-Guide-for-Students.pdf)). Universities increasingly run their own pre-CAS interviews.
- **Sponsor pressure.** From 1 June 2026 the Basic Compliance Assessment refusal threshold fell to under 5% and enrolment rose to at least 95% ([UKCISA](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-sponsorship-tightened-basic-compliance-assessment-metrics-and-new-rag-rating-now-live/)). Expect stricter university screening of higher-risk profiles.
- **Dependants.** Most taught-course students cannot bring family ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/family-members)). This is decisive for married mid-career Filipinos.
- **Graduate route.** 2 years if the application is made by 31 December 2026, 18 months from 1 January 2027, and 3 years for PhDs ([Univ. of Surrey](https://help.surrey.ac.uk/article/how-long-will-i-be-eligible-stay-uk-if-my-application-graduate-route-visa-successful)).
- **Care-worker route closed to new overseas applicants.** Since 22 July 2025, the Health and Care Worker (Skilled Worker) route no longer takes entry-clearance applications for care workers and senior care workers (SOC 6135/6136). In-country switching stays open until 22 July 2028 for workers employed by the sponsor for at least three months ([Pinsent Masons, Jul 2025](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uk-care-worker-visa-route-closes); [EIN, 1 Jul 2025](https://www.ein.org.uk/news/major-changes-immigration-rules-raise-skilled-worker-thresholds-and-end-overseas-recruitment)). GOV.UK now lists 6135/6136 only under extending, updating or switching (checked 9 Oct 2026, [gov.uk: your job](https://www.gov.uk/health-care-worker-visa/your-job)). Anyone in the Philippines offering a UK caregiver job from abroad, or "study now, switch to care work", is selling a route that does not exist for them.
- **Nursing (registered nurses).** The UK and Philippines have a government-to-government MoU on recruiting Filipino healthcare professionals ([gov.uk MoU](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/memorandum-of-understanding-between-the-uk-and-the-philippines-on-healthcare-cooperation)). NMC overseas registration ([NMC how-to guide](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/)):
  - eligibility and qualification application, with ID and registration evidence;
  - the Test of Competence: the CBT (theory), then the OSCE (practical) in the UK;
  - the registration application, with health, character and English-language evidence and an indemnity arrangement.
- **NMC costs (as of 9 Oct 2026; fees rose on 1 October 2026).** Evaluation fee £167 (non-refundable), CBT £83, OSCE £794, registration £182. That makes £1,226 if both tests pass first time. OSCE resits are £794 full or £397 partial ([NMC costs page](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/check-ready/costs/)). The NMC how-to page still shows the old £140 evaluation fee, so quote the costs page.
- For a licensed nurse, an employer-sponsored route is often more direct than a student visa. Discuss it honestly. A UK nurse job offer means DMW processing and an OEC (section 5).
- **TB test:** required (section 5).

### Canada
- **Student Direct Stream.** It closed on 8 November 2024; Philippine residents had been eligible ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/temporary-residents/study-permits/direct-stream.html)). Everyone now uses regular processing.
- **Caps and PAL.** A national cap is in place, and most applicants need a Provincial or Territorial Attestation Letter. From 1 January 2026, master's and doctoral students at public institutions are exempt ([Bridgepoint Law, secondary](https://bridgepointlaw.ca/canadian-study-permit-guide/)).
- **Approval climate.** Nationally, new study-permit approvals were 41% in January–September 2025, against 51% a year earlier ([IRCC CIMM briefing, Dec 2025](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/transparency/committees/cimm-dec-04-2025/international-students.html)). I found no Philippines-specific rate.
- **OEC after graduation.** Students on a study permit, even working part-time, need no OEC when they fly back to Canada. PGWP or open-work-permit holders who **are working** need one, after the Migrant Workers Office verifies their contract. The OEC is free and valid for 60 days ([Philippine Consulate Toronto OEC FAQ, 2024](https://www.philcongen-toronto.com/OEC/FAQs-OEC.pdf)). Brief students before their first home visit after they start work.
- **Nursing.** Outside Quebec, internationally educated nurses start with NNAS, which assesses RN, LPN and RPN credentials and passes them to the provincial regulator, which decides on the licence ([nnas.ca](https://www.nnas.ca/)). BC uses Inspire Global Assessments (formerly NCAS) and no longer requires NNAS ([BCCNM FAQ](https://bccnm.ca/RPN/applications_registration/how_to_apply/IEN/Pages/Frequentlyaskedquestions.aspx)). BCCNM warns about agents charging high fees to file registration applications.
- **Refusal themes:** purpose of study and ties, financial sufficiency and source, and a program that doesn't fit the background.

### United States
- **214(b).** Applicants must overcome the presumption of immigrant intent with ties abroad ([travel.state.gov: Visa denials](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/visa-denials.html)). With so many Filipino families anchored in the US (the largest remittance source, above), a US-resident sponsor is both a funding strength and an intent question. Prepare the student to explain return plans truthfully.
- **Process changes (as of Sept 2025).** Interviews are at the post in the country of nationality or residence. Interview waivers were largely curtailed from 2 September 2025 ([Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2025/9/new-home-country-only-visa-interview-rule-and-termination-of-the-interview-waiver)). F, M and J applicants are asked to set social media to public ([Skift](https://skift.com/2025/06/25/state-department-asks-student-visa-applicants-to-make-social-media-accounts-public/)).
- **Nursing.** The NCLEX has been offered in Manila since 2007 ([GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/57337/pinoy-nurses-take-first-ever-nclex-in-rp/story)). Work visas require a CGFNS VisaScreen certificate ([CGFNS](https://cgfns.org/help/what-is-the-visascreen-visa-credentials-assessment-service)). This is a work route, not an F-1 route. A US work departure needs DMW processing and an OEC (section 5).

### New Zealand
- **Funds.** NZD 20,000 per year for tertiary study ([INZ](https://immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/providing-evidence-and-documents-to-support-your-visa-application/student-fund-requirements/)).
- **Nursing.** The Nursing Council moved internationally qualified nurses to an online theory exam plus a 2-day orientation and an in-person OSCE in NZ, with a transition from December 2023 ([NCNZ letter, Oct 2023](https://nurse.org.nz/user/file/2428/Oct%202023%20letter%20from%20Nursing%20Council%20re%20IQN.pdf)). Enrolled-nurse applicants from 1 September 2025 need 1,800 post-registration hours ([NCNZ](https://nursingcouncil.org.nz/NCNZ/News-section/IQN_news/Nursing-Council-introduces-new-requirements-for-internationally-qualified-enrolled-nurses.aspx)). Rules for registered nurses differ, so check the NCNZ IQN pages.

### Germany and Ireland (brief)
- **Germany.** The Triple Win programme (Federal Employment Agency, GIZ and DMW) recruits Filipino BSN nurses with an active licence and runs language training and recognition support ([Arbeitsagentur](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/zav/projects-programs/health-and-care/triple-win/pilipinas)). It is a government work route. Philippine apostilles are not valid in Germany (section 5).
- **Ireland.** NMBI assesses non-EU nurses and may require a compensation measure such as the RCSI aptitude test (MCQ, then OSCE) ([RCSI](https://www.rcsi.com/dublin/about/faculty-of-nursing-and-midwifery/education/overseas-aptitude-test)). Nurses with a job offer can enter under the Atypical Working Scheme ([Irish Immigration](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/zh/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/atypical-working-scheme/atypical-working-scheme-for-nurses/)).

## 7. Counselling do's & don'ts

**Do:**
- Establish the segment first: nurse, caregiver, young graduate or family-linked applicant. For licensed nurses, compare a direct registration or employer route with a study route before selling a course.
- Collect the full study and work timeline with dates. Gaps are common for mature applicants and must be explained consistently across the application, the GS or SOP, and any interview.
- Map every sponsor, with PSA documents linking each one to the student.
- Put the transcript grading legend on file. Remember that 1.0 is the top grade.
- Sequence documents correctly: CAV, then apostille, then translation if needed. Allow lead time for DFA appointments.

**Don't:**
- Present a student visa as a job route. The DMW has told the Senate that jobseekers should never accept student or tourist visas for overseas work, because these are not legitimate labour pathways ([PNA](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1218772)).
- Promise caregiving, nursing or other jobs, or combine study placement with job placement. That requires a DMW licence. The DMW closed Volant Academy for sending trainees to Germany on student visas to work part-time ([PNA](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1218772)). It closed a "visa consultancy" in August 2025 that offered US jobs without a licence ([Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2025/08/02/dmw-shuts-down-agency-illegally-recruiting-teachers-for-us)). In May 2026 it raided a travel-visa consultancy over caregiver job offers ([Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/05/15/dmw-raids-unlicensed-consultancy-firm)). The DMW Secretary drew the line plainly: advice on visas is fine, but a job offer plus fees without a licence is illegal recruitment ([Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2025/08/02/dmw-shuts-down-agency-illegally-recruiting-teachers-for-us)).
- Arrange "show money" or tolerate questionable funds.
- Book DFA or CFO slots through fixers. Both agencies warn against it ([apostille.gov.ph](https://apostille.gov.ph/?p=256)).

**Illegal-recruitment red flags.** Stop and escalate if a student or family reports any of these:
- **A job promised on a student or tourist visa.** The DMW says these are not legitimate labour pathways ([PNA](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1218772)). Examples: "study now, work later", caregiver jobs in Germany via language schools, US caregiver jobs on tourist visas. In May 2026 a Pasig consultancy was raided for caregiver offers to the US and fees of up to PHP 150,000 ([Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/05/15/dmw-raids-unlicensed-consultancy-firm)).
- **A UK care-worker job from overseas.** The route has been closed to new overseas applicants since 22 July 2025 (section 6, United Kingdom).
- **Placement fees for "no placement fee" destinations.** The general cap is one month's basic salary, excluding documentation costs ([lawyer-philippines.com, secondary](https://www.lawyer-philippines.com/articles/excessive-placement-fees-for-overseas-employment)). A 2014 DMW/POEA advisory reportedly lists the UK, Canada, Ireland, the Netherlands, New Zealand and US H-2B jobs as destinations where workers may not be charged at all ([dmw.gov.ph archive](https://dmw.gov.ph/archives/news/2014/12-2.pdf)). **Not verified**: the document would not load on 9 Oct 2026, so check the current DMW list.
- **An unlicensed recruiter, or a "consultancy" or "visa agency" that also offers jobs.** Visa advice is legal, but a job offer plus fees without a licence is illegal recruitment ([Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2025/08/02/dmw-shuts-down-agency-illegally-recruiting-teachers-for-us)). Check the agency's licence and the job order with the DMW before anyone pays ([dmw.gov.ph](https://dmw.gov.ph)). Report suspected illegal recruiters to the DMW.
- **A "registration agent" charging large fees to file a nurse's regulator application.** BCCNM warns about this ([BCCNM FAQ](https://bccnm.ca/RPN/applications_registration/how_to_apply/IEN/Pages/Frequentlyaskedquestions.aspx)). Nurses can apply to the NMC, NMBA, NNAS and the US boards themselves.

**Regulation of education agents.** I found no Philippine licensing regime specific to education agents. The DMW licenses recruitment, and education agents enter its jurisdiction once they offer jobs. Destination rules also bind us. Australia legislated a ban on commissions for onshore student transfers, reported effective 31 March 2026 ([CAQA, secondary](https://caqa.com.au/blogs/news/the-commission-rule-most-providers-still-don-t-know-about); [OIA](https://oia.pmc.gov.au/published-impact-analyses-and-reports/addendum-prohibiting-agent-commissions-onshore-transfers)).

**Seasonal calendar.**
- **Schools.** DepEd SY 2026–27 runs 8 June 2026 to 8 April 2027 on a new three-term calendar ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/04/18/2521743/angara-classes-start-june-8)). Senior high graduates are free from April. HEI calendars vary by institution.
- **Festivals.** In practice, the long Christmas season (December) and Holy Week (March/April) are family and travel peaks, and offices slow down, so plan document runs around them. This is a practitioner note, not a sourced claim.
- **Licensure exams.** Nurses' timelines also depend on PRC exam dates, e.g. an August 2026 NLE ([Summit Express](https://www.thesummitexpress.com/2026/09/august-september-2026-nle-verification-rating-registration-oathtaking.html?m=0)).

**Crisis and wellbeing helplines** (checked 9 Oct 2026):
- **NCMH Crisis Hotline (Department of Health, National Center for Mental Health).** Call **1553**, toll-free nationwide from landlines, or **0919-057-1553** or **0917-899-8727**. It runs 24/7, including holidays ([Daily Tribune, 3 Jan 2026, reporting DOH](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/01/03/doh-mental-health-hotline-gets-nearly-1k-calls-during-holiday-season)).
  - DOH's own hotline page ([doh.gov.ph/NCMH-Crisis-Hotline](https://doh.gov.ph/NCMH-Crisis-Hotline)) and ncmh.gov.ph would not load (403) on 9 Oct 2026, so these numbers come from a January 2026 news report, not the official page. Older lists add 0966-351-4518 and 0908-639-2672. Those are **not verified as current**.
  - Hopeline PH and other NGO lines: **not verified** this pass.
  - For immediate danger, call **911**, the national emergency number.
- For a student already abroad, give the destination's own crisis line and the university's student-support service as well. The home-country line suits families in the Philippines.

**Communication note:** Write in plain, culturally neutral language. Don't use religious idioms or blessings ("God willing", "pray for the visa") unless the family uses them first, and then mirror their wording. Families differ in faith, and some have none. Never use faith or *utang na loob* to pressure a decision.

## 8. What Edge should check/ask first

1. What is the real goal: study, a professional licence, work, settlement or family reunion? Which segment is this?
2. If they are a nurse or health worker: degree, PRC licence status, years of experience, and any NCLEX, OBA, NMC, NNAS or NCNZ progress?
3. Full education and work timeline with gaps. Were they on the pre-K-12 or K-12 cycle? Which grading legend applies?
4. Prior visa applications or refusals in any country, including the US, Canada, Australia, the UK and Japan.
5. Who pays? List every sponsor, their country, their income evidence and their relationship documents (PSA).
6. Married or with children, and do they intend to bring dependants? Check against the destination's dependant rules.
7. English test status. Is an MOI letter acceptable for the provider, and separately for the visa?
8. Document readiness: PSA, NBI, CHED or DepEd CAV, DFA apostille, PRC certifications.
9. Has anyone promised them a job, charged "placement" fees, or offered a loan tied to work abroad? If so, follow the DMW escalation (section 7 red flags). Are they leaving to study (no OEC) or to work (DMW processing and OEC)?
10. Destination fit: does the course logically follow from their background, and what will they do afterwards?
