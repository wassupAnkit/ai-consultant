# Bangladesh: Source-Market Counselling Guide

*Prepared 8 October 2026 for Expert Education & Visa Services counsellors, as retrieval material for Edge. Anything time-sensitive is dated. Where a claim could not be verified, this guide says so.*

## 1. Student profile & goals

- **Scale and growth.** UNESCO counts **52,799** Bangladeshi students abroad in 2023, up from 49,151 in 2022 ([Bonik Barta, Sept 2026](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/Jvz2e3aLg5wvHKDF)). Bangladesh Bank data put money remitted through banks for overseas education at **USD 665.9m in FY2024-25 and USD 881.7m in FY2025-26**, roughly double the FY2021-22 figure of USD 430.2m ([Prothom Alo, July 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/yjd8x5vr1c)).
- **Mostly postgraduate in the US.** According to Open Doors 2024/25, there are **20,156** Bangladeshi students in the US, which makes Bangladesh the 9th-largest place of origin. Of these, 64.6% are graduate students, 20.0% are undergraduates and 14.8% are on OPT, and 83.5% are at doctoral universities ([Open Doors fact sheet](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_Bangladesh_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf)). Expect many applicants to be master's or PhD candidates with funding (TA/RA) in STEM. Field-level data for Bangladesh is **not verified** here.
- **Motivations.**
  - *Settlement.* Counsellor experience suggests PR and settlement are the main goal for many applicants. This is a practice note: no survey data was found.
  - *Remittance economy.* Remittances were **6.57% of GDP in FY25** according to Bangladesh Bank ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/remittance-share-gdp-imports-rises-7-year-high-3993151)) and reached a record **USD 35.56bn in FY2025-26** ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/2m49au9o3r)). Working abroad and sending money home is a family norm.
  - *Unemployment and politics.* Press coverage frames the trend as "brain drain, when leaving the country becomes the ultimate goal for youths" ([Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/education/brain-drain-from-bangladesh-when-leaving-the-country-becomes-ultimate-goal-for-youths)). Political churn since 2024 (see §7) adds to this.
- **Segments.**
  - Urban Dhaka and Chattogram families more often fund full-fee UK, Australia or North America study.
  - Sylhet has deep UK family ties. The Bangladeshi ethnic group in England and Wales numbered **644,900** (1.1%) in the 2021 Census ([GOV.UK ethnicity facts](https://www.ethnicity-facts-figures.service.gov.uk/uk-population-by-ethnicity/national-and-regional-populations/population-of-england-and-wales/latest/)). A census source for the share of Sylheti origin was **not verified**.
  - Lower-income and rural applicants are more exposed to fraudulent "fund support" and to low-quality providers (see §4 and §7).
- **Age and gaps.** University "session jams" commonly delay graduation by a year or more. Public universities were shut indefinitely from 1 July 2024 by a teachers' strike, compounding existing backlogs ([TBS analysis](https://www.tbsnews.net/analysis/public-unis-and-looming-threat-session-jams-948566)). A Bangladeshi graduate who is "older than expected" is often explained by the system, not by the student. Document it rather than treat it as an unexplained gap.

## 2. Family & decision-making

- **Who decides and pays.** Parents, and often the extended family, decide and pay. Fraud reports describe families who "mortgaged land and sold gold" to fund consultancy fees ([TBS, July 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/crime/overseas-education-scam-just-thought-consultancy-owner-arrested-while-trying-leave)). Treat the sponsor, usually a father, uncle or sibling abroad, as a co-client from the first meeting.
- **Trust.** Trust in agents is low after repeated scandals (§7). Families respond well to:
  - written fee schedules
  - direct-to-institution payment
  - showing them the official verification portals themselves
- **Language.**
  - Conversation is in Bengali (Bangla script, বাংলা). Drafts to parents should be offered in Bangla.
  - All visa and academic documents should be in English. Board certificates, university transcripts and the English version of the birth certificate are issued in English (§5).
  - Name spellings must match the passport exactly across every document. The MOFA apostille and VAC steps reject mismatches ([Canada VAC Bangladesh](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/bgd/en/can/attend-centre/what-happens-at-centre); [UGV apostille guide](https://ugv.edu.bd/apply-abroad)).

## 3. Academic background

**Secondary: SSC and HSC, GPA out of 5.00** ([Wikipedia: Academic grading in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_grading_in_Bangladesh); [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/ssc-and-hsc-grading-systems-need-a-critical-review-1523028707))

| Marks | Grade | GP |
|---|---|---|
| 80–100 | A+ | 5.00 |
| 70–79 | A | 4.00 |
| 60–69 | A- | 3.50 |
| 50–59 | B | 3.00 |
| 40–49 | C | 2.00 |
| 33–39 | D | 1.00 |
| 0–32 | F | 0.00 |

- **"GPA 5" is broad.** Everything from 80 to 100 marks earns 5.0, so ask for the subject mark sheet ([Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/ssc-and-hsc-grading-systems-need-a-critical-review-1523028707)). The official board circular was **not retrieved**, so verify the bands on the board mark sheet.
- **Equivalent streams.** "SSC and equivalent" includes:
  - Dakhil, from the Madrasah Board (the HSC-level equivalent is Alim)
  - the Technical/Vocational board (BTEB)

  These boards report separately, for example a Dakhil pass rate of 55.49% in 2026 ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/education/417109/ssc-results-dhaka-tops-madrasa-board-records)). Check that the destination accepts the specific stream.
- **HSC 2024 warning.** The remaining 2024 HSC papers were cancelled after the August 2024 uprising. The cancelled subjects were graded by **"subject mapping" from SSC results**. Results came out on 15 October 2024 with a 77.78% pass rate and 145,911 GPA-5s ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/m6nvo7juii)), up from 92,365 the previous year ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/education/361916/hsc-results-published-pass-rate-77.78%2525)). HSC 2024 grades for some subjects therefore reflect SSC performance rather than an HSC exam. Weight IELTS/PTE results and subject marks accordingly, and expect some admissions teams to ask.
- **SSC 2026** results were published on 10 August 2026. The pass rate was 62.25%, a 19-year low, and girls outperformed boys (66.68% vs 57.86%) ([Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/ssc-pass-rate-falls-to-19-yr-low-of-6225pc)).
- **O/A levels** (Cambridge or Pearson) are common in urban English-medium schools. Their local administration details were **not verified** in this pass.

**University: CGPA out of 4.00.** The UGC recommended a uniform scale from the 2006-07 session: A+ = 80%+ = 4.00, A = 75–79 = 3.75, down to D = 40–44 = 2.00 and F < 40 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_grading_in_Bangladesh); [Feni University policy](https://feniuniversity.ac.bd/content/grading-policy)). Private universities vary. IBA, for example, tops out at "A" = 4.00 ([Observer](https://observerbd.com/news/420151)). Always read the scale printed on the back of the transcript.

**Three-year degrees.** National University offers a **3-year Degree (Pass)** and a **4-year Honours** degree. Most affiliated colleges mainly offer the Pass course ([Wikipedia: National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University,_Bangladesh)). How destinations read these degrees:

- **UK (ENIC, 1 Aug 2025).**
  - 3-year Bachelor's awarded from 2024 are now comparable to a **Diploma of HE** (previously a Certificate of HE).
  - 4-year Bachelor's awarded from 2025 are comparable to a **UK Bachelor's degree** (previously a Diploma of HE). Degrees awarded before those years keep the older, lower comparability.
  - Engineering degrees of any date are comparable to a Bachelor's ([UK ENIC](https://www.enic.org.uk/our-role/news/1676)).
  - In practice, a 3-year Pass graduate usually needs a pre-master's or a 1-year Bangladeshi master's for UK PGT entry. Confirm with each university.
- **Other destinations.**
  - Sweden does not accept a 3-year Bachelor's without honours for master's entry ([universityadmissions.se](https://universityadmissions.se/en/apply-to-masters/provide-application-documents-masters/country-instructions/bangladesh/)).
  - The University of Manitoba requires a 3-year Bachelor's **plus** a master's ([UManitoba](https://umanitoba.ca/faculties/graduate_studies/admissions/bangladesh.html)).
  - WES treatment of the Pass degree was **not verified** against WES itself.
- **Medium of instruction.** MOI letters are institution-specific. Private universities typically teach in English. A source for national MOI norms was **not verified**.

**Verification and evaluation**

- **Board verification (SSC/HSC).** Apply online through the board website or mygov.bd ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/education/367932/how-can-students-verify-their-certificates-online)).
- **Attestation and apostille chain for degrees.** The chain is university, then UGC, then Ministry of Education, then MOFA apostille. This sequence comes from a university and a consultant guide, not from an official page ([UGV](https://ugv.edu.bd/apply-abroad)). See §5 for the apostille itself.
- **WES (US/Canada).** WES requires sealed transcripts from the university. The Controller of Examinations is a recognised signatory ([Ontario College of Teachers](https://oct.ca/becoming-a-teacher/internationally-educated-teachers/country-info/country-b/country-bangladesh)). Use the "Required Documents" list in the student's WES account.

## 4. Money

- **Regulator.** Bangladesh Bank, through Authorised Dealer (AD) banks. Study-abroad rules are in **FE Circular 37 (30 Sep 2025), para 29**, which replaced the 2018 GFET rules ([BB FE Circular 37/2025](https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/fepd/sep302025fepd37e.pdf)). It was issued for one year and was still cited on 10 Aug 2026; any re-issue after 30 Sep 2026 is **not verified**.
  - AD banks may release foreign exchange for regular undergraduate, postgraduate, language-prerequisite and professional diploma or certificate courses at recognised institutions abroad. School-level study is excluded ([BB FE Circular Letter 30/2020](https://cdn5.ogrlegal.com/files/forex/fepd/aug232020fepdl30e.pdf)).
  - FE Circular 15 (21 Apr 2025) added admission and exam fees for foreign secondary and higher-secondary qualifications ([BB circular](https://cdn5.ogrlegal.com/files/forex/fepd/apr212025fepd15e.pdf)).
- **The "student file".** The AD bank must see: the Appendix-6 application form, the admission letter (I-20, CAS/CoE or offer), the institution's estimate of annual tuition and living costs, attested academic certificates and a valid passport, in originals and photocopies ([BB FE Circular 37/2025, para 29](https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/fepd/sep302025fepd37e.pdf)). Banks add KYC and a file fee ([Sonali Bank](https://studentfile.sonalibank.com.bd/studentfile/); [IFIC](https://ificbank.com.bd/other-services/student-file)). No regulatory turnaround is set. A TIN is not on Bangladesh Bank's list; whether banks ask for one is **not verified**.
- **What the file pays.** Payment follows the institution's estimate, **one academic year at a time**, and later releases need a progress report. Tuition goes to the institution or its named payment platform; living costs can go on the student's international card. Seat deposits, mandatory health cover and refundable blocked accounts are allowed. Use **one AD branch** for the whole course; files move bank to bank, never through the student (same para 29).
- **International cards (from 10 Aug 2026).** Under **FEPD-1 Circular 29**, banks may load forex onto a debit, prepaid or credit card for education payments. The card can be in the student's or the payer's name. There is no fixed cap: the bank sets the amount and validity. It does **not** use the travel quota, and the other study-abroad rules are unchanged ([BB FEPD-1 Circular 29/2026](https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/fepd/aug102026fepd-129e.pdf); [UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/bb-allows-intl-card-payments-for-overseas-study-fees/192746)).
- **2022–2024 dollar crisis.**
  - Reserves fell to USD 29.85bn in May 2023 ([PIE News, July 2023](https://thepienews.com/bangladeshi-forex-reserves-causing-messy-student-payment-issues/)).
  - Many private banks stopped opening new student files, and tuition transfers were delayed ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/studying-abroad-students-struggle-open-profiles-dollar-crisis-3540301)).
  - No 2026 reports of the same bottleneck were found. Still, confirm that the student's bank is opening files **before** the deposit deadline.
- **Currency.**
  - The taka moved from about Tk 86/USD (Jan 2022) ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/taka-hits-86-against-dollar-first-time-2936036)) to Tk 122 at the end of 2024, a 12.72% fall in 2024 alone ([UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Business/bb-dollar-taka-loses-1272-value-in-2024-as-dollar-strengthens/150269)).
  - Bangladesh Bank introduced a crawling peg at Tk 117 in May 2024 ([Jago News](https://www.jagonews24.com/en/biz-econ/news/74404)). It announced a market-based rate on 14 May 2025 to unlock IMF tranches ([The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/05/14/fes35-bangla-imf-lending.html)).
  - The rate was roughly flat at about Tk 122–123 from mid-2025 to June 2026 ([Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/PhBnSsMwfnWPU5rT); [Ittefaq, July 2026](https://en.ittefaq.com.bd/11929/usd-stays-around-tk122-despite-market-based-rate)).
  - Build a buffer of 5–10% into BDT budgets, because the regime is still described as "managed flexibility" ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/bb-eases-currency-controls-imf-reform-push-3894811)).
- **Loans.**
  - Local banks offer overseas education loans, for example [Jamuna Bank](https://jamunabankbd.com/education-loan/) and Mercantile Bank (up to Tk 20 lakh, per [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/134008), undated).
  - MPOWER markets no-cosigner loans for the US and Canada ([MPOWER](https://www.mpowerfinancing.com/en-bd/financial-empowerment/education-loans-bangladeshi-students)).
  - Loan sizes rarely cover full costs.
- **Proof-of-funds red flags.** These are critical for Bangladesh.
  - *Fake solvency certificates.* An investigation in September 2026 describes branch staff charging **BDT 2–5 lakh** for documents showing fictitious **BDT 40–60 lakh** deposits. One method recycles one deposit as collateral for successive internal loans. Facebook "fund support" adverts are also described ([Bonik Barta, 8 Sept 2026](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/Jvz2e3aLg5wvHKDF)). Several embassies reportedly reject statements from specific banks (same source).
  - *QR codes on statements.* Bangladesh Bank's BRPD circular of **12 May 2026**, issued after repeated requests from embassies and visa centres, requires an online-verifiable **QR code** on customer-requested bank statements and on solvency and investment certificates. The QR code must confirm the account holder, account number, opening and closing balances and issue date, and records must stay verifiable online for at least six months. Banks had 90 days to comply, so the deadline was about **10 Aug 2026** ([Bangladesh Monitor](https://www.bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/en/bangladesh-bank-orders-qr-codes-on-bank-statements-for-visa-applications)). As of 9 Oct 2026, it is **not verified** whether every bank complies or whether any UK, Australian or other mission has formally made QR-coded statements mandatory. Ask for QR-coded statements and scan them yourself; treat a post-August statement without a working QR code as a red flag.
  - *Recent or unexplained deposits.* Watch for large recent deposits, FDRs opened just before lodgement, loans dressed up as savings, and sponsors whose TIN, tax returns, trade licence or land documents don't support the balance.

## 6. Destination-by-destination guidance

**Australia notes**

- Bangladesh had briefly been raised to Level 1 in September 2025 ([Times Higher Education, Oct 2025](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/739407)). Sources disagree on whether the January 2026 move was from Level 1 or Level 2.
- Higher-education visa success had been 99% in 2025, but providers raised course-hopping concerns (same THE source).
- Also apply:
  - Ministerial Direction 115 provider allocations ([Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/ministerial-direction-115))
  - the AUD 2,500 visa charge from 1 July 2026 ([Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/student-visa-application-charge-increase))
  - living costs of AUD 29,710 ([EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/australia-financial-requirement-student-visas/))
- Always run the [Document Checklist Tool](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/web-evidentiary-tool).

**UK notes**

- Bangladesh and Pakistan together made up about half of all 23,036 student visa refusals in the year to Sept 2025 (TBS, above).
- University actions:
  - London Met stopped recruiting from Bangladesh (60% of its refusals).
  - Hertfordshire paused until September 2026.
  - Wolverhampton is not taking Bangladeshi undergraduates.
  - Sunderland and Coventry have suspended recruitment.
  - Derby paused Pakistan and Bangladesh recruitment in March 2026 "until at least Autumn 2026". Students who already held a CAS could proceed; other applications were cancelled with deposits refunded ([PIE News, 10 Mar 2026](https://thepienews.com/derby-suspends-pakistan-and-bangladesh-recruitment-as-uk-compliance-pressures-grow/)). London Met's withdrawal dates from July 2025 (same source).
- **Current status (as of 9 Oct 2026): not verified.** Several pauses ended in September or autumn 2026 (Hertfordshire, Derby) or covered one intake. No report of a reopening and no Home Office statement on the pauses were found. Confirm with each university's admissions page or partner manager before applying.
- Of the 14,243 asylum claimants who had entered on student visas (year to Sept 2025), **12% were Bangladeshi** ([GSRA](https://gsra.org.uk/?p=6801)). Bangladeshi asylum claims hit a record 7,225 in 2024 ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/uycnt6sy2i)).
- Visa grants still rose 71% to **10,828** in 2025 ([Nairametrics citing Home Office](https://nairametrics.com/2026/02/26/uk-grants-30204-study-visas-to-nigerians-in-2025-up-59-year-on-year/)).
- A Dhaka counsellor estimated that about 70% of applicants are called for credibility interviews (THE, above; this is anecdotal).
- Sponsors now need refusal rates under 5% ([Wonkhe](https://wonkhe.com/blogs/the-new-ukvi-student-sponsor-regime-goes-live/)). The Graduate Route shrinks to 18 months from Jan 2027 ([PIE](https://thepienews.com/shorter-graduate-route-from-january-2027-uk-govt-confirms/)).
- **Implication:** UK universities will screen Bangladeshi files hard. Prioritise strong academics, clean funds and clear course progression.

**Canada notes**

- 2026 caps and the PAL exemption for public-DLI master's and PhD students apply ([CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2025/11/canada-announces-2026-study-permit-cap-1162628.html)).
- An official Bangladesh-specific approval rate was **not verified**. A local consultant estimated about a 40% drop in grants in 2024 ([Daily Star PDF](https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2025/02/27/Bangladesh/RIS/5_04/c99e313b7e3f9f84_04.pdf)).

**USA notes**

- The Dhaka embassy was closed for interviews from 16 July to 1 September 2024, and more than 10,000 appointments were rescheduled ([Bonik Barta](https://bonikbarta.net/home/news_description/398144/U.S.-Embassy-reschedules-over-10000-visa-interviews-in-Dhaka)).
- In 2025 one student's earliest slot was about 7 months out ([TBS, Sept 2025](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/how-visa-complications-are-affecting-bangladeshi-students-1227396)).
- Current rules:
  - interview in the country of nationality or residence
  - social-media vetting with profiles set to public
  - the USD 250 integrity fee ([Miller Mayer](https://millermayer.com/dos-resumes-visa-processing-for-foreign-students-and-exchange-visitors-announces-enhanced-social-media-vetting/); [BU](https://www.bu.edu/isso/2026/05/19/visa-integrity-fee/))
- The B-visa **bond** applies to parents visiting, **not** to F-1 ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/foreign-affairs/416459/us-makes-visa-bond-requirement-permanent-raises)).
- 214(b) refusals hinge on home ties. Coach the student on a credible return or career narrative, and never script it.

**Germany notes.** The blocked account is **€11,904** (€992 a month) ([German Embassy Dhaka](https://dhaka.diplo.de/bd-en/service/2685884-2685884)). Register on the CSP waiting list very early, or steer the student toward scholarships or a PhD.

**Alternatives.** Bank officials report rising student files for South Korea, Malaysia, China, Japan and New Zealand ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/yjd8x5vr1c)). Numbers by destination were **not verified**.

## 7. Counselling do's & don'ts

**The agent sector is unregulated.** Anyone with a trade licence can operate as an education consultant. About 2,000 firms are active in Dhaka, but the Foreign Admission and Career Development Consultants Association has only a few hundred members. Fraudsters are charged under minor Penal Code fraud clauses and get bail ([Financial Express, July 2026](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/article/the-other-side-of-studying-abroad)). BOESL and BMET regulate *labour* migration, not student agents. Their exact remit was **not verified** in this pass.

**Integrity climate**

- **Joint warning.** On 8 April 2026, **13 missions** (including the UK, Australia, Canada and Germany) jointly warned against falsified documents and unlicensed agents, stating that "no mission is affiliated with any agent" ([UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/13-countries-urge-bangladesh-visa-applicants-to-follow-official-procedures/183179)).
- **Sylhet case.** A Sylhet consultancy director was arrested after fake bank statements were sent to the US Embassy ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/294814/man-arrested-over-sylhet-us-embassy-fraud-case)).
- **Just Thought case.** In July 2026 the owner of "Just Thought" and four linked agencies was arrested over about Tk 15.5 crore taken from about 350 students ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/crime/overseas-education-scam-just-thought-consultancy-owner-arrested-while-trying-leave)).

**Do**

- Verify every statement by QR code or a bank call.
- Trace the source of funds through tax returns, the business and land.
- Pay institutions directly through the student file or card.
- Explain to the family how HSC 2024 results were graded.
- Flag 3-year Pass degrees early.

**Don't**

- Accept "fund support" arrangements.
- Promise visas.
- Route students to providers that are pausing Bangladesh intake.
- Coach asylum-adjacent narratives.

**Crisis and wellbeing helplines** (checked 9 Oct 2026):

- **999:** national emergency number (police, fire, ambulance) ([DGHS](https://www.dghs.gov.bd/)).
- **16263 (Shastho Batayon):** government 24-hour health call centre under DGHS. It is a general health line, not a dedicated mental-health service ([DGHS](https://www.dghs.gov.bd/)).
- **Kaan Pete Roi:** emotional-support and suicide-prevention helpline run by trained volunteers since 2013 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaan_Pete_Roi)). Its number is listed as **+880 9612-119911** ([Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/countries/bd)). This could not be checked on Kaan Pete Roi's own site: on 9 Oct 2026, kaanpeteroi.org redirected to an unrelated domain. Do not send students to that link. Confirm the number and hours before sharing (**not verified** on an official source).
- **1098:** Bangladesh Child Helpline, free and 24/7, for students under 18 ([Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/countries/bd)).
- No dedicated government mental-health hotline could be verified. For a student already abroad, use the destination's emergency number and the university's support services first.
- **Communication note:** keep wording culturally neutral. Don't use religious idioms or blessings unless the family uses them first, and don't assume a family's religion from their name or region.

**Political context**

- During the July 2024 uprising there was a near-total internet blackout, and universities were closed from 17 July ([VOA/AP](https://www.voanews.com/a/internet-still-down-in-bangladesh-despite-apparent-calm-following-deadly-protests-/7707887.html)). Embassies also suspended services, as noted above.
- The BNP won the February 2026 election. Election Commission figures gave the BNP alliance 212 seats ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/2/13/bangladesh-election-results-2026-who-won-who-lost-whats-next); [EWN](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/02/13/bangladesh-bnp-nationalists-win-elections-official-results)). Post-election stability was **not verified**, so build slack into document timelines.

**Calendar**

- **SSC.** Exams run April–May, with results around August. In 2026 they were published on 10 August ([Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/ssc-pass-rate-falls-to-19-yr-low-of-6225pc)).
- **HSC.** Results usually come in mid-October (15 Oct in 2024, per [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/m6nvo7juii)). The **HSC 2026 result date was unconfirmed** at the time of writing. This timing makes January and February intakes the natural fit for fresh HSC graduates.
- **Holidays.** Fixed closures include 21 Feb, 26 Mar, Pohela Boishakh on **14 Apr**, and 16 Dec. Moon-dependent holidays shift each year. Unofficial projections for 2027 put Eid-ul-Fitr around 9–11 March and Eid-ul-Adha around 16–18 May, with Durga Puja in early October ([Office Holidays](https://www.officeholidays.com/countries/bangladesh/2027)). Check the Cabinet Division gazette once it is published.
- **Planning.** Banks, boards and ministries close for multi-day Eid breaks. Productivity dips during Ramadan, so schedule bank documents and attestations around these periods.

## 8. What Edge should check/ask first

1. Which board and stream the SSC/HSC came from (general, Dakhil/Alim, BTEB, or O/A level), and the HSC year. If it was 2024, note the subject-mapped grades.
2. Whether the degree is a 3-year Pass or a 4-year Honours, the awarding university, its grading scale, and the year awarded (UK ENIC dates).
3. Any study gap or session-jam delay, and evidence for it.
4. Who the sponsor is, their relationship, and their documented income (TIN, tax returns, trade licence, land).
5. Whether bank statements are QR-verifiable, and the history of each deposit and FDR.
6. Whether the student's bank is currently opening student files, or whether card payment is planned.
7. Prior refusals in any country, especially the UK, US or Canada.
8. Whether the target provider is currently accepting Bangladeshi applicants.
9. Whether any agent or "fund support" service has been used already.
10. The status of the passport, PCC, BRN/English birth certificate, NID and apostille.
11. For Germany: the CSP waiting-list registration date.
