# Nepal — Source-Market Counselling Guide

*Reference for Edge and Expert counsellors. Researched 8 October 2026. Nepal policy changed a great deal in 2026: a new government, a renamed ministry, new consultancy regulations and stricter Australian assessment. Re-check anything marked "as of" before you rely on it.*

## 1. Student profile & goals

- **Scale.** About one in five Nepali tertiary-age students studies abroad. PIE News puts Nepal's outbound mobility ratio at about 19%, against 1.3% for India ([PIE News, Apr 2026](https://thepienews.com/nepal-proposes-scrapping-current-noc-process-in-sweeping-reform-push/)). The ministry issued **114,924 study-abroad No Objection Certificates (NOCs) in FY 2082/83** (mid-July 2025 to mid-July 2026), according to its annual progress report ([Collegenp, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.collegenp.com/news/education-ministry-annual-progress-report-2082-83)). It issued 112,968 in FY 2023/24 ([Onlinekhabar, Feb 2025](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/over-600000-students-obtained-noc-in-a-decade.html)). One student can hold more than one NOC, so these counts overstate actual departures (same source).
- **Main destinations.** Australia, Japan, the UK, Canada and the US lead, followed by South Korea and India ([Onlinekhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/over-600000-students-obtained-noc-in-a-decade.html)). Since 2024, Australia's tightening has pushed demand towards the UK and US (see §6).
- **Levels and segments.**
  - The UK market is mostly undergraduate and price-sensitive. PIE estimates about 75% of it is looking for affordable undergraduate options ([PIE News, Jun 2025](https://thepienews.com/explained-why-nepalese-students-are-choosing-the-uk/)).
  - US undergraduate enrolment from Nepal rose 69% in 2024/25, and 17.2% of Nepali students in the US are at community colleges ([Open Doors Nepal fact sheet 2025](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_Nepal_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf); [ACA summary](https://aca-secretariat.be/newsletter/open-doors-2025-update-on-international-students-in-u-s-higher-education/)).
  - Japan and South Korea are large language-school routes, mainly for students from lower-income families.
- **Motivations.** Many students aim to work, settle or send money home rather than earn a degree and return. Remittances were **25.99% of GDP in 2024** ([World Bank API](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/NP/indicator/BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS?format=json&date=2021:2024)).
  - Australian commentators link the 2026 tightening partly to Nepali students changing courses and extending their stay without finishing ([Kathmandu Post, Jul 2026](https://kathmandupost.com/nepali-diaspora/2026/07/26/australia-tightens-student-visa-rules-rejects-64-percent-of-nepali-applications)).
  - Police are investigating consultancies that allegedly sent workers abroad on student visas ([Kathmandu Post, May 2026](https://www.kathmandupost.com/national/2026/05/16/arrests-device-seizures-in-crackdown-on-consultancies-over-shady-student-migration-process)).
- **Counselling implication.** Probe the student's real goal early. A study plan written around work rights is the biggest genuineness risk for Nepali applicants.

## 2. Family & decision-making

- **Who decides and pays.** Parents and the extended family usually pay, often through property-backed loans. Enroly estimates that 78% of Nepali UK-bound students are funded wholly or partly by education loans ([PIE News, Jun 2025](https://thepienews.com/explained-why-nepalese-students-are-choosing-the-uk/)).
- **Sponsor paperwork.**
  - The sponsoring parent normally needs a ward-office **relationship certificate**.
  - Their income is evidenced with a municipal **income-source certificate**, tax clearance and a property valuation (see §4).
- **Involving parents.**
  - Hold at least one session with the paying parent.
  - Explain the visa fee, which is non-refundable even on refusal. Australia's is A$2,500, about Rs 260,000 ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/nepali-diaspora/2026/07/26/australia-tightens-student-visa-rules-rejects-64-percent-of-nepali-applications)).
  - Explain the loan obligations and the current refusal rates.
  - Families often trust word-of-mouth and the "agent who got my cousin a visa". Counter that with written, sourced figures.
- **Language and script.** Nepali is written in Devanagari. Official documents are bilingual or in English: citizenship certificates are in Nepali, while transcripts are in English. Offer parents summaries and fee breakdowns in Nepali (Devanagari). Keep everything that goes to universities or immigration in English, with certified translations of any Nepali-language civil documents.

## 3. Academic background

**School: NEB letter grading.** This scale applies to SEE (Grade 10) and Grades 11–12, which have used letter grades since the 2017/2021 reforms ([Nuffic](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/nepal/grades-and-study-results); [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/blog/letter-grading-system-in-nepal-grade-1-12/)).

| % | Grade | GP | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | A+ | 4.0 | Outstanding |
| 80–89 | A | 3.6 | Excellent |
| 70–79 | B+ | 3.2 | Very good |
| 60–69 | B | 2.8 | Good |
| 50–59 | C+ | 2.4 | Satisfactory |
| 40–49 | C | 2.0 | Acceptable |
| 35–39 | D | 1.6 | Basic |
| <35 | NG | — | Not Graded |

- **NG means a subject was not passed.** It is fixed through a later grade-increment exam. Sources disagree on the exact cut-offs. Most say 35% in theory, and some add a separate internal or practical cut-off ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/grade-12-2080-results-display-upward-trend/)). Always ask for the final, post-supplementary marksheet.
- **NG is common.** In 2082 BS (2025), 38.83% of Grade 12 candidates were non-graded ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/grade-12-results-2082-published/)).
- **Older records:**
  - Before 2017, the HSEB "+2" used divisions: Distinction ≥75%, First ≥60%, Second ≥45%, Pass ≥35% ([Nuffic](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/nepal/grades-and-study-results)).
  - SLC records use percentages and divisions.
- **University.** TU grades on A (4.0) / A− (3.7) / B+ (3.3) / B (3.0) / B− (2.7). Older TU and other university records use percentage divisions ([Nuffic](https://www.nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/nepal/grades-and-study-results)). Nuffic also notes:
  - Character certificates are not official qualifications.
  - Provisional certificates are not enough for credential evaluation.
- **Three-year bachelor's degrees (older TU BBS, BA and similar).** UK acceptance varies by institution:
  - **Hertfordshire** asks for 65% on a three-year Nepali degree against 60% on a four-year one ([Herts](https://herts.ac.uk/international/guidance-for-your-region/south-asia/nepal)). **Portsmouth** sets the same split ([Portsmouth](https://corporate-governance.docstore.port.ac.uk/study/international-students/your-country/nepal)).
  - **LSBU** accepts 53% or above on a three-year degree ([LSBU](https://lsbu.ac.uk/international/your-country/nepal)).
  - **Exeter** accepts a three-year degree only alongside a master's at 80% or GPA 3.7 ([Exeter](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/international-students/nepal/)).
  - **London Met** and **University Centre Leeds** write their postgraduate entry around four-year degrees ([London Met](https://www.londonmet.ac.uk/international/applying/countries/students-from-nepal/); [UCL Leeds](https://ucleeds.ac.uk/international/your-country/nepal)).
  - US graduate schools and Canadian institutions often want four years or a credential evaluation (not verified per institution).
  - Australia's treatment of three-year degrees was not verified here; check each provider.
- **English.** Some UK universities waive tests on NEB XII English grades. London Met, for example, accepts grade A, or B+ for standard courses ([London Met](https://www.londonmet.ac.uk/international/applying/countries/students-from-nepal/)). Medium-of-instruction (MOI) letters are widely used in UK applications, often through TNE partner colleges ([PIE News](https://thepienews.com/explained-why-nepalese-students-are-choosing-the-uk/)). Nepal is not a majority-English-speaking country for UKVI purposes, so check each university's own English policy.
- **Verification and evaluation:**
  - **NEB** issues verification letters and can send records in a sealed envelope to WES ([Collegenp](https://www.collegenp.com/service/national-examinations-board-neb-neb-services)).
  - **Kathmandu University** accepts online WES requests using the WES reference number ([KU exams](https://exam.ku.edu.np/?p=4811)).
  - **TU** transcripts come from the Office of the Controller of Examinations ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/blog/how-apply-transcript-tribhuvan-university/)).
  - Domestic certification of Grade 10/12 certificates requires NEB exam-control verification under the Educational Certificate Certification Procedure 2081 ([Collegenp, Jul 2024](https://www.collegenp.com/news/new-educational-certificate-certification-procedure-approved-from-moest)).
- **Medicine abroad.** Students also need a separate eligibility letter for foreign medical study ([Collegenp](https://www.collegenp.com/news/education-ministry-annual-progress-report-2082-83)).

## 4. Money

- **Regulator.** Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) sets foreign-exchange rules. Banks will not convert rupees for tuition or living costs without the ministry's NOC ([Muktinath Bank](https://www.muktinathbank.com.np/general-banking/loan/education-loan); [Oxford Somerville](https://www.some.ox.ac.uk/blog/the-no-objection-certificate-policy-in-nepal/)).
- **June 2026 NRB changes (as of Jun 2026).** NRB amended **Unified Circular-2082** ([Clickmandu, 9 Jun 2026](https://english.clickmandu.com/2026/06/9633/)):
  - Students may remit up to 5% above the required amount to cover exchange-rate movement.
  - Banks may release funds in a destination currency that is not on NRB's list when an embassy requires it, against documents.
  - Digitally signed documents are accepted.
- **Student forex rules (as of 9 Oct 2026).** The rules sit in circular 8 ("foreign exchange for higher education") of NRB's **Unified Circular 2082**, issued on 24 Chaitra 2082, about 7 Apr 2026 ([NRB Unified Circular 2082, pp. 132–137](https://www.nrb.org.np/fxm/%e0%a5%a7%e0%a5%a7-%e0%a5%a8%e0%a5%a6%e0%a5%ae%e0%a5%a8-%e0%a5%ae%e0%a5%a9-%e0%a4%8f%e0%a4%95%e0%a5%80%e0%a4%95%e0%a5%83%e0%a4%a4-%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%b0%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%8d/)). In short: living costs are capped at USD 8,000 a year for Canada, the USA, Australia and Europe when the documents state no amount; later instalments go through the same bank; and a PAN is needed above USD 5,000. NRB's latest amendment, circular 2/2083-84 of about 5 Oct 2026, did not change the study rules ([NRB](https://www.nrb.org.np/fxm/%e0%a5%a8-%e0%a5%a8%e0%a5%a6%e0%a5%ae%e0%a5%a9-%e0%a5%ae%e0%a5%aa%e0%a4%83-%e0%a4%8f%e0%a4%95%e0%a5%80%e0%a4%95%e0%a5%83%e0%a4%a4-%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%b0%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%8d/)).
  - **Tuition** is paid by bank transfer, in the institution's name only, as invoiced. The bank needs the application, a passport copy, the NOC's bank copy, the institution's visa-conditions letter and the invoice or I-20. If money is exchanged before the visa, the student signs an undertaking to bring it back if the visa is refused.
  - **Living costs, where the documents state no amount**, are capped per year at: USD 8,000 for Canada, the USA, Australia and Europe; USD 6,000 for Singapore and South Korea; USD 4,000 for China, Malaysia, Thailand and the Philippines; USD 2,500 for Bangladesh and Pakistan; and USD 5,000 elsewhere. If an embassy or institution letter states the amounts, the full stated amount can be exchanged. If the visa needs a set balance, such as a blocked account, that balance can be exchanged, but only for the student, not for dependants.
  - **Later instalments go through the same bank.** Second and later instalments are exchanged by the bank that made the first exchange, against its record copy of the NOC. A student can ask for that record to be moved to another bank. This confirms the "same bank" rule that consultancies report ([AccessEdu](https://accessedu.net/blogs/no-objection-certificate-nepali-students)).
  - **PAN.** Exchange of more than USD 5,000 for education needs a PAN, either the student's own or a family member's with proof of relationship (same circular, p. 120).
  - **Students already abroad without an NOC** can receive living costs only once, up to USD 500, sent by a parent or guardian.
  - **Not verified:** the USD 12,000 student cash limit from December 2023 ([ShareSansar](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/nepal-rastra-bank-increases-dollar-limits-for-students-and-workers-going-abroad-2023-12-31)) does not appear as a separate figure in circular 8, so confirm cash and card amounts with the remitting bank. Our reading of the living-cost clause for part-paid tuition (at most 50% of the annual amount over six months) also needs confirming with the bank.
- **Scale of outflow.** Rs 138.48 billion left Nepal for overseas education in FY 2024/25 ([Himalayan Times](https://thehimalayantimes.com/education/nepali-sudents-spent-rs-138-billion-on-overseas-education-last-year)).
- **Education loans (as of 2026).** Most are secured against land or buildings:

| Bank | Published terms |
|---|---|
| NIC Asia | NPR 0.3 million to 10 million over 1–20 years, secured on land or land and building ([NIC Asia](https://www.nicasiabank.com/loans/education-loan/)) |
| Global IME | Up to 80% of education costs ([Global IME](https://globalimebank.com/blog/education-loan-for-studying-abroad-from-nepal)); its document list includes the ward four-boundary (*char killa*) certificate and land-tax receipts ([Global IME docs](https://www.globalimebank.com/blog/required-documents-for-education-loans/)) |
| Nabil | Opened an Education Hub in Putalisadak in August 2026 ([ShareSansar](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/nabil-bank-opens-education-hub-to-support-students-bound-for-overseas-study-2026-08-04)) |
| Nepal SBI | Fixed 9.99–10.99% from 17 July 2026 ([NSBL](https://nsbl.statebank/en/interest-rates-loans)) |
| Muktinath | Requires the NOC and the offer letter ([Muktinath](https://www.muktinathbank.com.np/general-banking/loan/education-loan)) |

- **Sponsor evidence:**
  - A ward-office income-source certificate signed by the ward chair. Formats vary by municipality.
  - An IRD tax clearance. The online version is mainly for single-employer salaried people.
  - A bank-engineer property valuation.
  - Land ownership papers (*lalpurja*) ([Global IME tax clearance](https://www.globalimebank.com/blog/how-to-get-a-personal-tax-clearance-certificate-online-in-nepal/); [Global IME valuation](https://www.globalimebank.com/blog/property-valuation-loan-guide/)).
- **Red flags:**
  - **Cooperative deposits.** More than 500 savings and credit cooperatives could not repay depositors in 2024 ([AsiaNews, Feb 2024](https://www.asianews.it/news-en/Nepali-depositors-angry,-ruined-by-microcredit-60141.html)). A December 2024 ordinance created a National Cooperative Regulatory Authority ([News on AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/nepal-president-ramchandra-paudel-issues-cooperatives-ordinance)). In May 2025, police arrested a ring forging **cooperative statements for Japan student visas** ([Ekantipur](https://ekantipur.com/national/2025/05/15/en/three-people-were-arrested-for-making-forged-documents-49-24.html)). Treat cooperative balances as weak evidence.
  - **Sudden lump-sum deposits** or loans taken from several banks ([Nepali Times](https://nepalitimes.com/america-no-longer-prime-destination-for-nepali-students)).
  - **Forged income or property papers.** This has a long history: the Kathmandu Post reported in August 2015 that Australia had refused more than 2,000 Nepali students the previous year over fraudulent paperwork, and that consultancies were forging income and property documents ([Kathmandu Post 2015](https://kathmandupost.com/miscellaneous/2015/08/19/police-raid-22-firms-detain-owners)).
  - **Australia's level of scrutiny.** Evidence Level 3 means upfront financial evidence and manual document checks ([AHC Lawyers](https://www.ahclawyers.com/news-articles/26/01/13/student-visa-subclass-500-update-evidence-level-change-for-south-asian-students)).
- **Currency.** The NPR is pegged to the INR at 1.6 ([Onlinekhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/fixed-pegging-exchange-rate-nepal.html); [Kathmandu Post, Feb 2026](https://kathmandupost.com/columns/2026/02/26/is-the-peg-to-inr-still-a-safe-harbour)). It therefore moves with the INR against the USD, AUD and GBP. Build a buffer above the required amount; the 5% NRB allowance exists for this reason.

## 5. Origin-side steps & documents

- **NOC (Foreign Study Permit).** Apply on noc.moest.gov.np. The fee is NPR 2,000 per subject. No NOC is issued for top-up degrees, Level 3–4 courses or one-year diplomas (portal, Oct 2026; details below).
  - **Legal basis:** required under the Scholarships Act framework ([Kathmandu Post 2023](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2023/04/28/nepal-to-continue-issuing-no-objection-certificates-for-non-university-courses)).
  - **How to apply:** online at **noc.moest.gov.np**.
  - **Who issues it:** the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology split on 13 May 2026, and the NOC now sits under the new **Ministry of Education and Sports** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_and_Sports_(Nepal)); [Himalayan Times](https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/govt-slashes-federal-ministries-to-18)). As of 9 Oct 2026 the portal domain has not changed. The NOC branch is in Singha Durbar: phone 01-6635419, email noc@moest.gov.np ([ministry NOC notice, about 19 Aug 2026](https://nocfiles.moest.gov.np/noc/uploads/notices/image/epEJzB476rcqMhQ6FMViyBqm39ktfcXUTd7IBA6z.jpg)).
  - **Documents:** citizenship certificate, transcripts, the offer letter (stating subject, level and duration), the invoice, and any previous NOC. Some courses need more: council licences, Medical Education Commission pre-approval for health subjects such as nursing and public health, or an English test result for New Zealand ([NOC portal FAQ](https://noc.moest.gov.np/)).
  - **Fee: NPR 2,000 per subject** (as of Oct 2026). It is paid as government revenue against the portal's voucher number, through eSewa, Khalti or connectIPS ([NOC portal payment guide](https://noc.moest.gov.np/)). Processing time is not published. The 1–5 days given in consultancy guides is not verified ([AccessEdu](https://accessedu.net/blogs/no-objection-certificate-nepali-students)).
  - **Online by default since Aug 2026:** applications are online only. Students whose date of birth is verified (their citizenship and academic records match in the government's central system) get the NOC without visiting the office. The portal tells everyone else to come in person. The ministry warned consultancies against telling students they must attend, or giving any other false NOC information, and said it will monitor them and take legal action ([ministry notice](https://nocfiles.moest.gov.np/noc/uploads/notices/image/epEJzB476rcqMhQ6FMViyBqm39ktfcXUTd7IBA6z.jpg)).
  - **Not issued (portal FAQ, as of Oct 2026):** Level 3 and Level 4 courses, Certificate III/IV, **top-up degrees** and one-year diplomas. NOCs are also restricted for Taiwan and Mauritius ([NOC portal FAQ](https://noc.moest.gov.np/)).
  - **Institution check (notice of about 16 Mar 2026):** before applying, the student must confirm the institution on the official register for its country and check whether it appears in the THE, QS or ShanghaiRanking lists. The registers include CRICOS (Australia), the OfS / GOV.UK recognised-bodies check (UK), QQI (Ireland), DAPIP (US) and the Wissenschaftsrat (Germany). Canada and New Zealand are not listed ([ministry notice](https://nocfiles.moest.gov.np/noc/uploads/notices/image/EN2o4RPGzZjsxhtg3a3XZafA4DdSamqHboBZ0dGB.jpg)). How the ranking check is applied is not verified.
  - **Course restriction history:** in 2023 the ministry briefly stopped NOCs for diploma and language courses, then reversed this for students who have completed Grade 12 ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2023/04/28/nepal-to-continue-issuing-no-objection-certificates-for-non-university-courses)). Non-degree study is limited to institutions on a ministry-approved list ([PIE News, 2024](https://thepienews.com/australia-bound-nepalese-students-face-record-high-visa-rejections/)).
  - **Institution reviews:** the NOC can be withheld for a whole institution. In March 2026, the Nepali embassy reviewed NOC issuance for Coventry University ([PIE News](https://thepienews.com/coventry-under-noc-scrutiny-amid-nepalese-student-probe/)).
  - **Proposed reform:** a 2025/26 plan would replace the NOC and link foreign exchange to "priority" subjects ([PIE News, Apr 2026](https://thepienews.com/nepal-proposes-scrapping-current-noc-process-in-sweeping-reform-push/)). As of 9 Oct 2026 it has not replaced the NOC: the portal still issues NOCs, and NRB still requires one for student foreign exchange (see §4).
  - **Bribery:** NOC-branch bribery was exposed, and all branch staff were replaced ([Onlinekhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/noc-branch-staff-completely-replaced-after-reports-of-irregularities.html)). Never pay "facilitation" money.
- **Police clearance.** Apply through the Nepal Police **Online Police Clearance Registration System** at [opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np](https://opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np/#/login), which is in Nepali and English. It is issued by Police Headquarters, Naxal ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/nepal.html)).
- **Attestation.** Nepal is **not** a Hague Apostille party ([HCCH](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)). Documents are legalised by MOFA's **Department of Consular Services**. Its online **Nepal e-Attestation System (NEATS)** launched on 6 August 2026 and has run fully since 17 August 2026: apply online, pay, book a slot, then attend in person for verification ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/06/nepal-launches-online-consular-attestation-system-service-to-begin-from-august-17); [Republica](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/govt-launches-online-consular-attestation-system-57-36.html)). Educational documents usually need verification by the issuing body (NEB or the university) first ([Nepal Embassy, US](https://us.nepalembassy.gov.np/pages/attestation-of-documents-6/)). The department's street address was not verified.
- **Civil documents.** Birth, relationship and income certificates come from the ward office. Nepali-language originals need certified English translation and notarisation.
- **Visa centres and biometrics:**
  - **Australia:** biometrics at the Australian Biometrics Collection Centre in Kathmandu, booked through VFS. Decisions are made in New Delhi ([Australian Embassy Nepal](https://nepal.embassy.gov.au/kmdu/visa.html)).
  - **Canada:** VFS VAC at LS Complex, Thapathali, Kathmandu ([VFS Canada–Nepal](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/npl/en/can/)).
  - **UK:** VFS Kathmandu according to 2026 guides. The current address is not verified on an official page ([Burness Paull](https://www.burnesspaull.com/legal-insights-news-events/insights/a-guide-to-applying-for-a-uk-spouse-visa-from-nepal)).
  - **US:** interviews at the Embassy in Maharajgunj, booked via USTravelDocs ([MPOWER](https://www.mpowerfinancing.com/?p=39878)).
- **Medicals.**
  - **Australia:** six panel clinics in Nepal, under review because of high demand ([Australian Embassy Nepal](https://nepal.embassy.gov.au/kmdu/visa.html)).
  - **UK TB test:** the approved clinics are the Migration Health Assessment Center (IOM) in Kathmandu, Norvic International Hospital in Kathmandu, and IOM Damak. The fee is USD 60 at IOM or NPR 7,800 at Norvic. Expect waits of up to 10 days in the June–October peak ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tuberculosis-test-for-a-uk-visa-clinics-in-nepal/tuberculosis-testing-in-nepal)).

## 6. Destination-by-destination guidance

## 7. Counselling do's & don'ts

**Regulation of agents (important for Expert's Nepal offices):**
- The **Educational Consultancy, Language Teaching and Preparatory Class (Operation and Management) Regulations 2026** were approved by Cabinet on 8 July 2026 and took effect immediately ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/07/09/nepal-introduces-tough-rules-to-rein-in-educational-consultancies-foreign-linked-colleges); [Nepalnews](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/explainers/nepal-tightens-rules-for-foreign-affiliated-colleges-and-educational-consultancies-everything-you-need-to-know/); [PIE News](https://thepienews.com/nepalese-education-consultancies-protest-sweeping-new-regulations/)). They require:
  - a federal Education Ministry licence, renewed every year. Nepalnews reports a Rs 50,000 licence fee and the Kathmandu Post a Rs 25,000 renewal fee. Only 924 of about 1,500 consultancies had renewed as of July 2026;
  - a Rs 2.5 million security deposit for the office and for each new branch, which existing firms pay at renewal;
  - premises that are owned or on a lease of at least three years, with separate toilets for men and women and disabled access ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/07/09/nepal-introduces-tough-rules-to-rein-in-educational-consultancies-foreign-linked-colleges));
  - counsellors with a bachelor's degree or recognised training;
  - no cash: payments by bank or digital channels only, with receipts issued and the fee schedule published ([Nepalnews](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/explainers/nepal-tightens-rules-for-foreign-affiliated-colleges-and-educational-consultancies-everything-you-need-to-know/));
  - a written agreement with each student before any service, covering fees, registration, admission conditions, scholarships and refunds;
  - an "education equity fee" on income from students or institutions, at a rate the government sets ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/07/09/nepal-introduces-tough-rules-to-rein-in-educational-consultancies-foreign-linked-colleges));
  - limits on commissions from overseas institutions ([PIE News, 20 Jul 2026](https://thepienews.com/nepalese-education-consultancies-protest-sweeping-new-regulations/)). The exact cap is not verified;
  - no overseas-recruitment fairs;
  - full liability when a student is stranded abroad or placed at a fraudulent institution, including "reasonable compensation" for financial loss and mental distress;
  - grounds for cancelling the licence and forfeiting the deposit: failing to renew, exploiting or defrauding students, giving misleading information, not filing tax returns, or stranding students abroad;
  - ministry grading of agencies as A, B or C, based on student outcomes, service quality and complaints;
  - an end to foreign ownership, with existing foreign-owned firms given one year to transfer to Nepali owners.
- Existing law already bars consultancies from job placement, job interviews and recruitment fees ([Nepalnews](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/explainers/nepal-tightens-rules-for-foreign-affiliated-colleges-and-educational-consultancies-everything-you-need-to-know/)).
- **FY 2083/84 renewal.** A ministry notice of about 6 Jul 2026 told licensed firms to renew online at **cims.moest.gov.np** between about 6 and 20 Jul 2026. It set the renewal revenue at Rs 20,000 from FY 2082/83, plus Rs 3,000 a year for earlier arrears. Firms had to upload:
  - tax clearance and audit reports up to FY 2081/82;
  - a report of the students served in FY 2082/83, by country, level and institution;
  - branch registration and rent agreements;
  - letters of authorised representation for each country.

  Firms that do not renew are left off the list of permitted firms for 2083/84 ([ministry notice](https://giwmscdnone.gov.np/media/pdf_upload/img096_h1kfiub.pdf)). That notice was issued under the 2073 directive, two days before the new regulations. Whether the Rs 25,000 regulation fee has replaced its Rs 20,000 is not verified, so check the voucher in CIMS.
- The Education Consultancy Association of Nepal (ECAN) is the industry association, not the regulator. Its members wore black armbands on 17 July and warned of a nationwide shutdown ([PIE News](https://thepienews.com/nepalese-education-consultancies-protest-sweeping-new-regulations/)). It asked for:
  - an insurance scheme instead of the deposit;
  - five-year licences;
  - recognition of commissions;
  - regulated fairs;
  - warnings rather than cancellation for minor advertising breaches;
  - a longer transition period.

  Talks were under way as of 20 Jul 2026 (same source). **No amendment was verified as of 9 Oct 2026.** The detailed advertising and signboard rules were not verified either.
- In about Aug 2026, the NOC branch warned consultancies against spreading false NOC information or sending students to its office needlessly ([ministry notice](https://nocfiles.moest.gov.np/noc/uploads/notices/image/epEJzB476rcqMhQ6FMViyBqm39ktfcXUTd7IBA6z.jpg)). Expert's Nepal offices should give students only what the NOC portal says.
- The ministry renewed 760 consultancies in FY 2082/83 ([Collegenp](https://www.collegenp.com/news/education-ministry-annual-progress-report-2082-83)).
- In May 2026, police raided 95 Kathmandu Valley consultancies, put 69 operators under investigation and seized fake government seals ([Kathmandu Post](https://www.kathmandupost.com/national/2026/05/16/arrests-device-seizures-in-crackdown-on-consultancies-over-shady-student-migration-process)). In January 2026, consultancy operators were arrested over a UAE study scam ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/01/15/police-nab-consultancy-operators-behind-uae-study-scam)).

**Do:**
- Verify financial documents at source with the bank or ward office.
- Insist on the final marksheet, not a provisional one.
- Check that the institution is NOC-eligible before taking deposits.
- Record every fee in the written agreement.
- Tell students when Australian refusal risk is high.

**Don't:**
- Accept cooperative balances or newly deposited lump sums at face value.
- Pitch a course on its work rights or PR.
- Route students into VET or language courses as a back door.
- Pay or advise anyone to pay NOC "speed" money.

**Crisis and wellbeing helplines** (checked 9 Oct 2026):
- **1166**: Nepal's national suicide-prevention helpline. Wikipedia's crisis-line list credits it to Tribhuvan University Teaching Hospital ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_suicide_crisis_lines)). We could not confirm the operator or hours on an official page (not verified).
- **TPO Nepal, 1660-010-2005**: free psychosocial support and counselling, every day 9 am–5 pm, toll-free from Nepal Telecom ([TPO Nepal](https://tponepal.org/)).
- Once the student is abroad, point them to the destination's emergency number and their university's counselling service. A Nepal number is for family members at home.
- **Communication note:** use plain, culturally neutral language with students and parents. Avoid religious idioms or blessings unless the family uses them first. Do not assume a family's faith, caste or festival observance.

## 8. What Edge should check/ask first

- What is the student's real goal: degree and return, work, PR, or family pressure? Is it consistent with the chosen course?
- What are the highest qualification and its format: SEE/NEB letter grade, old SLC/HSEB division, TU percentage or GPA? Any NG subjects or supplementary attempts?
- Is the bachelor's degree three or four years? Which university is it from (TU, KU, PU or another)?
- Who is the sponsor? Is there a relationship certificate? What are the income sources, with municipal certificate and tax clearance?
- Where do the funds come from: education loan, property, fixed deposit, cooperative? When were they deposited?
- Any previous visa refusals, especially from Australia, the US or Canada?
- Is the institution NOC-eligible? Is it on its country's official register? Is the course a level the NOC portal refuses (top-up, Level 3–4, Certificate III/IV, one-year diploma)? Has the NOC been applied for? Which bank will remit, and does the student have a PAN for remittances above USD 5,000?
- Is a police clearance or attestation needed, and are there enough days before the Dashain/Tihar closures?
- Is the Nepal office's licence current under the 2026 regulations, and is a written client agreement signed?
