# Canada — Compliance guide for Expert counsellors

*Researched 9 October 2026. Facts marked "as of" can change, so re-check them on the linked official page; anything marked "not verified" could not be confirmed against an official source and must not be stated to students as fact.*

## 1. Bottom line for counsellors
<!-- keywords: can i, allowed, rules for canada, canada compliance, quick summary, what should i do, canada counsellor, red lines, refer to rcic, canadian law -->

**Help students choose and apply to Canadian schools. Do not advise on or handle their study permit for any payment unless you are a lawyer, Quebec notary, law-society paralegal or CICC licensee.**

- Paid immigration advice by anyone else is an offence: up to $200,000 and 2 years in prison ([IRPA s.91](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-2.5/section-91.html)).
- "Educational agents" may help students choose and register in courses without being a representative ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/learn-about-representatives.html)).
- Anyone acting as a representative goes on IMM 5476, even when unpaid.
- Never promise a permit. Never touch a fake LOA, fake funds or an untrue answer: counselling misrepresentation is a crime ([IRPA s.126](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-2.5/section-126.html)).
- Refer study-permit strategy, refusals and status problems to an RCIC or Canadian lawyer you have checked on the [CICC register](https://register.college-ic.ca/).

## 2. IRPA section 91: who may advise for payment
<!-- keywords: section 91, s.91, irpa, for consideration, unauthorized practice, offence, fine, prison, outside canada, offshore agent, ghost consultant, who can give immigration advice, lawyer, paralegal, quebec notary -->

**[IRPA s.91](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-2.5/section-91.html) bars anyone from knowingly representing or advising a person "for consideration" in connection with an application under the Act, or offering to do so, directly or indirectly.**

- **Who may do it:**
  - lawyers and Quebec notaries;
  - paralegals and other law-society members;
  - members in good standing of the CICC.
- **"Consideration":** since 23 Oct 2024, IRCC reads this as "direct or indirect compensation by the client or on the client's behalf by a third party" ([IRCC Q&A](https://ccrweb.ca/sites/ccrweb.ca/files/2025-05/Section_91_IRPA_IRCC_Questions_and_Answers_May2025.pdf)). It covers advising on options or form answers, and dealing with IRCC (Q8).
- **Penalties:**
  - on indictment, up to $200,000 and/or 2 years;
  - on summary conviction, up to $40,000 and/or 6 months.
- **Offshore:**
  - IRCC may return or refuse an application that used an unauthorized representative "in Canada or abroad".
  - IRCC says such representatives may be charged under IRPA ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/file-complaint-against-representative.html)).
  - Actually prosecuting an agent who never enters Canada: **not verified**.
- **Proposed penalties:** administrative penalties of up to $1.5M were proposed in Jan 2025 ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/proposed-penalties-for-paid-immigration-representatives-break-the-law.html)). In force as of Oct 2026: **not verified**.

## 3. CICC, RCICs and declaring a representative
<!-- keywords: cicc, rcic, rcic-irb, college of immigration and citizenship consultants, public register, licence number, code of professional conduct, imm 5476, use of a representative form, declare representative, unpaid representative, find a consultant -->

**Canadian immigration consultants are licensed by the CICC. Check each one on the [public register](https://register.college-ic.ca/) before you refer a student.**

- **What to check on the register ([CICC](https://college-ic.ca/protecting-the-public/find-an-immigration-consultant)):**
  - "Entitled to Practise" must say Yes.
  - Status must be Active.
  - An L2 RCIC practises before IRCC. Only an L3 RCIC-IRB may also appear before the Immigration and Refugee Board.
- **The [Code of Professional Conduct](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-128/FullText.html) requires licensees to:**
  - make no guarantees and no false or government-link claims (s.44(2));
  - never assist fraud (s.12);
  - sign a written service agreement before giving any service (s.24);
  - pay no referral inducements unless the agent is registered with the College (s.13);
  - supervise their assistants (s.38).
- **Declaring a representative:**
  - IMM 5476 must be filed "even if your representative is uncompensated" ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/change-cancel.html), as of 31 Mar 2025).
  - Not declaring one "is considered lying" ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/protect-fraud/study-permit-fraud.html)).
- **Unauthorized paid representatives:** "We won't deal with representatives who charge a fee but are not authorized" (IRCC, as of 18 Aug 2026).

## 4. What an unlicensed education agent may and may not do
<!-- keywords: education agent, unlicensed agent, study permit advice, advise on study permit, study permit, for a fee, school selection, dli application, offer letter, loa logistics, pal request, study permit form, fill out application, sop for study permit, commission from college, paid by institution, service fee, transcribing, administrative help -->

**IRCC splits school and logistics help, which anyone may give, from paid immigration advice, which needs a licence.**

- **Allowed without a licence:**
  - course and school selection;
  - DLI applications and PAL requests through the school;
  - translation, travel and medical bookings;
  - pointing students to IRCC pages;
  - help with uploads.

  Source: [IRCC stakeholder guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/service-delivery/use-representatives/guidelines-other-stakeholders.html).
- **Grey zone:** "transcribing responses and information provided by an applicant into IRCC application forms" is administrative support ([IRCC Q&A](https://ccrweb.ca/sites/ccrweb.ca/files/2025-05/Section_91_IRPA_IRCC_Questions_and_Answers_May2025.pdf)). Deciding what the answers say is advice.
- **Licence needed if paid:**
  - explaining immigration options;
  - advising on form answers;
  - writing the study-permit explanation;
  - contacting IRCC for the student;
  - advertising visa advice.
- **Paid only by the institution?**
  - IRCC has published no position on DLI commission (**not verified**).
  - Its DLI guidance still says any agent advising on study permits "must be an authorized immigration representative" ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/educational-institutions/best-practices.html), as of 30 Jan 2025). Treat it as restricted.
  - A service fee charged to the student is clearly consideration.

## 5. DLIs, LOA verification, PAL and agent integrity
<!-- keywords: dli, designated learning institution, letter of acceptance, loa verification, fake loa, pal, tal, provincial attestation letter, graduate exemption, canada course for education agents, caps-i, recognized institutions, bc code of practice, agent contract -->

**IRCC verifies LOAs with schools; provinces hold schools responsible for agents.**

- **LOA verification:**
  - Since 1 Dec 2023, DLIs must confirm every post-secondary LOA directly with IRCC ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/2023/10/changes-to-international-student-program-aim-to-protect-students.html)).
- **PAL/TAL ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/provincial-attestation-letter.html), as of 2 Sep 2026):**
  - usually required, and requested through the school;
  - master's and PhD students at public DLIs are exempt from 1 Jan 2026;
  - most Quebec students need a CAQ instead.
- **Agent requirements:**
  - IRCC tells DLIs not to reward agents for submitting study permit applications ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/educational-institutions/best-practices.html)).
  - **British Columbia:** BC's [Code of Practice](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/post-secondary-education/institution-resources-administration/eqa/eqa_code_of_practice.pdf) (Jan 2025) requires written agent contracts. It also bars taking students from agents suspected of unlicensed visa advice or falsifying documents.
  - Many DLIs require RCIC status or the [Canada Course for Education Agents](https://caps-i.ca/agents/canada-course-for-education-agents/) *(industry)*.
- **Not verified as of Oct 2026:** the recognized institutions framework (planned for fall 2024), and any federal rule requiring disclosure of agent commissions.

## 6. Complaints, fraud reports and escalation
<!-- keywords: complaint, report fraud, report agent, scam, cicc complaint, unauthorized practitioner, law society complaint, border watch line, canadian anti-fraud centre, cafc, ircc web form, fake visa, student was cheated -->

**Match each complaint to the right body. Report fraud even if the student is not your client.**

- **Licensed consultant:**
  - Complain to the [CICC](https://college-ic.ca/protecting-the-public/complaints-process/make-a-complaint).
  - Disputes that are only about a refund after a refusal are out of scope.
- **Unlicensed person giving paid advice:**
  - Use the [CICC Unauthorized Practitioner](https://college-ic.ca/protecting-the-public/unauthorized-practice) form.
  - The CICC can seek court orders but cannot discipline non-members.
- **Lawyer or paralegal:** complain to the provincial law society ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/file-complaint-against-representative.html)).
- **Immigration fraud ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/protect-fraud/report-fraud.html), as of 11 May 2026):**
  - false information on an application: the CBSA Border Watch Line;
  - a representative on an open file: the IRCC web form;
  - scams and fake visa documents: the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/report-signalez-eng.htm);
  - personal or financial data handed over: local police or the RCMP.
- **Replace a bad representative:** the student files a new IMM 5476 at once.
- **Inside Expert:** log the issue, tell the branch manager, and alert the DLI if one of its offers is involved. *(Inference.)*

## 7. Expert's own obligations for Canada
<!-- keywords: expert policy, our obligations, branch, service fee canada, in-house rcic, referral to consultant, commission disclosure, written consent, staff training, onshore office, audit, liability -->

**These points are mostly inferences from the law above. Treat them as Expert policy, not as legal advice.**

- **Who does study-permit work:** a named RCIC or Canadian lawyer does it, declared on IMM 5476. Other counsellors stay with schools, admissions and logistics. *(Inference.)*
- **Service fees:** any Expert fee on a Canada file likely makes visa help "for consideration" under [s.91](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-2.5/section-91.html). *(Inference.)*
- **An RCIC who also recruits must, under [Code](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-128/FullText.html) s.18 (verified):**
  - get written consent;
  - keep the two services separate in the agreement;
  - charge the student nothing for recruitment;
  - disclose the institution's commission.
- **Staff must never:**
  - promise a permit;
  - be declared "unpaid" while Expert is paid;
  - draft study-permit explanations without a licensee.
- **Offices:** *(Inference.)* These limits apply in every office, offshore (South and Southeast Asia) and onshore (Australia, UK). IRCC refuses unauthorized paid representatives "in Canada or abroad".
- **Records:** keep Canada Course certificates and written student agreements. Partner DLIs in BC must audit their agents ([BC Code](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/post-secondary-education/institution-resources-administration/eqa/eqa_code_of_practice.pdf)).
