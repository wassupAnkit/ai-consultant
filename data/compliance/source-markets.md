# Source markets — Agent regulation guide for Expert counsellors

*Researched 9 October 2026. Each market section states the rules as of that date with the newest dated source; anything marked "not verified" could not be confirmed against an official text and must be checked with the named authority before it is relied on or told to a student.*

## 1. Nepal
<!-- keywords: nepal, kathmandu, ecan, consultancy regulations, nepal licence, renewal, security deposit, rs 2.5 million, bachelor degree counsellor, cash payment, grading, stranded, compensation, education fair, foreign ownership, 2083 -->

**Nepal licenses education consultancies under the Educational Consultancy, Language Teaching and Preparatory Class (Operation and Management) Regulations 2026 (2083 BS), approved by Cabinet in July 2026 and in force immediately ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/07/09/nepal-introduces-tough-rules-to-rein-in-educational-consultancies-foreign-linked-colleges) *(secondary)*).**

- **Licence:** register and renew every year with the federal Ministry of Education (renewal fee Rs 25,000); provincial registrations transfer to the federal system. A Rs 2.5 million deposit is due to open an office or branch, and from existing firms at renewal.
- **Counsellors:** a bachelor's degree, or recognised consultancy training run by a diplomatic mission.
- **Written agreement before any service:** fees, registration details, admission conditions, scholarships and refund policy.
- **Money:** bank or digital channels only; no cash.
- **Grading:** the ministry grades agencies A, B or C on student outcomes, service quality and complaints.
- **Liability:** full responsibility and compensation if misleading advice strands a student abroad or places them at a fraudulent institution; licence and deposit can be forfeited.
- **Fairs:** foreign institutions may not hold fairs to recruit Nepali students.
- **Foreign ownership:** barred; one year to divest.
- **Status:** the old 2073 directive was repealed ([Kendrabindu via Hamro Patro, 4 Aug 2026](https://www.hamropatro.com/en/news/detail/7348524983279636-nayan-niyamavali-lagu-bhaepachhi-shaikshik-paramarshasambandhi-purano-nirdeshika) *(secondary)*). ECAN wants amendments and a draft was being prepared as of 6 Aug 2026 ([Nepal Press via Hamro Patro](https://www.hamropatro.com/news/detail/3621231003208646-sarakar-r-kansaltensi-vyavasayibich-varta-niyamavali-sanshodhanako-prastavit) *(secondary)*). Any amendment adopted since: **not verified**. Gazette text: **not verified**.

## 2. India
<!-- keywords: india, punjab, haryana, travel professionals act, travel agents act, deputy commissioner licence, ielts centre, licence number, advertisement, emigration act, overseas mobility bill, mea, registration certificate -->

**India has no national licence for study-abroad agents, but Punjab and Haryana require state registration, and offences carry prison terms.**

- **Punjab:** the [Punjab Travel Professionals (Regulation) Act 2012](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22439/1/the_punjab_travel_professionals_regulation_act.pdf) (Punjab Act 2 of 2013), as amended in 2014, covers travel agents and IELTS coaching institutes. Licences are issued by the district Deputy Commissioner.
  - The 2014 amendment lets a licensee skip prior notice of an advert if the licence number is printed on it, and caps imprisonment at up to seven years ([amendment text](https://www.casemine.com/act/in/67dbe514e82e546e679bc112) *(secondary)*). Fine amounts: **not verified**.
  - Districts revoke licences at scale ([The Tribune](https://m.tribuneindia.com/news/jalandhar/licences-of-263-immigration-firms-ielts-centres-revoked-486964) *(secondary)*).
- **Haryana:** the [Haryana Registration and Regulation of Travel Agents Act 2025](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/haryana/2025/Act10of2025HRE.pdf), amended in 2026 (Gazette of 4 May 2026), defines a travel agent to include visa consultancy or guidance for "acquiring education". Registration requires police verification and is valid for three years.
  - Working unregistered: 2–7 years' imprisonment and a fine.
  - Forged documents: 7–10 years.
- **National:** the Emigration Act 1983 covers employment, not study ([MEA](https://www.mea.gov.in/Images/attach/Emigration_and_You_new.pdf)). The draft [Overseas Mobility (Facilitation and Welfare) Bill 2025](https://www.mea.gov.in/overseasmobilitybill2025.htm) is employment-focused. Enacted as of Oct 2026: **not verified**, and none found.
- **Other states:** no study-agent law found (**not verified**).

## 3. Bangladesh
<!-- keywords: bangladesh, dhaka, sylhet, trade licence, unregulated agents, joint warning, 13 missions, falsified documents, unlicensed agents, special access, embassy affiliation, bmet, penal code fraud -->

**Bangladesh has no licensing scheme for education agents; anyone with a trade licence can operate, so foreign missions and destination rules are the real constraint ([Financial Express, July 2026](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/article/the-other-side-of-studying-abroad) *(secondary)*).**

- **No regulator found.** About 2,000 firms operate in Dhaka. Fraud cases are charged under general Penal Code cheating clauses, and offenders often get bail (same source). No 2026 draft law or ministry proposal was found (**not verified** against the Gazette).
- **Joint warning, 8 April 2026:** 13 diplomatic missions in Dhaka, including the UK, Australia, Canada and Germany, jointly told applicants ([UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/13-countries-urge-bangladesh-visa-applicants-to-follow-official-procedures/183179) *(secondary)*):
  - "Do not use falsified documents, unreliable or unlicensed agents";
  - "No mission is affiliated with any agents";
  - do not use intermediaries claiming special access or influence over visa decisions;
  - consequences include refusal, financial loss and serious legal consequences.
- **What this means for Expert Bangladesh:**
  - never claim an embassy link, insider access or a guaranteed outcome;
  - never submit or tolerate altered bank statements or certificates (a Sylhet consultancy director was arrested over fake statements sent to the US Embassy, [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/294814/man-arrested-over-sylhet-us-embassy-fraud-case) *(secondary)*).
- **Labour recruitment** is separately licensed (BMET/BOESL). Whether that remit reaches study agents who also offer jobs: **not verified**.

## 4. Vietnam
<!-- keywords: vietnam, decree 86/2021, decree 322/2026, decree 125/2024, investment law 2025, conditional business, study abroad consultancy certificate, doet, department of education and training, study-cum-employment contract, counsellor competence, language level 4 -->

**Vietnam's agent rules changed in 2026. Study-abroad consultancy is no longer a conditional business line under the 2025 Investment Law, so the separate DOET certificate under Decree 125/2024 appears to have lapsed. Agencies still operate under Decree 86/2021 as amended by Decree 322/2026 (in force 5 Oct 2026).**

- **Investment Law 2025 (No. 143/2025/QH15):** Art. 51 makes its Appendix IV list of conditional business lines effective on 1 July 2026, replacing the 2020 list. The education entries in that list do not include study-abroad consultancy ([law text](https://apolatlegal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Luat-143-2025-QH15.docx) *(law-firm copy)*; [Luật Việt An](https://luatvietan.vn/tra-cuu-giay-phep-tu-van-du-hoc.html) *(secondary)*).
- **Decree 125/2024 (from 20 Nov 2024)** required a 5-year certificate from the provincial DOET and counsellors with a degree, foreign language at level 4 and a training certificate ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2024/11/legal-update-decree-124-and-125-updates.html) *(secondary)*). MoET confirmation that this no longer applies: **not verified**.
- **Decree 322/2026 (17 Aug 2026, effective 5 Oct 2026)** amends Decree 86/2021 ([LuatVietnam](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decree-no-322-2026-nd-cp-dated-august-17-2026-of-the-government-amending-and-supplementing-a-number-of-articles-of-the-governments-decree-no-86-20-444451-doc1.html); [Hoatieu](https://hoatieu.vn/phap-luat/nghi-dinh-322-2026-nd-cp-sua-doi-nghi-dinh-86-ve-viec-cong-dan-viet-nam-ra-nuoc-ngoai-hoc-tap-giang-day-257773) *(secondary)*):
  - agencies are firms with the line on their business registration;
  - sign a consultancy contract with each student, and no combined study-and-work contracts;
  - counsellors must have suitable professional knowledge and competence;
  - no sub-delegation to or from another agency;
  - the agency may sign recruitment contracts with foreign institutions.
- **Inference:** keep counsellor credentials on file anyway.

## 5. Philippines
<!-- keywords: philippines, manila, cebu, dmw, illegal recruitment, ra 8042, ra 10022, job offer, caregiver, nursing job, student visa work, work and study, placement fee, recruitment licence, economic sabotage -->

**The Philippines has no licence for education agents, but the moment an agency offers or promises a job abroad for a fee it is doing recruitment, which needs a Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) licence.**

- **The legal line:** under [RA 8042 as amended by RA 10022, s.6](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_10022_2010.html), illegal recruitment covers recruiting, referring, promising or advertising overseas jobs by a non-licensee, "whether for profit or not". A non-licensee who "offers or promises for a fee employment abroad to two or more persons" is deemed a recruiter.
- **Penalties (s.7):**
  - 12 years and 1 day to 20 years' imprisonment and a PHP 1–2 million fine;
  - life imprisonment and up to PHP 5 million when it amounts to economic sabotage (3 or more offenders or victims).
- **DMW position:** jobseekers should never accept student or tourist visas for overseas work ([PNA](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1218772)). The DMW Secretary's line is that visa advice is fine, but a job offer plus fees without a licence is illegal recruitment ([Manila Bulletin, Aug 2025](https://mb.com.ph/2025/08/02/dmw-shuts-down-agency-illegally-recruiting-teachers-for-us) *(secondary)*).
- **Enforcement:** the DMW raided a visa consultancy over caregiver job offers in May 2026 ([Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/05/15/dmw-raids-unlicensed-consultancy-firm) *(secondary)*).
- **For counsellors:**
  - never pitch study as a job route;
  - never bundle placement with a job;
  - never "guarantee" caregiving or nursing work;
  - part-time work rights come from the destination's visa rules only.

## 6. Thailand
<!-- keywords: thailand, bangkok, tieca, no thai licence, consumer protection board, ocpb, voluntary association, work and study abroad, department of employment, unaccredited school, thai complaint -->

**Thailand has no government licence or register for education agents; TIECA is a voluntary, self-regulating association, so destination rules and general consumer and criminal law are what bind Expert Thailand.**

- **No licensing found.** TIECA (Thai International Education Consultants Association) has been registered with the Ministry of Commerce since 1997 ([Quality English–TIECA](https://quality-english.com/blog/quality-english/quality-english-and-thai-agency-association-tieca-sign-mou) *(secondary)*). Its president was quoted saying there is "no authority to directly oversee these consultancies" ([AseanNow, undated](https://aseannow.com/topic/349514-thai-students-warned-of-unqualified-foreign-schools/) *(secondary)*). No 2026 rule was found (**not verified** with MHESI).
- **Complaints:**
  - Consumer complaints go to the Office of the Consumer Protection Board (OCPB).
  - A 2022 legal summary says the OCPB may pass education matters to specialised agencies, and it refers cases involving a legal violation to the police ([Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/how-thailands-consumer-protection-board-handles-consumer-complaints/11/) *(secondary)*).
- **Work-and-study schemes:** the Department of Employment once ordered schools to suspend programmes sending students to work abroad after students were exploited in Korea ([Bangkok Post, undated](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/general/1273947/overseas-work-study-courses-abused-halted) *(secondary)*). Whether that order is still in force: **not verified**. Recruiting Thais for overseas jobs needs a Department of Employment licence (**not verified** in this pass).
- **Main fraud risk:** low-quality or unaccredited foreign institutions. Check CRICOS, the UK sponsor register or SEVP, plus home recognition.

## 7. Sri Lanka
<!-- keywords: sri lanka, colombo, slbfe, foreign employment licence, unauthorised recruitment, student visa job, no education agent regulator, ministry of education warning, scholarship agents, azerbaijan, tvec -->

**Sri Lanka has no regulator or licence for education agents, but recruiting for jobs abroad needs a Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) licence, and the SLBFE prosecutes "student visa" agencies that promise work.**

- **No education-agent scheme found.** After three Sri Lankan students died in Azerbaijan in 2020, the Ministry of Education said it would create a regulatory framework, but no details followed ([The PIE News, Feb 2020](https://thepienews.com/sri-lanka-students-azerbaijan-agents/) *(secondary)*). Status as of Oct 2026: **not verified**.
- **The employment line:** the SLBFE says a valid licence is mandatory to recruit workers, advertise foreign jobs, collect money or personal information, or process passports for overseas employment ([Ada Derana, 30 Jan 2026](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/117814/slbfe-warns-of-overseas-based-job-scam-promising-employment-in-moldova) *(secondary)*). The SLBFE Act No. 21 of 1985 makes unauthorised recruitment an offence ([Act text](https://www.srilankalaw.lk/revised-statutes/volume-vii/1153-sri-lanka-bureau-of-foreign-employment-act.html) *(secondary)*). Penalty amounts: **not verified**.
- **Case:** in April 2026 the SLBFE arrested the operator of a "student visa" consultancy that promised jobs in Albania and Canada without a licence ([Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/Maharagama-visa-scam-Woman-arrested-for-Rs-10-million-fraud-promising-overseas-jobs/108-337509) *(secondary)*).
- **Scholarships:** in May 2026 the Ministry of Education warned families against agents claiming links to its overseas scholarship, which is applied for directly ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/05/06/govt-scholarship-programme-parents-and-students-warned-against-unauthorized-agents/) *(secondary)*). Never imply a government link.
- **TVEC registration** for institutes that teach and send students abroad: **not verified** (one 2011 report only).

## 8. Rules that apply in every market
<!-- keywords: every market, all markets, offshore agent, destination rules, standard 4, national code, s.91, offshore exemption, agent quality framework, written agreement, commission disclosure, fee disclosure, fraudulent documents, refuse documents -->

**Wherever an Expert office sits, the destination's rules follow the file: they bind offshore agents through partner institutions and visa law, whatever the home market allows.**

- **Australia:** [National Code Standard 4](https://www.education.gov.au/download/11456/standard-4-education-agents/43665/document/pdf) makes providers hold written agreements with agents, and ensure agents act ethically and honestly and give no false or misleading information.
  - Commission on onshore provider transfers has been banned since 2026 ([education.gov.au](https://www.education.gov.au/esos-framework/changes-legislative-framework-overseas-students)).
  - Visa advice in Australia needs a registered migration agent ([Ombudsman](https://www.ombudsman.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0033/79683/Factsheet_student_education-agents-links-fixed-A1576258.pdf)).
- **New Zealand:** agents based outside NZ are exempt from licensing for student-visa advice only ([INZ](https://immigration.govt.nz/assist-migrants-and-students/assist-students/understand-your-obligations/providing-immigration-advice-to-students)).
- **Canada:** paid study-permit advice by non-licensees breaches IRPA s.91. IRCC may refuse applications that used an unauthorized representative "in Canada or abroad" ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/learn-about-representatives.html)).
- **UK:** the [Agent Quality Framework](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/our-work/working-with-agents/) and its National Code of Ethical Practice apply to agents of licensed sponsors.
- **Always:**
  - a signed written agreement with the student, with itemised fees and refunds;
  - disclose which institutions pay commission;
  - never charge both sides for one service without disclosure;
  - refuse forged or "arranged" documents and funds ([Agent Code of Ethics](https://internationaleducation.gov.au/news/latest-news/Documents/Australian%20International%20Education%20and%20Training%20-%20Agent%20Code%20of%20Ethics.pdf)).
