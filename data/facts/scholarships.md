# Scholarships: dated facts register

Scope: major scholarships for students from Nepal (NP), India (IN), Bangladesh (BD), Vietnam (VN), Philippines (PH), Thailand (TH) and Sri Lanka (LK) to study in the UK, Australia, Canada, the US and NZ.
On 2026-10-09 most programmes are between cycles: the 2026-27 intake has started, and applications for 2027-28 are open or have just closed. Each row says which cycle it covers.
Rows marked "(secondary — verify)" or "(not verified — verify)" could not be confirmed on an official page on the check date. Do not recommend a programme as "open" without checking the official link.
Checked: 2026-10-09.
