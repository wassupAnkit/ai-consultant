Notes
- UKVI does accept a bank education loan letter instead of a 28-day balance if the letter meets FIN 9.2, including "no conditions on release other than a successful application". Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-finance
- Loan funds do not need to meet the 28-day rule (ST 12.6). Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student
- Fixed deposits, or money borrowed against them, count only if the money can be withdrawn and the account shows the full 28 days. Pledged or lien-marked deposits are a common refusal reason (secondary — verify against Student caseworker guidance: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/student-route-caseworker-guidance).
